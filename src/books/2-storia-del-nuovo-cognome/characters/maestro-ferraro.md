The volunteer librarian of the *biblioteca rionale*, the small lending
library the children of the neighbourhood used in book 1. He awarded
medals to the most prolific readers — and gave several to Elena and,
under several invented names, to most of the Cerullo family, because
Lila was checking out books on her father, mother and brother's library
cards as well as her own.

In book 2 Ferraro appears only once, by his absence. The morning after
she finds out her first novel will be published, Elena slips into the
old library hoping to share the news with him. The library is empty;
the man behind the counter is a stranger; Ferraro has retired. *Proprio
ora che stavo diventando scrittrice, non c'era in tutto il rione
nessuno capace di dire: che cosa straordinaria sei riuscita a fare.*
