Don Achille and Donna Maria's daughter, sister to Stefano and Alfonso.
She works in the family salumeria. She is engaged to **Rino Cerullo**,
Lila's older brother — the cross-marriage that braces the Cerullo and
Carracci businesses together.

A few days after Lila returns from her honeymoon, Pinuccia tells
Elena that there has been a big lunch at Lila's new apartment to
celebrate Pinuccia's engagement to Rino. Elena was not invited.
Pinuccia adds, with a sly smirk and a dialect phrase, that Lila *si
sta imparando* — she's learning her place.
