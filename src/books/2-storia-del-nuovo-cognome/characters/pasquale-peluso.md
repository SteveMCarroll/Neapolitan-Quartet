Bricklayer by trade and committed *militante comunista*; brother of
Carmen, son of the late Alfredo Peluso (the carpenter found guilty,
in book 1, of having killed Don Achille and who died in prison).
Pasquale is one of the rione's few first‑generation autodidacts —
he reads newspapers and pamphlets at building sites and at the
parish hall and is forever picking ideological fights.

In book 2 he is for several years Ada Cappuccio's quiet fiancé, a
modest, devoted, mostly invisible third party in the rione's gossip.
He is destroyed when Ada eventually picks Stefano. He is also one of
Lila's longest‑standing allies, and is among the few who go up to
San Giovanni to keep an eye on her after Enzo moves her out of the
rione. By book 2's end he has begun seeing a much younger girl, the
merciaia's elder daughter.
