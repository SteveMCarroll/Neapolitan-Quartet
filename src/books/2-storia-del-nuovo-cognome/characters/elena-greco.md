Elena, called **Lenuccia** or **Lenù**, is the first-person narrator of
the entire Neapolitan series. She was born in Naples in August 1944,
the same month as her friend Lila. After their shared elementary
school years she went on to middle school, then high school — a path
denied to Lila, whose parents kept her home to work in the family
cobbler shop.

Elena is the eldest child of a porter at City Hall and a housewife who
walks with a pronounced limp. She has three younger siblings — Peppe,
Gianni and Elisa. Her academic success has set her apart from the
*rione* (the working-class neighbourhood she grew up in), and she has
spent her entire life pulled between the desire to escape Naples
through study and the gravitational pull of Lila and the rione.

From early childhood she has been in love with **Nino Sarratore**, an
equally gifted classmate whose family briefly lived next door. She has
kept this love almost entirely secret. After spending a summer on
Ischia where Nino's father, Donato, sexually harassed her, she
returned home shaken; the experience hangs over her relationship with
the Sarratores.

At the start of book 2 Elena is in her late teens, still in school,
and unofficially dating **Antonio Cappuccio**, a mechanic from the
neighbourhood. She has just attended Lila's wedding to **Stefano
Carracci**, where she saw the marriage betrayed before it even began
and felt herself drifting permanently apart from her friend.
