Lila's older brother and a cobbler like their father Fernando. With
Lila's design help and Stefano Carracci's money he has set up the
**calzaturificio Cerullo** — the Cerullo shoe factory — the family's
shot at climbing out of poverty. Rino is as ambitious as his sister
but lacks her instincts; he resents the way the neighbourhood is run
by violence yet is happy enough to do business with the Solaras when
it serves the factory.

He is engaged to **Pinuccia Carracci**, Stefano's sister, which knits
the Cerullos and the Carraccis together commercially as well as
personally.

In the opening chapters Rino is delighted to see Marcello Solara
strut into the wedding wearing the prototype shoes — to him this is
the deal landing, not a betrayal. When Lila pointedly snubs the
Solaras on her way out he laughs it off to Stefano: *"That one was
born crooked, I'm sorry for you."*
