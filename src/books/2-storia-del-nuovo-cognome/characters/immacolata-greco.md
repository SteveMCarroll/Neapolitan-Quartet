Elena's mother. A housewife in the rione, mother of Elena, Peppe,
Gianni and Elisa. She is a strict, humourless figure whose limping
walk and slightly wandering eye obsess her eldest daughter. She has
never quite forgiven Elena for being chosen by Maestra Oliviero for
higher study, and she watches her daughter for signs of slipping —
out of the neighbourhood, or back into it.

When Elena gets home late from Lila's wedding, her mother slaps her
across the face, breaking her glasses; Elena answers, "Look what
you've done — now thanks to you I can't study anymore." In the middle
of book 2, with Elena ill at the Normale, she puts herself on a night
train alone — she has barely ever left the rione — and arrives loaded
with home‑made Christmas food to feed her, shout at her, take her
temperature too many times, and quarrel with her about every girl in
the rione, betraying just how proud and terrified she is that Elena
might slip and lose her status. By the end of book 2 she has bought
an album to paste in every kind review of Elena's first novel — even
though she will never read the book herself.
