Antonio's younger sister, daughter of Melina the *pazza* and of the
late Cappuccio (the porter who died on a building site). Ada is
hard‑working, sharp‑tongued, and ambitious in a way the rione doesn't
quite know what to do with: she has worked since she was a child to
keep her mother and brother fed, and she has watched Lila glide into
luxury beside her.

In book 2 she begins as Pasquale Peluso's quiet, loyal *fidanzata*.
Stefano, lonely and humiliated by Lila, takes her on as a clerk at
the rione's old salumeria and then as his lover. By the second half
of the book she is the recognised mistress, taking pizza dinners with
Michele Solara and Gigliola, dragging her mother along, and beginning
to talk openly about the *Sacra Rota*. After she gets pregnant and
goes to confront Lila, Stefano hits her once — and then yields. By
the end of book 2 Ada lives in Lila's old apartment, runs both
salumerie, and has a daughter named Maria.
