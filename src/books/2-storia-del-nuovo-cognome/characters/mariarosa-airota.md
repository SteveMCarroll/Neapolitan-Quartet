Pietro's older sister, twenty‑four when Elena meets her — already a
lecturer in Art History at the University of Milan, working on Piero
della Francesca, driving the family's old Fiat 1100 around Pisa for
the day with her parents and Pietro. She hugs and kisses Elena like
an old friend on first meeting, and across a single political lunch
puts Elena at ease and changes the social weather of her last Normale
year.

In book 2 Mariarosa is Elena's first glimpse of a woman who has
inherited the easy, ironic intellectual habits Elena has only
laboriously copied — *l'addestramento a sentire intimamente mie le
questioni del mondo*. She also makes time, even as it is
inconvenient, to come to Milan and stop by the bookshop on the
evening of Elena's first public appearance, before her own
commitments take her elsewhere.
