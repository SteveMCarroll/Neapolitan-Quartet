Pasquale's sister, daughter of the late carpenter Alfredo Peluso.
Formally Carmela, but called Carmen by everyone in the rione. In book
2 she is hired by Lila to staff the new salumeria in the *rione
nuovo* — Lila pays her well, gives her shifts she can manage with the
family at home, and uses the shop to mock the Carracci/Solara
respectability the marriage has saddled her with.

Carmen is loud, devoted, easily wounded, and a fierce gossip. She is
the rione's main early conduit of news about Lila and Nino over the
Ischia summer, and later the chief sympathetic ear for Ada once Ada
becomes Stefano's mistress. By book 2's end she is engaged to a kind
forty‑something fuel‑station attendant on the *stradone*.
