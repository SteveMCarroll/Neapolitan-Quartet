The first child of Rino Cerullo and Pinuccia Carracci, named Fernando
after his grandfather but called *Dino* in the family. He is born
during book 2's middle stretch and lives with his parents in the
apartment above the new salumeria.

Lila, struggling with her own son Rinuccio, latches onto Dino for a
while as a kind of pedagogical project: she sits with him every day,
reading flash‑cards, naming objects, working out word‑games. The
obsession scares Rino, who is convinced his sister is somehow
draining the boy's life force, and ends in a violent scene in which
Stefano flings Rino down the stairs. Dino is fine.
