The youngest son of Don Achille and Donna Maria, brother to Stefano
and Pinuccia. He sits next to Elena in class and the two are quietly
close — he is one of the only people from the rione who shares her
school world. Alfonso is engaged (in the loose teenage sense) to
**Marisa Sarratore**, Nino's younger sister, and at Lila's wedding
the two of them spend the afternoon at the same table as Elena,
talking with shining eyes.
