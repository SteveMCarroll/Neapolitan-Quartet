Never named in the books. A housewife in the rione, mother of Elena,
Peppe, Gianni and Elisa. She is a strict, humourless figure whose
limping walk obsesses her eldest daughter. She has never quite
forgiven Elena for being chosen by Maestra Oliviero for higher study,
and she watches her daughter for signs of slipping — out of the
neighbourhood, or back into it.

When Elena gets home late from Lila's wedding, her mother — held back
ineffectually by the younger siblings — slaps her across the face,
sending her glasses flying. Elena turns on her with: "Look what you've
done? You've broken my glasses, and now thanks to you I can't study
anymore, I won't go to school anymore."
