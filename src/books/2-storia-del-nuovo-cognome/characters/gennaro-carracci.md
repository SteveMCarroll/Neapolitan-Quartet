Lila's son. He is born about two‑thirds into book 2, after the long
twenty‑three days at the Maronti with Nino on Ischia and the
Pasolinian downpour in the Forio piazza — Lila has long claimed him
in private as Nino's child, but he is legally a Carracci and goes
through life as one. He is registered as Gennaro and called *Rinuccio*
in the family, after his maternal uncle Rino.

Through book 2 he is a placid, alert, very early‑speaking baby and
toddler. Lila pours an unsettling amount of teaching into him —
flash‑cards, sentences, the alphabet — and her sister‑in‑law Pinuccia
worries that he's being over‑stimulated. After Lila leaves Stefano
and moves to San Giovanni a Teduccio, Rinuccio is uprooted and
becomes briefly capricious, sleeping next to her on the camp bed and
demanding to be carried everywhere, until the neighbour Titina takes
him in for most of each day so Lila can work at the Soccavo factory.
