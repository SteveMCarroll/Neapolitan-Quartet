Elena's youngest sister. Quiet, attentive, often a small softening
presence in the Greco household. When Elena's mother knocks Elena's
glasses to the floor in a slap, it's Elisa who scoops them up and
hands them back, saying gently, "Here, Lenù, they're not broken."
