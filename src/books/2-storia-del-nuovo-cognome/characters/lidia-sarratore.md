Donato Sarratore's wife and the mother of Nino, Marisa, Pino, Clelia
and Ciro. In book 1 the family lived in the rione, in the apartment
above Melina Cappuccio; after the affair between her husband and
Melina, Lidia insisted the family leave for the Mergellina.

In book 2 she rarely appears on the page. Elena, summering on Ischia
with the family in book 1's flashback‑like opening to Lila's parallel
story, finds her dignified, exhausted, and very far from naïve about
her husband. By book 2's main chronology she is mainly an absence
behind the always‑awkward fact of Nino's family, glimpsed only at
fleeting moments around the Sarratore household.
