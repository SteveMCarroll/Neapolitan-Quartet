Daughter of the rione's *pasticciere* who supplies the Solara bar with
its cakes. She was Elena's classmate in primary school and then again
in middle school; she has been Michele Solara's *fidanzata* since
late book 1.

In book 2 she is Lila's main rival in social standing — engaged to
the cleverer Solara brother, working behind the counter at the
*bar Solara* on the stradone, fully inside the family's network. She
is the one who first picks Ada up out of pity in the parish cinema
and then deliberately draws her into pizza nights with Michele,
priming Ada to push for a real place at Stefano's side. By the end
of the book she is publicly grumbling that if Michele doesn't fix a
wedding date by spring 1968 she's done.
