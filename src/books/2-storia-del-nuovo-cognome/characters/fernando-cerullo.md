The rione cobbler — the *scarparo* of his daughter's contemptuous
shorthand. Father of Rino and Lila, husband of Nunzia. In book 1 he
refused to let Lila sit the entrance exam for middle school, and
later threw her out of an upstairs window when she pushed him too
hard. He is honest, very skilled with his hands, and pragmatic to the
point of myopia — he never believes Rino and Lila's project for the
Cerullo shoes can become a business, and is dragged into it only
when Stefano puts up the money.

In book 2 he and Rino quarrel constantly; the small workshop limps
along, then revives, then is undermined again by the Solaras' moves on
the brand. Fernando refuses to receive his daughter once she has left
her husband, and forbids Nunzia from doing so either.
