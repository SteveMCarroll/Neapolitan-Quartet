Nino's younger sister and a friend of Elena's from childhood — they
became close during Elena's summer on Ischia with the Sarratores.
Marisa is studying half-heartedly to be a company secretary. She is
engaged (in the loose teenage sense) to **Alfonso Carracci**, and at
Lila's wedding the two of them are inseparable at Elena's table.

After the reception ends she and Alfonso slip away early to meet her
brother Nino in town.
