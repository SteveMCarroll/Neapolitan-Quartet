A wealthy, charismatic Milanese student at the *Scuola Normale
Superiore* and a well‑known militant of the student left. He is two
or three years ahead of Elena and is, for a long stretch of book 2's
final third, her boyfriend, her unofficial finishing school, and her
public certification at Pisa.

Franco buys her clothes, takes her to Paris and to Versilia, lends
her books, gives her the political slogans she will later embarrass
herself with at the Airota family lunch, and gently bullies her into
becoming the woman who can take her *laurea* without flinching. He
also pushes her past her own scruples about *libertà sessuale* and
gives her a private rule about *mezze vergini* that she silently
mocks for the rest of the book. He is expelled from the Normale for
political activism near the end of her third year — and with that
expulsion her unofficial protection at piazza dei Cavalieri lapses.
