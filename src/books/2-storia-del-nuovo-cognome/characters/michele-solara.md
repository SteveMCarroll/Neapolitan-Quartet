The younger son of Silvio and Manuela Solara, just slightly younger
than his brother Marcello. The book-1 recap describes Michele as
"colder, more intelligent, more violent" than Marcello — the more
dangerous of the two. He is engaged to **Gigliola Spagnuolo**, the
pastry-maker's daughter who works in the Solara café-pastry shop.

In the opening chapters of book 2 he is mostly in the background of
Marcello's bigger moves — present at the wedding, part of the family
front that descends on Lila's reception.
