Elena's fiancé from the last year of book 2. A young classical
philologist at the *Scuola Normale Superiore* — *occhialuto, timidissimo,
una matassa ingarbugliata di capelli nerissimi, i piedi storti* — son
of a famous socialist professor and brother of a brilliant Milan art
historian. He writes his graduating *lavoro* on Bacchic rites and
fully expects to publish it as a book at twenty‑two.

He meets Elena outside a café where a rejected suitor has been
shouting *Napoli, ricordati di riportarmi il pullover blu*; he is
ashamed enough on her behalf to come and apologise. They begin a
chaste relationship in the dark lanes around the Normale. By the end
of book 2 he has graduated, attended Elena's *laurea*, given her a
small diamond ring and the proofs of his book, accepted from her a
mysterious *quaderno* (her secret novel), and set a wedding date of
September 1969.
