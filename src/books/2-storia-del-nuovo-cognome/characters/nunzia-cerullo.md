Lila and Rino's mother, wife of the cobbler Fernando. A small, worn,
devout woman who keeps the household running through her husband's
tempers and her son's grand schemes. In book 2 she watches Lila move
out of the rione to the new neighbourhood, and then watches her
marriage to Stefano go visibly wrong.

In the Ischia summer Nunzia is the chaperone — Stefano has paid for
the whole holiday so that Lila can spend the season with Pinuccia and
Elena under Nella Incardo's roof. She is alternately scandalised and
helpless when the *fidanzati* arrive at weekends and when, eventually,
Nino Sarratore takes up daily residence on the Maronti beach. After
Lila leaves Stefano and goes to live with Enzo at San Giovanni a
Teduccio, Nunzia is forbidden by Fernando from seeing her — though,
through Stefano's own bitter report, the message that travels to Lila
is that even her mother no longer wants to lay eyes on her.
