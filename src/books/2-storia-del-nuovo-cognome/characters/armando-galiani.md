Elder son of Professoressa Galiani; a medical student in Naples and
already a competent player at his mother's political dinner parties.
Elena first meets him at the New Year's Eve party at the Galiani flat
in book 1.

In book 2 he turns up on Ischia in the middle stretch as a friend of
Nino's, often in a small group on the Maronti beach with his sister
Nadia, Nino, Bruno Soccavo and the three young women from the rione.
He is gentle, attentive, slightly *playboyish* in a clean, well‑bred
register that fascinates Pinuccia. He drifts out of the page again
once the Ischia idyll collapses.
