The older son of **Silvio Solara**, the monarchist-fascist *camorrista*
who owns the neighbourhood bar-pastry shop and runs its illegal
businesses. Marcello and his younger brother **Michele** spend their
days swaggering around the rione in their Fiat 1100, harassing girls
and enforcing their father's interests.

In book 1 Marcello fell hard for Lila and tried to make her marry him
— even gluing himself to the Cerullo dinner table night after night
until she finally and publicly refused him. Her rejection of Marcello
in favour of the mild Stefano Carracci is one of the great social
shocks of the early neighbourhood story.

Book 2 opens with Marcello striding into Lila's wedding reception
*wearing the prototype Cerullo shoes* — the shoes Stefano had sworn
to keep forever. Whatever business deal his appearance announces, it
is the moment Lila understands she has not in fact escaped him.
