## Summary

Antonio is breaking down. He has applied for an exemption from
military service as the only male provider for his widowed mother
Melina, but the army is taking its time. Enzo Scanno is being called
up in the autumn and Antonio is terrified the same notice is coming
for him. He hops off work without warning to come find Elena outside
school. *Find out from Lina,* he begs, *whether Stefano was excused
because he's a widow's son or for some other reason.*

A pizza-night with Pasquale, Carmela, Enzo, and Ada turns political.
Pasquale, exempted because of a childhood tubercolosi, says the
fascists are creeping back to power with the *democristiani*'s help
and that "people like us" had better learn to use weapons. Enzo
nods. Antonio is mainly preoccupied with whether he'll be drafted.
Alone with Elena afterwards he blurts out his real fear: *if I leave
you'll be with someone else.*

Elena asks Lila about Stefano's exemption. Lila finally pulls Pinuccia
aside on a Rettifilo errand and Pinuccia tells her, cheerfully: the
only sure way out was to **pay** — and the Solaras arranged it. The
Solaras had paid off the same district commission years ago to get
themselves marked "insufficient chest capacity"; Stefano simply used
their contacts. Lila relays this to Elena not with horror but with
something close to triumph: *he tricked me from the start.* It's
proof, she says, that her marriage to Stefano was already an alliance
with the Solaras before it was a marriage at all. Elena timidly asks
whether the Solaras might do the same favour for Antonio if needed.
Lila's face goes mean: *Antonio would never go to the Solaras.*

| esonerare / esonero | to exempt / exemption | the noun and verb |
| sostegno della famiglia | the family's support / provider | the legal exemption category |
| un'iniziativa per festeggiare | a gesture to celebrate | iniziativa = initiative |
| celerini | riot police (slang) | postwar Italian police term |
| levantinismo trafficone | shifty-Levantine wheeler-dealing | Elena's wry word for her father |
| democristiani | Christian Democrats | the dominant postwar centrist party |
| insufficienza toracica | insufficient chest capacity | a classic faked medical exemption |
| una vecchia tubercolosi | an old case of TB | Pasquale's real exemption |
| sotto le armi | under arms / in service | sotto = under |
| diserzione / disertore | desertion / deserter | the dread alternative |
| accollarsi la responsabilità | to take on the responsibility | accollare = to load on |
