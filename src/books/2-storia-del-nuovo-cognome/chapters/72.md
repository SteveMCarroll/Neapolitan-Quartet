## Summary

Elena fears she'll find Nella and Lidia awake, but both women are
asleep, *non erano preoccupate al punto da perdere il sonno, mi
consideravano giudiziosa, si fidavano di me.* She sleeps deeply.
In the morning the previous night arrives in pieces and she still
feels fine — chats with Nella, eats breakfast with the Sarratores,
finds Donato's *gentilezza fintamente paterna* not so terrible. She
does not, *nemmeno per un secondo*, feel that sex with that *uomo
un po' gonfio, vanitoso, ciarliero* was a mistake. And yet seeing
him at the table and registering that *era stato lui a sverginarmi*
fills her with *ribrezzo*. She swims with the kids, leaves behind
a wake of likeability, and arrives at Forio on time.

Lila isn't down yet. Elena calls; Nino looks out, signs *one
minute*. She refuses to come up — *un po' perché non volevo
custodire nella memoria immagini di stanze che Nino e Lila avevano
abitato da soli per quasi due giorni.* Anxiety returns. Maybe
Stefano has left Naples early, is arriving in advance. A quarter of
an hour later Lila and Nino come down kissing in the doorway; Lila
runs to Elena, stops, runs back, kisses Nino again. Elena looks
away, the old conviction returning that she is *fatta male, senza
una vera capacità di coinvolgimento*. They look beautiful, *perfetti
in ogni movimento*, until Elena's *Lina, sbrigati* gashes the
picture. Lila walks back as if tugged by *una forza crudele*, her
hand running slowly *dalla spalla di lui, lungo il braccio, fino
alle dita, come nella figura di un ballo.*

On the motocarrozzella they barely speak.

— *Tutto bene?*
— *Sì. E tu?*
— *Bene.*

The silences are not the same shape. Elena has no wish to put words
to what happened to her: *era un nudo fatto, riguardava il mio
corpo, la sua reattività fisiologica*, the foreign mass of Sarratore
that has receded *come un temporale che non arriva*. Lila is silent
for the opposite reason — *senza parole*, having left every part of
herself with Nino, including her ability to say what's happened.
This dissymmetry depresses Elena. She tries to find in her own
Maronti experience something matching Lila's *doloroso-felice
smarrimento*; she realises she's left nothing behind, not even her
newly-uncovered self — *avevo portato via tutto e perciò non
sentivo l'urgenza, che invece leggevo negli occhi di Lila*, of
going back. Next to Lila she feels *acquitrinosa, terra troppo
intrisa d'acqua.*
