## Summary

Four days later the couple is back. That evening Stefano hosts the
in-laws in the new flat and asks Fernando to confirm the Solara deal
to Lila. Fernando does, sourly. Then Rino is asked to explain the
shoes; he does so with the swagger of a man who knows how the world
works. *Did you want Pasquale, Antonio, and Enzo to take three times
the beating they gave the Solaras for the sake of a pair of size-43
shoes Stefano can't even fit?* Lila — usually the room's best with
words — says nothing. Rino, emboldened by her silence, reminds her
that ever since they were kids she's been the one telling him they
have to get rich.

Right on cue, Maria, Pinuccia, and Alfonso arrive with a tray of
pastries from Spagnuolo (the Solaras' pastry chef). What looked like
a welcome-home party turns out to celebrate something else: **Rino
and Pinuccia's engagement**, with a diamond ring not unlike Lila's
own. Talk turns to who will run the planned Solara-Cerullo shoe shop
in Piazza dei Martiri — Pinuccia? Pinuccia together with Gigliola
Spagnuolo (who has just gotten officially engaged to Michele)?
Everyone is hopeful and loud.

Lila stays mostly on her feet. Sitting hurts. Nobody — not even her
mother, who is silent the whole evening — appears to notice her swollen
black right eye, the split lower lip, the bruises down her arms.
