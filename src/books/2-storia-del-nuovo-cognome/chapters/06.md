## Summary

We get Lila's account of the honeymoon, reconstructed by Elena from
the notebooks. The convertible has barely pulled away from the
reception before Lila is hurling at Stefano the worst dialect insults
a rione woman can throw at a man. He takes it with his usual tight
smile. When she goes silent he tries to drive on. She demands to be
let out; he stops, grips her arm, and explains "calmly."

His version: to keep the shoe factory from closing before it opens,
they had to take on Silvio Solara and sons as partners. The Solaras
guarantee placement in city shops and an autumn opening of a Cerullo-
brand shoe store in Piazza dei Martiri. Marcello agreed on one
condition — Lila's prototype shoes — and **Rino and Fernando went
along**. The honeymoon shoes on Marcello's feet at the reception
weren't Stefano's betrayal alone; they were the whole household's.
Lila punches him in the shoulder, screams she wants to drive home
and watch him repeat the story to "the other two pieces of shit."
The Neapolitan *uommen'e mmerd* finally breaks Stefano's polite-
shopkeeper register: he slaps her, hard.

He then lectures her, half threat, half manifesto: she is not Cerullo
anymore, she is *signora Carracci*, she does what he says, and money
grows only because he makes it grow. If she insults him again like
this, he'll smash her face so she can't leave the house. *Capito?*
She doesn't answer. Her cheek is dark; the rest of her face is
chalk-white.

## Key vocabulary

| Italian | English | Note |
| --- | --- | --- |
| viaggio di nozze | honeymoon | literally "wedding trip" |
| arrendersi facile | to give in easily | what Elena had wrongly assumed of Lila |
| degradare | to degrade, demean | "per poterla degradare" |
| ribrezzo | revulsion, disgust | physical loathing — Lila's word for him |
| incassare gli insulti | to absorb / take the insults | very common idiom |
| accostare | to pull over (driving) | accostare = to draw near |
| portiera | car door | as distinct from porta (a regular door) |
| divincolarsi | to wriggle free | a fight-on-her-arm verb |
| calzaturificio | shoe factory | calzature = footwear |
| ammanigliato/a coi Solara | thick / cosy with the Solaras | informal for being "in" with someone |
| una pezza per lavare il pavimento | a floor rag | how she feels they treat her |
| mappina | rag (Neapolitan) | dialect equivalent — a household rag |
| strunz | "piece of shit" (Neap.) | from *stronzo* |
| uommen'e mmerd | "men of shit" (Neap.) | what finally makes Stefano snap |
| sfiammata dolorosa | a painful flare-up | of the slap on her cheek |
| didascalico/a | preachy, schoolmasterly | of Stefano's lecture |
| azzardati un'altra volta sola a... | just you dare again to... | a classic threat construction |
| la faccia te la rovino | I'll ruin your face for you | dative-of-possession + ruin |
| fare gli occhi a fessura | to narrow one's eyes to slits | fessura = slit |
