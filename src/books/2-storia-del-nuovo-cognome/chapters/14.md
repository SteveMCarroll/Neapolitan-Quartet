## Summary

It takes everything Elena has to convince Antonio that the kiss on
the cheek was nothing — *he's already engaged, you saw it.* He
catches the wobble in her voice and threatens her; they nearly
break up, then reconcile. From that day on his jealousy hardens: he
skips work to stand outside the liceo hoping to catch her with Nino.

His sister **Ada** stops Elena in the street one afternoon, still
in her grease-streaked salumeria apron. Together with Pasquale, she
corners Elena in the courtyard that evening: Antonio is unwell,
short-tempered with their mother Melina, missing work for no reason;
even Gallese the mechanic is alarmed. Ada is blunt: *I've looked
after one madwoman since I was little. Two would be too much, Lenù.
Spend less time with Lina; you'll find the time.* Elena bristles but
goes back to seeing Antonio more.

At the ponds that night Antonio shows her the postcard: he has been
called up; he'll leave in the autumn with Enzo. He drops to the
ground and starts stuffing handfuls of dirt into his mouth. She
holds him and digs the earth out of his mouth with her fingers.
That night she lies awake feeling her hunger to quit school and
marry him quietly drain away. She decides she will help him
somehow, and then peel herself out of the relationship.

The next day she goes to Lila and announces — to test her, really —
that she's going to go to the **Solaras** behind Antonio's back and
ask if they can fix his draft. Lila first mocks her, then turns
nervous, jokes that maybe she herself should have married Marcello.
Then, abruptly serious: *I'll come with you.* Elena protests that
Stefano will be furious. *Chi se ne fotte. If he can go to them, so
can I — I'm his wife.*
