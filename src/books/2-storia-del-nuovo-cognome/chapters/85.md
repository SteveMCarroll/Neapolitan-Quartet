## Summary

Back in Naples after Ischia, Lila considers escapes. Slipping out
at night to return to Forio — Stefano would track her down. Asking
Alfonso to find out from Marisa when Nino would be back from
Ischia — Alfonso would tell Stefano. She finds the Sarratore home
number in the *elenco telefonico* and calls; **Donato** picks up;
she says she's a friend of Nino's; he cuts her off *con voce
risentita* and hangs up. In despair she nearly buys a boat ticket
when, in early September, **Nino appears in the doorway of the
salumeria**, beard grown out and *totalmente ubriaco*.

The shop is crowded. Carmen, not recognising this *ragazzo
sbandato*, has already moved to throw him out. *Ci penso io*, Lila
says, and steers him outside *gesti precisi, voce fredda*, certain
Carmen has not recognised in this drifter the boy they all knew at
elementary school. Outwardly she's *la solita, quella che sa
risolvere ogni problema*. Inwardly she has lost coordinates:
*erano sbiadite le pareti cariche di merci, la strada aveva perso
ogni definizione*, the new buildings have dissolved, *soprattutto
non sentiva il pericolo che stava correndo*. *Nino Nino Nino*: only
joy and desire. Every line of his face proclaims that he has
suffered, is suffering, has been looking for her, wants her — *tanto
che cercava di afferrarla, baciarla per strada.*

She hauls him *home*, the safest place — *passanti? Non ne vide.
Vicini di casa? Non ne vide* — and they begin making love *appena
lei si chiuse la porta dell'appartamento alle spalle. Non sentiva
nessuno scrupolo.* Only need: take him, hold him, keep him. The
need doesn't slacken when they're spent. The rione, the neighbours,
the salumeria, the train sounds, Stefano, the perhaps-anxious
Carmen, all reassemble — but only *come oggetti da sistemare in
fretta evitando non solo che fossero di intralcio, ma anche
badando a che impilati alla rinfusa non cadessero all'improvviso.*

Nino scolds her for leaving without warning, takes her again,
demands they go away immediately, together — *non sapeva dire
dove.* She answers *sì, sì, sì*, shares his madness — and unlike
him feels real time, *i secondi e i minuti veri che scivolando via
ingigantivano il rischio di essere sorpresi*. Lying with him on
the floor, she looks at the *lampadario* over them as a threat:
having before been concerned only with having Nino now-now-now,
she shifts to the strategic question of *come continuare a
tenerlo stretto a sé senza che il lampadario si staccasse dal
soffitto, senza che il pavimento si spezzasse e lui precipitasse
per sempre da un lato, lei dall'altro.* — *Vattene.* — *No.* —
*Sei pazzo.* — *Sì.* — *Ti prego, per favore, va' via.* He goes.

She waits — for Carmen to say something, for the neighbours to
gossip, for Stefano to come back from the other salumeria and beat
her. Nothing. Relief. She gives Carmen a raise, turns *affettuosa
col marito*, and invents the cover stories that will let her keep
meeting **Nino in secret**.
