## Summary

Lila tells the whole story. She'd been leaning on Nino *per stare a
galla*, exhausted from the swim, and *si era approfittato della
vicinanza*. She kept her lips clamped shut against the tongue he
tried to push between them. *Sei pazzo, sono sposata*, she said.
He answered: *io ti voglio bene da molto prima di tuo marito, fin
da quando abbiamo fatto quella gara in classe.* She told him not
to do it again and they swam back. *M'ha fatto male alle labbra
per quanto premeva*, she finishes, *e mi fa male ancora.*

Elena, miraculously, says nothing she means. *Non andare sulla
montagna con lui a meno che non ci accompagni anche Bruno*, Lila
warns. *Con freddezza* Elena says it wouldn't matter — *io non ero
sposata e nemmeno fidanzata* — and adds, vicious: *peccato solo
che non mi piace: il suo bacio mi farebbe l'effetto di mettere la
bocca sopra un topo morto.* A pretended yawn. Lila looks at her
*di affetto e insieme di ammirazione*, says goodnight, walks out.
The instant the door closes, Elena starts crying — and *fino
all'alba, non feci che piangere.*

The rest of the chapter is one long internal collapse. *Baciava
Nadia, baciava me, baciava Lila* — how can Nino be *tanto profondo
nell'affrontare i grandi problemi del mondo, quanto superficiale
nei sentimenti d'amore?* She tears herself apart: bassina, *troppo
piena, occhialuta, diligente ma non intelligente*; she has no
right to think a boy like that would ever want her. Why does she
never say plainly what she feels — to Lila, to herself? Why is
she so quick with the *sorriso aggraziato* whenever things go
wrong? Why, every time, does she find a plausible excuse for the
person hurting her? Toward dawn she makes herself believe a story
that lets the bruise scab over: Nino *aveva sinceramente creduto
di amare Nadia*, but here in Ischia he has met Lila and realised
she has been *fin dall'infanzia, e sarebbe stata sempre in
futuro, il suo vero unico amore.* That makes the kiss bearable
because impossible — *come il mio per lui.* She thinks she might
go back to Naples tonight with Stefano and Rino, invent a letter
from her mother. Then she falls asleep. When she jolts awake, the
*formulario* in her head has *addomesticato un po' il dolore*.
She runs to the appointment.
