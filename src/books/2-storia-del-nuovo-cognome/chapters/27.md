## Summary

Inauguration day at Piazza dei Martiri. Lila arrives in the
convertible looking unwell again. She drags Elena off to look at via
dei Mille shop windows — and brings up the **green-dressed girl in
the bowler hat** from the long-ago piazza brawl that ended with
Michele swinging an iron bar. *Are you prettier than her now?* Elena
lies and says yes, then thinks privately: no. It's not about money
any more. Inequality goes deeper than the till of two salumerie and
a shoe shop. *Lei ci era superiore, così, senza volerlo. E questo era
insopportabile.*

Inside it's a second wedding: Solaras, Carraccis, Cerullos, all in
the same suits as at Lila's wedding. **The defaced photo** towers
over the room — Lila is "an image of a one-eyed goddess" pushing
her well-shod feet into the centre of the floor. **Alfonso** is the
day's revelation: lit up, elegant, animated, working the room like
he was born to it. He kisses Lila on both cheeks and says, *I've
always known you were dangerous.* Elena wonders if his future may
not lie in school after all but here, in this richer Naples.

At dusk a sign blazes on outside: **SOLARA**. Lila murmurs: *they
caved on this too.* She baits Rino — *if the shoes are Cerullo why
is the shop Solara?* — and lets him have it: *from now on, every man
for himself*. She turns on the heel and **walks off into the square
with Michele**, arm in arm, circling the stone lions while Stefano's
eyes track her and Gigliola whispers furiously to Pinuccia. Then
Lila comes back, ducks into the toilet, comes out very pale, and
grabs Elena: *Ho un po' di sangue. Che significa, il bambino è
morto?*
