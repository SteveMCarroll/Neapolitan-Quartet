## Summary

Lila launches into a manic period. She vomits every morning, then
walks to the half-built **new salumeria** to bully workmen into
shelf positions; she invades the Piazza dei Martiri shop too,
climbing ladders and rearranging Michele's space; she takes over
the **old salumeria** as well, sidelining Maria and Pinuccia, doubling
Ada's pay, and freeing Alfonso to study. She hands out cash from
the till as if it were tinfoil: Ada for Antonio's draft expenses,
Pasquale for an urgent triple tooth-extraction, Elena (refused) for
school books. The friends are uncomfortable about it but take it.
*La mortadella si vende, le scarpe pure,* Pasquale tells Elena
darkly. *Lina è stata sempre amica nostra* — she invented the
Cerullo shoes, the money is in some sense hers.

She turns up unannounced at the Sea Garden one Sunday, alone, by
metro and two buses. She tells Elena that Stefano has caved: the
giant wedding portrait *will* go in the Piazza dei Martiri shop after
all, in exchange for letting Pinuccia and Gigliola jointly run it.
Lila has won the bet — Elena now has to get 8s. She also lays bare
what the pregnancy feels like: *i maschi ti inseriscono il loro coso
e diventi una scatola di carne con un pupazzo vivo dentro. Lo so che
devo pensare cose belle, ma non ne vedo.* She doesn't think she's
"made" to be a mother. Elena pushes back gently — try, see — and
hands her Linda, the youngest girl. Lila warms up; she holds Linda
over the bar fountain, both laughing.

Then a yelp. Linda has fallen against the basin's rim and split her
chin. Lila freezes: *È stata sua sorella a farla cadere, non sono
stata io* — her sister knocked her down, it wasn't me. Elena
patches Linda up with the lifeguard. When she gets back, Lila is
gone. The next day the *cartolaia* tells Elena her daughters have
had enough sun for the summer; she's let go. She doesn't tell Lila.
