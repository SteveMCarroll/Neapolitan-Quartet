## Summary

Worn down by pressure, Lila pretends to give in: tells Stefano and
Michele she'll design new shoes — and then **tells Rino in private
to do them himself, with their father**. Cover for her until the
shoes sell. If they flop, the blame is hers; if they succeed, she
will publicly hand him the credit. Rino loves the deal. He starts
coming to her secretly with models — and she's startled to find
they're good: coherent with the existing line but genuinely
reinvented. Maybe — she admits to Elena, lighter for it — *the
original shoes weren't mine either; they really were my brother's.*
The old affection for Rino re-opens. After every consult he asks
her in a whisper for the apartment keys: he's still seeing Pinuccia.

Elena, doggedly trying to keep Lila inside her life, takes her on
Sunday outings. Two of Elena's classmates come on a trip to the
Mostra d'Oltremare and freeze up when they hear Lila is a married
woman: *Have you got a child?* No. *No children coming?* No. The
evening dies. Better is the night Elena drags Lila to a lecture by
**Giuseppe Montalenti** on Darwin in a bare cultural-circle room.
Galiani sent her; Galiani doesn't show. Lila refuses to leave;
listens to the end. Walking home: *Ha detto una cosa che sapevo
già — sei una scimmia.* Lila is dead serious: *Non me lo voglio
dimenticare più — che siamo animali.* They tease out the
differences (her pierced ears versus a monkey's) and laugh.

Back at the *stradone* Pasquale and Ada stop them: **Stefano has
been hunting Lila everywhere, in a panic.** He sends Pasquale and
Ada to drive her home in their place. Elena only learns the reason
the next day: **Pinuccia is pregnant by Rino**. Worse — Pinuccia
has been sneaking sex with Rino in Stefano's apartment, with Lila
handing her the keys. Worst of all, when Stefano slapped his
sister, Pinuccia threw at him: *you're jealous because I'm a real
woman and Lila isn't, because Rino knows how to do it with women
and you don't.* Lila, watching Stefano fly out the door, laughed —
to keep him from killing her. He went out, she tells Elena, *to
find a whore.*
