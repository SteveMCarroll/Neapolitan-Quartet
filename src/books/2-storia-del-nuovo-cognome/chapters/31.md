## Summary

Three theories of the fire: Rino says **Gigliola set it on purpose**
with her lighter; Michele says it was the lighter but by accident;
**Gigliola says the photo caught fire by itself** — *like the devil
when he takes a woman's shape and the saints call on Jesus and he
turns to flame.* She broadcasts the rumour that Lila is *a
fattucchiera zoccola* — a slut witch — who can refuse to get
pregnant and let babies "flow away" at will.

Michele begins coming to the new salumeria every other day —
flirting with Lila, flirting with Carmen as cover. Lila pushes back
with venom-mock: *better get out of here, we're witches, very
dangerous.* She is unwell. A bruise on the arm? Stefano caressed me
too hard. Red eyes? Not crying from sadness, crying from joy.
Gigliola plays it for war; she works on Pinuccia; the two fiancées
push Stefano and Rino into an ambush. The brothers-in-law confront
Michele under the bar: *leave her alone, she has to work.* Michele
sees through it: *non la volete capire le nostre necessità
commerciali. E se non le volete capire, ci devo per forza pensare
io.* They argue. Compromise: **Lila will design new shoes** by
winter; she **will not** run the Piazza dei Martiri shop, for now;
discussion deferred to after the summer with a threat in it.

Elena goes to the salumeria. Lila looks relieved. Then she says
quietly: *I'm not going to design anything. Not a sandal, not a
slipper.* And she explains: **the shoes only came out of her head
that one time, when she was twelve, and they will not come again.**
She can't go back to that small workshop, or that bond with Rino;
the affection has gone dry; *quel gioco è finito, non sapeva farlo
ricominciare.* The whole Cerullo invention, she admits with a
nervous laugh, was *soprattutto un modo per dimostrarti che sapevo
fare bene le cose anche se non venivo più a scuola.* Then she
pivots and says she has learned how to cheat on the scale at the
deli — Stefano taught her, then she invented her own ways. *Non
fidarti di quello che dico e faccio.* And then: *Aiutami. Stammi
vicino. Anche se sei meglio di me, anche se sai più cose, non mi
lasciare.*
