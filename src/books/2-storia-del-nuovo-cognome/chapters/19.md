## Summary

Elena scrapes through the school year — sevens in humanities, sixes
in sciences, an 8 in conduct that's really a slap from the religion
teacher and the council. No scholarship. Her mother goes for the
carpet-beater. Elena tries to find Antonio at home, at work, in the
shop where Ada now works — Ada turns her face away. She drifts to
school during the *maturità* exams hoping to see Nino. He's there,
but so is *the pretty, well-bred girl*, who turns up to wait for him
with her mouth ready. Elena flees.

She turns to Lila for comfort, carrying a guilty secret: she never
told Lila about the trip to the dressmaker with Stefano. Lila is
warm, lets her use the bath, but no longer confides anything back.
That afternoon, with Pinuccia and Rino over, the talk turns to the
Piazza dei Martiri shop. Pinuccia is angling to run it — preferably
without Gigliola — and is dead set against Lila's wedding photo
going on the wall. *I'll be running that store,* she insists. Lila is
indifferent. Elena, asked her opinion, says diplomatically that
Stefano has already decided no — he went to the dressmaker on purpose
to retrieve the photo.

Lila, quiet up to now, says: *Quanto vuoi scommettere che hai torto?*
Want to bet you're wrong? Stefano will give the permission. The
stakes: if Elena loses, she has to get back to school and never
again be promoted with less than an 8 average. And if Lila loses?
*I enrol in a private school, get back to studying, and I swear I'll
take the* licenza *with you and better than you.* Pinuccia gets
catty about babies (*when are you making me an aunt?*). Lila just
says *Chi lo sa.*
