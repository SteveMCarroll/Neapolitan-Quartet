## Summary

Sunday opens hot and slow. Elena slept badly, *non osavo aprire la
finestra per timore delle zanzare*, and wakes at ten with *una
sensazione di mancanza che mi diede angoscia.* She gives up the
idea of going to Barano: *passare la giornata a giocare con Ciro,
Pino e Clelia, mentre Nino faceva lunghe nuotate o se ne stava al
sole senza dire una parola, in polemica sorda con suo padre?* In
the kitchen Nunzia tells her Stefano and Lila are still asleep
and Pinuccia and Rino are already on the beach. Elena dunks bread
in caffellatte without appetite and walks down.

On the beach, Rino is face-down asleep, his *capelli bagnati* and
*corpo pesante abbandonato a pancia sotto sulla sabbia*. Pinuccia
paces the *battigia*, refusing to go anywhere with her. Elena
walks the long way toward Forio to calm down, comes back, swims,
sunbathes, and overhears the couple from a few metres away. Rino
must work next week (*le scarpe devono essere pronte per
l'autunno*) but reassures Pinuccia he loves her, kisses her
belly. Pinuccia begs him to stay. He can't. Then she says she
wants to go with him. *Non la voglio fare più, questa
villeggiatura.* Why? *Perché appena mi addormento faccio
bruttissimi sogni e sto sveglia tutta la notte. Anche quando dormo
con tua sorella — ancora di più, tua sorella se mi potesse
uccidere, mi ucciderebbe.* Elena hears something true under the
*lagna* — Pinuccia is *cercando di dirgli qualcosa che lei stessa
ignorava*.

The mood breaks when Lila arrives with Stefano. She wants to show
him how she's learned to swim. They push out *in alto mare* — really
only a few metres — and she leaves him behind with her clean
strokes, then floats back to wait, laughing, while he plods up
*sbuffando contro l'acqua*. In the afternoon, since Pinuccia is
too pregnant to ride and Lila is going out with Stefano, Rino
takes Elena on his Lambretta: *vieni tu, Lenù*. Wind, fear of
falling, the violent *eccitazione*, the smell of Rino's sweat,
his *smargiassa pienezza di sé* that breaks every traffic rule and
threatens anyone who protests — Elena finds it *un ritorno alle
emozioni di ragazzina mal cresciuta*. That evening, when the
husbands leave for the *vaporetto*, Stefano tells *her* to give
Nino's friend his regards as though Lila and Pinuccia hadn't even
met the boys. Pinuccia recovers her spirits and goes to wash
her hair. Lila drifts to the bedroom, doesn't undress, opens the
stolen *Hiroshima il giorno dopo*. Elena pokes her head in: *com'è
che all'improvviso ti è tornata la voglia di leggere?* Lila,
without looking up: *non sono fatti tuoi.*
