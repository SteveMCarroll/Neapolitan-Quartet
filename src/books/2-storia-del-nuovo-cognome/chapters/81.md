## Summary

The hectic week before leaving. A litany of farewells: her mother,
*immusonita*, half threat half hostage-note — *l'ho sempre saputo
che ti credevi meglio di me e di tutti quanti*; her father playing
the dying patriarch — *ho un dolore qua… non so se quando torni mi
trovi ancora vivo*; her brothers wheedling for sleepovers in Pisa;
Pasquale's warning *sta' attenta a dove ti porta tutto questo
studio, Lenù. Ricordati chi sei e da che parte stai*; a fragile
Carmen still wrecked over Giuseppina, in tears; Alfonso, *restò di
stucco* and murmuring *lo sapevo che avresti continuato a
studiare*; Antonio repeating *mi sento proprio bene, Lenù, mi è
passato tutto, era fare il soldato che mi faceva male*; Enzo's
silent bone-crushing handshake; Ada, with a little laugh: *gliel'hai
detto a Lina, eh, gliel'hai detto? Diglielo, così schiatta.*

Elena assumes word will have reached Lila already through Alfonso,
Carmen, or Stefano (via Ada). If she hasn't come to congratulate,
the news must already have *disturbato* her; turning up after a
year of barely speaking just to break it in person would feel
gloating. She writes to Nella for Oliviero's address, collects an
old suitcase from a cousin, and tours the houses where she's
tutored, picking up final fees and being given coffee and small
gifts in return. She crosses via Garibaldi, climbs the *Tribunali*,
takes a bus from piazza Dante up to the Vomero, then funicolare to
piazza Amedeo, and realises she's a short walk from piazza dei
Martiri. She heads down via Filangieri *incerta sul da farsi.*

She remembers the shop's grand opening, Lila dressed *da gran
signora*, the anxiety at not really being changed. **Io invece sono
veramente cambiata.** Same rags, but the diploma is hers and Pisa
is hers. *Sono cambiata non all'apparenza, ma in profondità.
L'apparenza verrà presto e non sarà più apparenza.* She stops at an
optician's window, picks out lighter, round frames; she'll pin her
hair up; she'll learn makeup.

In piazza dei Martiri the shop has its *saracinesca* three-quarters
down. She knocks. Nothing. Knocks again. *Chi è?* — *Elena.* —
*Lenù!* Lila lifts the shutter. She looks changed: *aveva una
camicetta bianca e una gonna stretta blu, era pettinata, truccata
con la solita cura. Ma il viso le si era come allargato e
appiattito, l'intero suo corpo mi sembrò più largo e più piatto.*
She pulls Elena inside and lowers the shutter. The interior is
*sfarzosamente illuminato* and looks more like a salon than a shoe
shop. *Che bella cosa ti è successa, Lenù*, Lila says with such
naked sincerity that Elena believes her. *Sono proprio contenta che
sei venuta a salutarmi.* Two kisses, tears welling. Then, toward
the bathroom door: ***Vieni, Nino, puoi uscire, è Lenuccia.***

Elena loses her breath. Nino emerges in his usual head-down,
hands-in-pockets pose, *la faccia scavata dalla tensione*, mumbles
*ciao*, shakes her hand without strength. Lila lays it out in a
single fast bouquet of sentences: **they have been meeting in
secret here for nearly a year; she had decided for Elena's sake not
to drag her into it any further; she is two months pregnant; she's
going to confess everything to Stefano and leave him.**
