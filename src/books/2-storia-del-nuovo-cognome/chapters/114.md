## Summary

Lila ventures out of Rinuccio's room only when Stefano and Ada aren't there. She makes the boy's meals, picks at her own, hears the rione's gossip and lets it run. One late November afternoon the phone rings.

*«Tra dieci minuti sono lì»*. She recognises him without surprise. *«Va bene»*. Then: *«Enzo». — «Sì». — «Non sei obbligato». — «Lo so». — «Ci sono di mezzo i Solara». — «Me ne fotto dei Solara»*.

He arrives exactly ten minutes later. She has packed her things and the baby's into two suitcases and left every jewel on the bedside table, including the engagement ring and the wedding band. *«È la seconda volta che me ne vado, ma questa volta non torno indietro»*. Enzo looks around — he has never been in this house. She tugs his arm: *«Stefano potrebbe arrivare all'improvviso, certe volte lo fa». — «E che problema c'è?»*. He touches expensive things — a vase, an ashtray, the silver — flicks through her shopping notebook, then turns and asks her a direct question: is she sure. He says he's found a job in a factory in San Giovanni a Teduccio and rented three rooms there, with a dim kitchen. *«Ma tutto quello che ti ha dato Stefano non lo avrai più: io non te le posso dare. Forse hai paura perché sei poco convinta»*.

*«Sono convinta»*, she answers, picking the baby up impatiently, *«e non ho paura di niente. Andiamo»*. Enzo lingers a moment longer. He tears a page out of the shopping notebook and writes something. He leaves it on the table. *«Che hai scritto?» — «L'indirizzo di San Giovanni». — «Perché?» — «Non stiamo giocando a nascondino»*. Finally he picks up the suitcases and starts down the stairs. Lila locks the door behind her and leaves the key in the lock.
