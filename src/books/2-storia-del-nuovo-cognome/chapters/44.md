## Summary

A long day at the Maronti: Elena plays with the kids and bathes
with them; **Lila and Pinuccia** spend the afternoon entirely
swallowed up by **Donato Sarratore's** charm. He walks them all
the way to the hot springs and back. To spare Pinuccia's exhausted
legs Donato puts them on a little wooden bath house *come una
palafitta* and they hire a boat for the trip home.

On the water, away from his earshot, Lila ironises: *Nino didn't
give you the time of day.* Elena defends him. Pinuccia weighs in:
*how nice the father is, how rude the son is.* Both girls believe
Nino snubbed Elena and Elena lets them. *Mi sarebbe più facile per
loro digerire* — easier to forgive him for ignoring *them* if even
a top student got nothing from him. *Volevo proteggerlo dal loro
astio.* They drop the subject; Pinuccia gushes about Donato's
*modi da gran signore*; Lila says, satisfied, *Mi ha insegnato a
stare a galla, e anche come si nuota. È bravo.*

The sun starts to set; the sky turns violet, the air cools. The
old memory of Donato's hand on her in the dark moves through Elena
and she says: *He's the one who wrote that the panel in Piazza dei
Martiri is ugly.* Pinuccia smiles. Lila answers calmly: ***Aveva
ragione.*** Elena tries again: *He's the one who destroyed
Melina.* Lila laughs: ***O forse, almeno per una volta, l'ha fatta
stare bene.*** It is a slap. Elena, stung, decides to break her
own secrecy on the spot. *Nino has given me his address. If you
don't mind, when Stefano and Rino arrive I'll go see him.* She
spells it out — *indirizzo, andare a trovarlo* — adult words.
Lila narrows her eyes; a sharp vertical line cuts her forehead.
Pinuccia, malicious: *capito? Lenuccia ha un appuntamento.* Elena,
flushing: *if you're with your husbands, what am I supposed to do?*
A long pause; just the motor and the silent helmsman. Then Lila,
cold: ***Fai compagnia a mammà. Mica ti ho portata qua per farti
divertire.*** Elena swallows it. *Avevamo avuto una settimana di
libertà* — for one week Pinuccia and Lila had been *donne-bambine*
under an "anomalous father" who, instead of punishing them,
encouraged their desires. Tomorrow the husbands return. Elena's
announcement has reminded both of them that their week of
suspension from wifehood is over.
