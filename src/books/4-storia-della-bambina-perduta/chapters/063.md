## Summary

The hours after the crisis are terrible. **Nino** comes home shaken, speaking dialect and turning **Immacolata**'s hospitalization into a point of honor: he cannot bear the idea that **Marcello** might move her to some corrupt private facility. He phones influential people, but whenever he names the Solara family the conversations grow complicated and he falls silent. Elena tries not to show her panic, because she fears he will rush back to the hospital.

At six in the morning **Lila** calls. She has spent the night in the hospital. Marcello seemed to give up and left; Lila then slipped through stairs and corridors until she found Immacolata in what Elena calls a *stanza dell'agonia* ("room of agony"), among five suffering women. Immacolata lay rigid, begging the Madonna to let her die at once. Lila crouched beside her and calmed her, then escaped before the nurses' day shift began. Elena senses that Lila's joking pleasure in breaking rules is partly an act to hide her exhaustion.

Nino wants to speak to Lila, but Elena lies and says she has hung up. They plan to go to the hospital with **Imma** bundled against the cold: Nino will wait in the car while Elena visits between feedings. But when they arrive, neither Lila nor Immacolata is there. Immacolata has already been discharged.
