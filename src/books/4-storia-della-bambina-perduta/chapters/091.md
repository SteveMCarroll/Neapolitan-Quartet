## Summary

Agitated, Elena goes to **Lila**'s apartment, where the children are. **Elsa** and **Dede** greet her half-heartedly, while **Imma** and **Tina** smother her with kisses. Elena barely attends to them and shows Lila *Panorama*, saying the Solaras are angry. Lila reads calmly and says only that the photos are beautiful.

Elena wants to protest that her book is literature, not reportage. Lila answers that she remembers many recognizable things in it: *un po' romanzo, un po' no* — "partly a novel, partly not." This wounds Elena almost as much as the Solaras' reaction. Meanwhile the girls look at the magazine. Elsa tells Tina she is in the paper, then cruelly tells Imma she is absent because Tina is beautiful and Imma is ugly. Dede reads the mistaken caption and argues that, because Imma is named Sarratore rather than Airota, she is not really Elena's daughter. Elena, exhausted, tries to leave, but Lila persuades them to stay for dinner.

Then Lila calms her. Speaking in an unexpected formal Italian, she invokes the earthquake and the chaos inside and outside human beings. The Solaras are dangerous animals, she says, but Michele and Marcello are still only flesh, money, and threats. Their fury at Elena's book and the article is really fury at Lila. Elena slowly relaxes, rereads the article, and admits it may help the book. Before dinner, Imma asks whether Elena's daughter is herself or Tina. Lila answers that they are mothers to both girls. When **Enzo** comes home, he proudly hangs the photo of Tina in his office, removing the wrong caption.
