## Summary

Elena waits eagerly for the *Panorama* feature. At first she is proud: the press office is doing well, and she imagines herself at the center of a whole photographic spread. But a week passes, then two, and nothing appears. By late March the book is in stores, and Elena is busy with interviews and a Milan presentation in the same bookstore as fifteen years earlier. The audience is larger than before, mostly women, and some respond warmly to the protagonist's complex humanity. Elena returns to Naples exhausted.

On the way home, dragging her suitcase, she is approached by a car. **Michele** is driving, with **Marcello** beside him. Elena remembers the old fear of the Solara brothers trying to pull girls into their car, and instinctively protects the bracelet from her mother on her wrist. Marcello does not greet her with his usual false family warmth.

Michele is furious. He asks what filth Elena has written about the place where she was born, his family, the people who watched her grow up, and Naples itself. He hands her the fresh issue of *Panorama*. Elena sees a large color photo of herself sitting with **Tina**, captioned "Elena Greco with her daughter Tina." At first she thinks this mistake is the issue and says they got it wrong. Michele answers that "they" did not get it wrong — "you two" did. When Elena asks who he means, Marcello says Lina is manipulating her and she does not even notice. The car speeds away, leaving Elena on the pavement with the magazine.
