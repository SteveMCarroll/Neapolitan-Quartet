## Summary

Elena understands that Lila's announcement is not a passing bad mood. Lila says that if **Enzo** can run Basic Sight alone, fine; otherwise they will sell it. When Elena asks what she will do instead, Lila answers that perhaps one need not do anything. She wants, she says, *perdere tempo* — "to waste time." She also wants to detach Enzo from her and sleep alone. Elena presses her about affection, medicine, and love, but Lila dismisses sex as overrated and says Elena has already forgotten Tina, while she has not.

Elena hears Enzo and Lila quarrel more and more. Enzo's voice is low and despairing: everything has been damaged — Tina, the work, their relationship — and Lila is letting it all worsen. For once he says harshly that Lina has never had balance. Elena disagrees. Lila still has good days, when she is calm, affectionate, and curious about Elena's travels, writing, and daughters.

In one generous mood Lila brings Elena an old computer and teaches her how to use it. Elena is thrilled by the miraculous writing on the screen, though terrified of losing work in a power cut. Lila jokes to **Dede**, **Elsa**, and **Imma** that their mother has the enthusiasm of someone who understands nothing. She often takes the girls to the office to teach them the newer machines, partly to win them back, but cannot recover Dede and Elsa's trust. Soon the girls, especially Elsa, know more than Elena. Dede also draws **Gennaro/Rino** toward computers, and Lila laughs that Dede is changing her son.
