## Summary

**Elena** takes refuge in the car with **Imma** on her lap and drives without a destination. Imma is delighted to be close to her mother, babbling and trying to honk the horn as **Elsa** has taught her. Elena ends up below Sant'Elmo, turns off the engine, and realizes she has no tears. She is not yet suffering; she is *raggelata dall'orrore* — frozen by horror.

She cannot make the man she saw in the bathroom fit with any stable version of **Nino**. Was that the adolescent boy she loved, or some alien creature wearing the skin of her third daughter's father? She thinks of the many versions of him: the man who got **Silvia** pregnant, **Mariarosa**'s lover, **Eleonora**'s unfaithful husband, the married man who claimed to love Elena.

At first she tries to cling to the tender Nino of the neighborhood and school. But the memory of his expression in the mirror forces clarity. There is no split: Nino is one person. His face reminds her not of an alien but of his father **Donato**, when Donato touched her long ago. Nino is *laido* — sordid — and always has been. The thought does not comfort her. Then she feels warmth spreading over her lap: naked Imma has urinated on her.
