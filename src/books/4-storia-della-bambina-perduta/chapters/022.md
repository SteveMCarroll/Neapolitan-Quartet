## Summary

Elena raises the children again with **Pietro** and sees that, despite complaints, he will accept any arrangement that leaves him free for **Doriana**. She goes to Naples to tell **Nino** in person that she is ready to move there with **Dede** and **Elsa**. In his shabby Via Duomo apartment he is overjoyed, promises to find them a home quickly, and offers to handle the practical annoyances. Elena imagines a bright apartment with a sea view, enough money from Pietro and her writing, and a productive adult return to Naples.

She avoids telling **Lila**, fearing interference, and instead calls **Carmen**, with whom she has grown close through the search for **Pasquale**. Elena has met **Armando Galiani**, now a doctor and Democrazia proletaria figure, who praised her book but gave evasive news about **Nadia** and none about Pasquale. Carmen's quiet pain makes Elena fond of her.

They meet in a Via Duomo bar. Carmen listens as Elena describes her elegant plans, then announces that Lila is coming. While they wait, Carmen praises Lila's generosity: she cares for friends, family, **Antonio**, even **Stefano**, and wants to start her own computer business. Elena hears Lila's gestures in Carmen and wonders whether the neighborhood is settling into Lila.

When Lila arrives, beautiful and composed, she says Elena's mother is ill and then delivers the real news: she has asked Antonio to follow Nino. In dry Italian she reports that Nino never left **Eleonora**, still lives with his wife and son, and has just been rewarded with a prestigious research post backed by his father-in-law's bank. Elena says she believes her and leaves before giving Lila the reaction she wants.
