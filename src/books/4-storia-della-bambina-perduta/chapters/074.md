## Summary

On a cold autumn morning, **Elena** drives **Dede** and **Elsa** to school and finds herself separating the old **Nino** from the man she now lives with. The childhood love, she thinks, may have been only a private fantasy; the later passion for him in Milan and Florence may be something else entirely. Between the two stands the break in her love for **Lila**, a *frattura* — a fracture — that should have erased Nino from her life.

Elena had arranged to be out all morning so she could work at the Biblioteca nazionale. Instead, ordinary life interrupts her fantasy: she remembers she has forgotten diapers for **Imma**. Angry at her own disorganization, and unable to rely on Nino or send **Silvana** out, she returns to via Tasso, buys the diapers, and expects to hear Imma screaming.

The apartment is silent. Elena glimpses Imma playing without a diaper, then follows a noise to the large bathroom. There, first in the long mirror and then in reality, she sees Nino having sex with Silvana. Elena shuts the door just as they see her. Nino calls after her, but she grabs Imma and runs down the stairs without waiting for the elevator.
