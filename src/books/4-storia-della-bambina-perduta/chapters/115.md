## Summary

Elena sometimes escapes the apartment, the stairs between her rooms and Lila's, the gardens, and the stradone by traveling for work. She feels relief in elegant clothes and in the world of publishing, cinema, television, congresses, and artistic spectacle. **Lila**, by contrast, remains fixed at the center of her horror. When Elena gets an invitation to the San Carlo opera house and tries to take her, Lila refuses; **Carmen** goes instead. Only a new pain can distract Lila from the old one.

One evening Lila hears that **Gennaro** has started injecting drugs again. Without telling **Enzo**, she goes to retrieve him from **Stefano**, who now lives with **Marisa** in the house where Lila once lived as a bride. Stefano is ill, frightened after his heart attack, and surrounded by demands for money from **Ada**, **Pinuccia**, Marisa, and his mother. Lila understands that he wants her money, using Gennaro as the excuse. When she says she will not give another cent, the quarrel explodes. Marisa screams that **Alfonso** was ruined by Lila and that whoever stole Tina did well.

Lila goes silent, shaken in the old white living room that was once hers. Stefano takes her arm and they go to **Rino** Cerullo's house, where Gennaro is staying. Another fight erupts among father, son, mother, and uncle. Lila drags Gennaro away. Rino follows, begging for money. When Gennaro pleads that she give it, Lila drives her brother off and asks her son if he wants to end up like his uncle.
