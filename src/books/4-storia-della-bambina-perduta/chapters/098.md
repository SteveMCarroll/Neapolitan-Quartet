## Summary

**Elena** presses Roberto until he gives her the address of **Carmen**'s relatives in Giugliano, then drives there with **Imma** in suffocating heat. The relatives brusquely claim Carmen has returned to Naples, but Elena, unconvinced, soon runs into her carrying shopping bags. Carmen bursts into tears, embraces Elena and Imma, and the three sit at a shaded café table while Imma is ordered to play quietly.

Carmen confirms what **Lila** had said: she was forced to file a complaint, *querelarmi* (to sue or denounce Elena). **Marcello** had made her believe he knew where **Pasquale** was hiding and could have him killed whenever he chose. Elena tries to reason with her. If the Solaras really knew where Pasquale was, she says, they would already have taken him; the only way to protect him is to hand him over to the carabinieri. Carmen hears this as betrayal, not safety.

The conversation dissolves into grief, guilt, and confusion. Elena realizes she is no longer sure what she wants from Carmen: justice, protection, revenge, or the withdrawal of the complaint. She leaves forgiving her, but Carmen flares in anger, accusing Elena of losing nothing and even gaining publicity. Elena drives home bitterly, afraid Carmen will now report everything to the Solaras.
