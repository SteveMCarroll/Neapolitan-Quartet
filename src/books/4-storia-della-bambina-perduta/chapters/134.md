## Summary

Elena looks for reliable lawyers and, after many calls, even tries to reach **Nino**, now the only parliamentarian she knows personally. She cannot speak to him directly, but when his secretary finally offers an appointment Elena coldly says she will bring their daughter. Soon the secretary calls back: the Honorable Sarratore will be delighted to see them. The place and time keep changing until the meeting is finally set at Montecitorio. Elena and **Imma** dress up and go to Rome; Imma brings her cherished campaign flyer and studies the photo on the train.

At Montecitorio Elena repeatedly says, for Imma's benefit, that they are expected by the Honorable Sarratore and that the child is his daughter, Imma Sarratore. Nino arrives radiant and elegant with an attractive young secretary. He embraces Imma with theatrical warmth, holds her in his arms, and she instantly clings to him, delighted that he is even more handsome than the flyer.

Elena explains **Pasquale**'s situation while Nino keeps charming Imma. He agrees to check his health and legal protections, but insists Pasquale should collaborate like **Nadia** and that murderers' victims have rights too. Elena leaves angry and unhappy, though for Imma it is the most important day of her life so far.

Nino later keeps his word. He tells Elena what lawyers have not: Nadia's detailed confessions already place Pasquale at the center of major political crimes, and now she is attributing more to him, including the murders of **Gino**, **Bruno Soccavo**, **Manuela Solara**, and the Solara brothers. Nino warns Elena to tell **Lila** to be careful: Nadia has always hated her.
