## Summary

Elena reconstructs the murder of **Marcello** and **Michele** Solara, shot one December Sunday in 1986 outside the church where they were baptized. The neighborhood instantly knows the details of their deaths: Michele dies at once, while Marcello sits on a step trying and failing to button his jacket. Yet when people are asked who fired, certainty dissolves. The killers become one man, two men with a woman driving, three masked men, or almost no one at all.

**Carmen**'s version makes the scene look like effects without causes: bodies fall, wives and children run, but the shooters vanish from the story. **Armando** and other journalists interview Elena, and soon the newspapers publish huge lists of the Solaras' crimes and possessions. Elena realizes that what she and **Lila** had exposed while they were alive was almost nothing.

But she also knows what the papers do not: as girls, she and Lila had admired the brothers' beauty; Marcello wanted to marry Lila and later married **Elisa**; Michele recognized Lila's powers and loved her ruinously. Elena understands that respectable Naples had always been inside the Solara world — buying, celebrating, accepting money, enduring violence, pretending nothing was happening. Even **Pasquale** seemed more clearly separable than the Solaras. Later, after they blur into the list of Naples's dead, a boy brings Elena a red case from a jeweler: Marcello's note says only *scusa* ("sorry"), and inside is her polished bracelet.
