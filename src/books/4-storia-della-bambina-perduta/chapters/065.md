## Summary

The clinic soon becomes a meeting place for the old and young neighborhood. Elena sees **Vittorio** sleeping beside **Immacolata**, frightened and wordless. **Elisa**, **Peppe**, and **Gianni** behave with more confidence: they scold nurses, tip them lavishly, and invoke the Solara name to prove their mother is *roba dei Solara* — "Solara property." Elena is ashamed, yet she cannot deny that the alternative was the hospital.

Visits from **Carmen** and **Alfonso** make the mornings easier. They come partly to honor Immacolata, but mostly to support Elena, waiting in corridors or cars so she can get back to school pickup. Their presence joins her dying mother's neighborhood to the new one forming around **Lila**. Carmen speaks of Lila almost as if she has magical powers. Alfonso, in a rare confidence, says Lila taught him clarity: without her he would have remained trapped in a false self. He describes his desire for men, his painful marriage to **Marisa**, and the childhood shame of being treated as the family's disgrace.

Both Carmen and Alfonso then ask more of Elena. When **Marcello** appears, Carmen admits she is trying to be friendly to the Solaras only for **Pasquale**'s sake, though she hates them. Alfonso fears Marcello most of all, convinced Marcello blames him for **Michele**'s madness and may kill him. Elena realizes they want her fully reintegrated into the neighborhood, beside Lila as another protective power.
