## Summary

**Lila**'s call ruins the evening. **Nino** explains carefully that **Adele** told Lila **Elena** was in Naples, and that Lila found him through his sister and his colleague's phone. Elena immediately suspects renewed contact between them. Nino swears he has not seen or heard Lina since 1963, but admits she told him that **Gennaro** was not his child. Elena resents spending her stolen time with Lila, yet recognizes that she has been avoiding Lila's many calls and agrees to the meeting at Piazza Amedeo.

Over dinner Nino talks about the future. He wants Elena to ask **Pietro** for a separation after France, says he has contacted a lawyer, and insists he will withstand **Eleonora** and her family. He praises the Airotas as civilized people, unlike his own and Elena's parents. That mention sends Elena back to Ischia: Nino with Lila, Elena with **Donato**. She thinks some secrets remain *impronunciabili* — unsayable — even between lovers.

They discuss practicalities. Nino's work ties him to Naples; **Dede** and **Elsa** tie Elena to Florence. He urges her to move to Naples with the girls and arrange turns with Pietro. The more impossible the plan seems, the more desire makes it feel necessary. On the lungomare, Elena imagines Naples, the neighborhood, and Lila all far away. Only she and Nino feel close.
