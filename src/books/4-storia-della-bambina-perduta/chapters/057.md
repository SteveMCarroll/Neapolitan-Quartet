## Summary

Elena feels herself getting more tangled in the neighborhood **Lila** has made visible to her, breaking her own rule not to be sucked back into the place she was born. After visiting her mother, she goes to Lila's office. **Ada** greets her; Lila is busy with a client, **Enzo** is away with **Rino**, and Ada chatters about her daughter. Then Ada calls **Alfonso**, who takes Elena into a small bare room where **Michele Solara** is waiting.

The encounter is painfully awkward. Michele is grayer and unusually polite, without his old mocking fluency. Alfonso, more feminine than ever, is also nervous. Elena suspects the relation between them but does not say so directly. Instead she pushes conversation toward separations: Michele and **Gigliola**, herself and **Pietro**, Alfonso and **Marisa**. Alfonso talks about money and domestic entanglements, saying Marisa is taking **Stefano** from Ada just as Ada took him from Lila.

Elena feels her old solidarity with Alfonso return, precisely because he never fit the neighborhood's masculine pattern. Michele irritates her, especially when he reduces her brothers' work for **Marcello** to a matter of whose money their mother accepts. Lila finally appears and takes Elena away before Michele can speak to her. In her room, glowing with late pregnancy, Lila warns Elena to stay out of Solaras' affairs unless she is ready to take them seriously. About reassuring Elena's mother, she advises lies: lies are better than tranquilizers.
