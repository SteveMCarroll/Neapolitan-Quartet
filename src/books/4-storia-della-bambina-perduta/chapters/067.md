## Summary

Elena reconstructs **Lila**'s delivery from two sources: Lila herself and their gynecologist. It is raining; Elena has recently given birth, **Immacolata** is still in the clinic, **Dede** has a fever, and **Elsa** refuses school. Elena calls Lila for help, cautiously offering excuses for her to refuse. Lila jokes that a boss assigns work and takes the time she wants, then sets out.

But Lila is delayed by a bodily premonition. Though she has no contractions, she feels unwell and has **Enzo** drive her. She reaches Elena's building, feels the first pains, summons **Carmen** to help the neighbor with the girls, and is taken to the clinic. The labor lasts sixteen hours. Lila later describes it with grim irony: the body keeps a child for nine months and then violently expels its guest. She swears she will never be pregnant again.

The gynecologist, invited to dinner by **Nino**, tells a harsher version. In her view Lila's "crowded head" made a normal delivery difficult: Lila resisted pushing, begged to be cut open, and hurled insults. Elena objects that the doctor should not be telling them this. The doctor replies that Lila's birth was a *lotta contro natura* ("struggle against nature"), a fight between mother and child.

The baby is a girl, not the boy everyone predicted. Exhausted but proud, Lila shows her daughter to Elena: she weighs almost four kilos and is named **Nunzia**, after Lila's mother. Later, to satisfy **Fernando** and Enzo's relatives, Lila has her baptized and throws a party at Basic Sight.
