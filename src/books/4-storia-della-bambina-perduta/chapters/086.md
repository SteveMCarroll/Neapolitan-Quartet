## Summary

Elena begins by wondering what kind of errands **Antonio** now does for **Lila**: not paid work, she thinks, but favors done out of friendship or admiration, perhaps even with **Michele**'s permission. Yet she also notices that Michele may no longer grant Lila everything. Lila speaks of him with irritation and worry, and he appears less often at Basic Sight.

The change becomes clear at **Marcello** and **Elisa**'s lavish wedding. Marcello keeps Michele close, murmuring to him and laughing with him, while Michele seems resurrected into his old pompous self. **Gigliola** and the children sit beside him as if all earlier humiliations have been set aside. Elena feels that the old neighborhood vulgarity has modernized into something metropolitan, and that she and her daughters look out of place in their sobriety.

Then Michele explodes. During his speech, **Tina** screams because **Imma** has taken something from her; Michele shouts at Lila to silence that *sfaccimm'e criatura* — roughly, "damned child." Lila only stares and rests a hand on **Enzo**'s hand. Elena removes the girls.

Elisa cheerfully says Michele is back to himself, though he should not treat children that way. After marriage, Elisa becomes polished, calm, and Solara-like, but Elena senses her little sister has vanished behind a false gentility. Elisa still resents Elena's closeness to Lila, and praises Marcello for restoring Michele's old head.
