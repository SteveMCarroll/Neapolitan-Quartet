## Summary

Elena returns often to the rione, especially when **Pietro** is with **Dede** and **Elsa**. Sometimes she only walks through it, but more often she fights with **Immacolata** to make her see a doctor, trying to involve **Vittorio**, **Peppe**, and **Gianni**. Her mother stubbornly refuses, shouting that Elena is the one killing her.

Meanwhile **Lila** has real *stoffa* — substance, talent — as **Michele** had long known. Elena realizes **Elisa**'s hostility comes not only from some quarrel involving **Marcello**, but from Lila's having escaped the Solaras again and risen after using them. Basic Sight gives Lila the prestige of novelty and money. She is no longer only the girl who could organize or scramble people's minds; she now embodies a profitable new skill. **Enzo** may be looking for an office, but Elena sees him as subordinate: Lila makes and unmakes everything. In the rione, it seems, one must learn to be either like the Solaras or like Lila.

Elena starts seeing Lila's imprint everywhere. **Stefano**, heavy and yellowish, seems to use his former wife's phrases. **Ada** praises Lila because she gives Stefano money and even imitates her gestures. Relatives gather around Lila for jobs: Ada is hired, **Rino** inserts himself into the business, and, as **Nino** hears from **Marisa**, even **Alfonso** has joined after Michele abruptly closed his shop.

Elena could learn more by calling Lila, but avoids her. When they meet by chance, Lila is offended and curt. She mocks the sea view from via Tasso as only colour over filth — *monnezza, lota, pisciazza* (trash, mud, piss) — and suggests Elena take a house in the rione.
