## Summary

Elena cuts **Lila** out of everything that follows. What wounds her is not simply learning that **Nino** has lied for more than two years about his marriage; it is that Lila has proved her right from the beginning, as if Elena's choice really was stupid.

Hours later Elena meets Nino and pretends nothing has happened, only avoiding his embraces. The next day she even goes with him to see an apartment in Via Tasso while he says not to worry about rent because a new appointment will solve their money problems. That evening, in the Via Duomo apartment, she finally explodes: she wants to meet **Eleonora** and ask what she knows, when Nino left home, whether they have legal separation, and whether Eleonora's parents know the marriage is over. Nino tells her to ask him instead. Elena calls him a liar.

She begins shouting in dialect and beating his chest with her fists. A violent other self seems to detach from her, a furious *ombra* ("shadow") made of her mother and all their female ancestors, wanting to spit, scratch, blind, even kill. Nino first parries playfully, then collapses into explanation. After Montpellier, he says, Eleonora became unstable: threatening herself with a knife, a balcony, and disappearance with **Albertino**. To prevent tragedy he told her he had left Elena; now she pretends to believe him, while knowing everything.

Nino cries and begs not for justification but understanding. Elena says he lied to both women for himself, out of cowardice. When he suggests that Eleonora's acquiescence means he and Elena can now live together, Elena calmly tells him it is over and returns to Genoa.
