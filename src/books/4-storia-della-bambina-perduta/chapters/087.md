## Summary

Michele's return to his old fluency is marked not only by his renewed *parlantina fiorita* — flowery talk — but by the absence of **Alfonso** from the wedding guests. Alfonso, who had evidently been close to Michele during his crisis, is deeply hurt. For days he asks aloud what wrong he could have done the Solaras after working for them for so long.

Soon afterward, Alfonso comes to dinner at Elena's apartment with **Lila** and **Enzo**. He is depressed and arrives dressed in women's clothes, something he has almost never done in Elena's presence. **Dede** and **Elsa** are stunned. Alfonso drinks too much, becomes intrusive, and repeatedly asks Lila whether he is getting fat, growing ugly, no longer resembling her. He asks Enzo who is more beautiful, himself or Lila, then makes crude jokes about his *culetto* — "little bottom" — until Lila and Enzo hurry him away.

The next day Alfonso leaves Basic Sight, in men's clothes and without makeup, saying he is going to the Solara bar for coffee. At the entrance he meets Michele. No one learns what they say, but Michele soon attacks him with punches, kicks, and then the metal pole used for the shutter. Alfonso returns badly battered, insisting only that it is his own fault, that he failed to *regolarsi* — regulate himself, behave properly. Enzo, enraged by violence against the weak, wants to confront Michele, while Lila tries to calm him because he is frightening Tina.
