## Summary

Elena denies that **Nino** has any say over the baby's name; at this stage even his name irritates her because he has disappeared again into work. That evening, however, he appears while she is having dinner with **Dede** and **Elsa**. He is cheerful, ignores her bitterness, eats with them, and puts the girls to bed with jokes and stories. His easy superficiality makes Elena angrier. She imagines that he is afraid to be present when labor begins and therefore forced to tell **Eleonora** that he must stay with Elena while she gives birth to his child.

When the girls are asleep, Nino kneels before Elena and kisses her belly. Suddenly she thinks of **Mirko**, **Silvia**'s son, now perhaps twelve. Without preamble she asks what Nino knows about that child: has he recognized him, has he ever looked after him? Nino is displeased and says Elena sometimes becomes another person. When she pushes him, he blames Lila, saying Elena listens to her too much and that Lila ruins her head and feelings.

Elena loses her temper and tells him she wants to sleep alone. He leaves quietly, posing as the injured party. Two hours later Elena feels small contractions, like menstrual pain. She calls **Pietro** and tells him to come the next day for Dede and Elsa. Then her waters break. She takes her packed bag, rings the neighbor Antonella until she opens, and leaves the girls with her. At once, Elena's anger and anxiety vanish: the moment has arrived.
