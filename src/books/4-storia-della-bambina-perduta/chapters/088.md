## Summary

By January, Elena's new book has absorbed many small echoes of the neighborhood, and this frightens her as the final proofs approach. She asks **Lila** to reread it, but Lila refuses: she says she has not even read Elena's previous book and lacks the competence. Elena considers asking **Nino**, then remembers that he has ignored both her and **Imma** for months. The manuscript leaves her hands, and she dreads seeing it again in its final, irreversible form.

The publisher's press office says *Panorama* is interested and will send a photographer. Elena wants warning, time to tidy herself and her bleak apartment. Instead, one morning while **Dede** and **Elsa** are at school and she is on the floor playing castle with Imma and **Tina**, a glamorous Milanese photographer in a blue raincoat appears without notice.

The woman loves the setting precisely because it is ugly. She circles Elena, photographs her in every room, and refuses to let her neaten herself. Tina imitates Elena's reading pose, enchanting the photographer. When the woman asks for a photo with Elena's daughter, she chooses Tina, "the other one," because of her striking face. Imma grows sad, and Elena calls her back to "mamma." They go outside for more shots among the battered benches and peeling walls, while Elena anxiously keeps the two little girls from the traffic.

That evening Tina excitedly tells Lila about the beautiful lady and the photographs. Lila tests her story, then turns to Imma to learn what really happened.
