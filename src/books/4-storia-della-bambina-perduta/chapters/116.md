## Summary

With **Gennaro** back, the apartment below becomes an inferno. Elena sometimes runs downstairs fearing he and **Lila** will kill each other, but Lila coldly tells her to mind her own house. Lila now calls **Dede** and **Elsa** the *signorine* — "young ladies" — with sarcasm, while the girls' awe of her fades. Dede decides Lila is mad, says she lost **Tina** on purpose, and compares her lovelessness to Elsa's. Elena tries to explain that pain does not always produce tears, but Dede insists that without tears no one can be sure the pain exists.

The sisters' quarrels focus on Gennaro. Dede defends him as sensitive and oppressed; Elsa calls him filthy and stupid, and says Lila and **Enzo** do right to beat him when he tries to leave. Because both girls read constantly, they fight in bookish Italian until they switch to brutal dialect insults. Elena tries to dampen the conflict, but Dede's malice shifts from her mother toward Elsa and Lila.

Then, while Elena is in Milan, Lila drags Dede, Elsa, and **Imma** inside from the courtyard, shouting that outside is dangerous. Dede answers in dialect that Lila is a shitty mother who lost Tina and never cried. Lila slaps her, then slaps Elsa when she intervenes. On Elena's return, she explains the danger as Chernobyl radiation and says Lila was protecting them, but the girls remember only being hit and fed frozen food. Later Lila confronts Elena; Elena clumsily mentions Tina in the past tense and the difficult birth. Lila snaps: mind your own business.
