## Summary

Only **Nino** and **Lila** never visit **Immacolata** at the clinic. Nino says openly that he has no desire to meet "that Camorrist," meaning **Marcello**. Elena sometimes suspects this is an excuse for his absences, but more often she thinks he is genuinely bitter: he worked hard to help her mother, only for Elena's family to accept the Solaras' solution. When she tries to explain the tangled mechanism of family need and her mother's happiness, he mutters that Naples will never change.

Lila says nothing about the move to the clinic, but she keeps helping Elena, though she might give birth at any moment. Elena urges her to take care of herself; Lila jokes that the baby is late because neither of them feels like it. When **Dede** or **Elsa** has a fever and Elena cannot leave the girls at school, Lila drops work with **Enzo** and **Alfonso** and comes to via Tasso to watch all three children.

Elena values Lila's skill with the girls: she draws Dede and Elsa toward **Imma**, steadies Elsa, and soothes the baby without a pacifier. Yet Elena is never fully calm. On the way back from Capodimonte, she imagines Nino finding time to visit Lila in her absence, or Lila going into labor while he is there. Something like that happens, but without Nino: Elena returns to find Lila gone. Labor has begun, and Elena prays that Lila can withstand the body's frightening *scrollarsi* ("shaking itself apart").
