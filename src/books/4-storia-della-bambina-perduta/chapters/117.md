## Summary

Things worsen again in June 1986, when another disappearance occurs. **Nunzia** arrives, darker than usual, and tells Elena that **Rino** did not come home the night before and that **Pinuccia** is searching the whole rione. Nunzia does not look Elena in the face; Elena understands that the message is really for **Lila**.

Elena goes downstairs to tell her. Lila immediately calls **Gennaro**, assuming he knows where his uncle is. At first the boy resists, afraid that any information will make his mother harder on him. But after a full day passes and Rino still has not been found, he agrees to help. The next morning he refuses to search with Lila and **Enzo**, but accepts the company of his father, **Stefano**. Stefano arrives breathless and frightened, touching his throat and saying he cannot breathe.

Father and son cross the railway yard and walk along old tracks lined with abandoned carriages. In one of them they find Rino dead, sitting with his eyes open, his beard growing up his face like an invasive plant. Stefano, forgetting his weak heart, explodes with rage. He screams that Rino was always a *strunz* — a shithead — and deserved such a death because he ruined Pinuccia, his children, and Gennaro. He tells Gennaro to look at what awaits him. Gennaro restrains his father, then loses his temper and says he is disgusted to be Stefano's son; the only people in the rione he respects are his mother and Enzo. Stefano begins to cry. They sit beside the body only long enough to calm down, then return with the news.
