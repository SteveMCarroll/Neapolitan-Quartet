## Summary

For the half hour **Elena** spends in the Solara shop, past and present collide: **Lila**'s shoe designs, her wedding photograph, old inaugurations and secret meetings, and now everyone's changed adult lives. Elena acts cheerful. She greets **Gennaro**, now a heavy twelve-year-old who resembles adolescent **Stefano** so strongly that Lila seems absent from him.

**Marisa**, with **Alfonso** beside her, hints that Elena will now come often to Naples. Elena notices Marisa's resemblance to **Donato**, and Alfonso's disturbing elegance: his long hair, grace, and softening gestures make him seem to be feminizing himself. Marisa jokes crudely about Elena's books and pornography, while Alfonso politely invites Elena and **Nino** to dinner.

No one criticizes Elena's affair. **Enzo** embraces her silently; **Carmen** pulls her aside to speak urgently about **Pasquale**, insisting he is brave and good, not merely a hunted terrorist. She gives Elena her contact details, convinced Elena knows important people who can help him. **Antonio** adds the real danger: the Solaras believe Pasquale killed **Manuela**, and if they find him, Antonio cannot protect him.

Alone outside, Antonio reminds Elena that she once lied about wanting Nino and hurt him badly. Then he warns her: if Nino hurts her, she should say so. Lila, he says, knows Nino and says Elena must not trust him. Elena sees Lila watching everyone, still governing the scene. As Elena leaves, she finally understands what unsettled her: Alfonso now resembles Lila.
