## Summary

After Pietro's brutal phone call, **Elena** cries, calms herself, and goes to **Nino** for comfort. Outside his room she hears him speaking on the telephone and immediately imagines **Eleonora** on the other end. Is he calling his wife every night after Elena leaves? Are they arranging a peaceful separation, or quietly reconciling?

Elena knocks again and again before he opens. She attacks him for hiding her from his wife while she has risked everything with **Pietro**, **Dede**, and **Elsa**. The night becomes a harsh quarrel. Nino soothes, kisses, calls her mad, and swears on his son that he has not spoken to Eleonora since leaving Naples. He claims he was calling a colleague in the hotel, even at midnight. Elena calls him *bugiardo* — a liar — and resists making love, then gives in because the alternative is to imagine the affair already over.

Morning brings bad temper and the end of the conference. When **Colombe** and **Augustin** renew their invitation to Paris, Elena hopes Nino will seize the chance to prolong their suspended life. He refuses, speaking of tickets, work, and money. Hurt, Elena asserts her own literary importance: she has business with the publisher in Nanterre. Nino then argues that they must return and face their spouses, yet Elena still insists on the detour. They leave for Paris because she wants *qualche giorno ancora* — a few days more.
