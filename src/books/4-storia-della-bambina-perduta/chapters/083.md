## Summary

One evening, when **Lila** speaks badly of **Gennaro**, **Dede** blushes and defends him: he is very intelligent. Lila smiles, and from then on Dede feels authorized to praise him whenever possible. Gennaro, now eighteen, handsome but stocky and surly, barely notices twelve-year-old Dede, yet she adores him, watches for him at the window, and uses **Imma** as an excuse to go downstairs to Lila's.

Elena and Lila have never lived so close: Elena's floor is Lila's ceiling. The arrangement soon becomes useful and intimate. Dede and **Elsa** help with Imma; Lila cares for all three girls when Elena travels; **Tina** often stays upstairs. Elena has time to read, revise her book, and live without **Nino**. Relations with **Pietro** improve too. He visits more, sleeps over, and talks amiably with Lila.

Lila is fascinated by Pietro's learning and says he puts writing into his voice without sounding like a printed book. She often speaks of Gennaro with regret, blaming herself for not having given him enough. Then she turns to Tina, afraid the same waste could happen again. She asks Elena to help make Tina like Elena's daughters; school alone, as **Maestra Oliviero** showed, is not enough. Elena blames the rione, but Lila replies: since Elena has chosen to stay, they should change it.
