Roberto is **Carmen**'s husband and appears briefly when **Tina** disappears. He joins Carmen, **Antonio**, and many others in the frantic neighborhood search along the stradone and through the rione. His small presence helps show how quickly the whole local network, not only **Lila** and **Enzo**'s closest friends, is drawn into the false sightings, rumors, and exhaustion of that day.
