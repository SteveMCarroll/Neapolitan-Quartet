Daughter of **Professoressa Galiani** and sister of **Armando**, Nadia is mostly offstage in book 4 but remains charged with the dangerous glamour of the old militant years. Early news places her abroad or underground with **Pasquale**, part of the political violence that Elena encounters only through rumors, police checks, and worried friends.

In the later chapters, Nadia's arrest changes everything. Her decision to cooperate with the authorities, unlike Pasquale's silence, protects her while deepening the charges against him and eventually against **Enzo**. Elena comes to see Nadia as one of **Nino**'s useful relations and as a woman whose old revolutionary severity has narrowed into denunciation, self-preservation, and a bitter hostility toward **Lila**.
