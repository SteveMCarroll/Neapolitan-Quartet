**Ada** remains with **Stefano** in the diminished Carracci household, mother of little **Maria** and practical replacement for **Lila** in everything but law. By book 4 she is thin, strained, and loyal to a man whose money and authority have almost vanished. **Carmen** remembers how Ada tormented her after taking Lila's place in the grocery, but Elena also sees Ada's exhaustion more clearly now.

Like many rione relatives, Ada is pulled into Lila's orbit. She praises Lila for giving Stefano money, imitates her gestures, and is hired at Basic Sight when Lila's business becomes the new source of jobs and prestige. Her story stays minor, but it shows how old domestic victories can become another form of entrapment.
