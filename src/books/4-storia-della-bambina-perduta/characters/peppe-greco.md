Peppe, usually mentioned with **Gianni**, is **Elena**'s younger brother and one of the Greco children most exposed to the rione's economy of favors. By book 4 he and Gianni have become confident enough in **Marcello Solara**'s protection to tip clinic nurses, invoke the Solara name, and answer Elena's worries with resentment.

Elena fears they are caught up not just in Solara work but in the neighborhood's drug traffic. Peppe pushes back: without his money, he says, their mother would already be dead. **Immacolata** nevertheless sees the danger clearly and, near death, compels both brothers to take jobs found by **Lila**. Peppe's arc stays mostly at the edge of Elena's vision, where guilt, family obligation, and dirty money blur together.
