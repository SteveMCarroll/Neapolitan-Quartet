**Mariarosa** is the Airota who remains most usable to **Elena** after the family rupture. She organizes a Milan bookshop event where Elena faces rival groups of women and discovers a new public authority, then takes Elena, Dede, and Elsa in after the disastrous break with Adele. Her apartment is still a crossroads for damaged, politically serious people: Franco, Silvia, and Mirko are there, and the old revolutionary certainties sound increasingly exhausted.

She has limits, especially around her mother, but she is a real shelter. By the final decades she has left the university in disgust, lives on family wealth, and judges Guido's compromised innocence with bitter clarity: small reforms, she says, have left him trapped inside a system of lies.
