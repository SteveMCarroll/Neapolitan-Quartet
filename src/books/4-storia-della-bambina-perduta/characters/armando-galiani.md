Son of **Professoressa Galiani** and brother of **Nadia**, Armando carries into book 4 the afterlife of the far-left circles Elena knew in the 1970s. Early on he is still a doctor and Democrazia proletaria figure, useful to **Elena** mainly as a guarded source on Nadia and **Pasquale**; he praises Elena's work, but gives little away.

After **Tina** disappears, Armando reappears changed into an aggressive private-TV journalist. He pursues a theory about post-earthquake building sites and a scrapped truck, trying to turn Lila's loss into a public case. **Lila** rejects him brutally as a failed doctor, revolutionary, and journalist. His story briefly reaches national attention, then collapses, leaving him as another figure whose old political seriousness has become opportunistic noise.
