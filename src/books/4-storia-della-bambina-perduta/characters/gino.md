**Gino**, the pharmacist's son and Elena's first boyfriend, is only a minor book-4 presence but carries the residue of the rione's political ugliness. Elena's return to the neighborhood includes even the old revulsion of Gino's pharmacy, and when **Lila** is bleeding and in pain Elena runs toward the pharmacy hoping Gino's father can advise her.

By the later legal-political aftermath, Gino belongs to the book's list of unresolved dead. Nadia Galiani's confessions, reported through **Nino**, attribute his murder — along with those of **Bruno Soccavo**, **Manuela Solara**, and the Solara brothers — to **Pasquale**. For Elena, he remains one of the small local faces through which private memory meets Italy's violent years.
