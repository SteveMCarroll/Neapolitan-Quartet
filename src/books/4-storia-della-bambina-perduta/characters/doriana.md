Doriana appears when **Pietro** is hospitalized after being beaten by students. She is pretty, nearly finished with her degree, writing on Catullus, and already intimate enough to sit on Pietro's bed and hold his hand. Pietro praises her with a devotion that tells **Elena** the relationship is real.

Her presence shifts the family balance. **Adele** already knows about her, and **Dede** and **Elsa** are quickly charmed by her jewelry and playfulness. For Elena, Doriana is not an enemy so much as proof that Pietro has begun a life outside their ruined marriage, clearing the way for Elena to move the girls to Naples while also making the children's future more complicated.
