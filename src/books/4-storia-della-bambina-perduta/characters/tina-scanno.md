**Tina** is **Lila** and **Enzo**'s daughter, born Nunzia and quickly called by the name of Elena's lost childhood doll. She grows up close to **Imma**, admired for the quickness and charm that make Elena worry her own child is fading beside her. For Lila, Tina becomes a second chance: the child whose intelligence must not be squandered as **Gennaro**'s was.

Her disappearance on a holiday street gives Book 4 its wound and title. Neighbors search, rumors harden around a truck and a blue ball, but no trace is found. Afterward Tina is present as absence — in photos, recordings, theories, guilt, and Lila's fear that the wrong child may have been taken.
