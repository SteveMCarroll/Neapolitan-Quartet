**Fernando** is mostly a distant old presence in book 4, evoked through **Lila**'s tenderness toward her parents and through the Cerullo family's continuing poverty. When Lila's daughter is born, the baby is named Nunzia, but Fernando's expectations still matter enough that Lila has the child baptized and holds a party at Basic Sight to satisfy him and **Enzo**'s relatives.

Rino's death briefly brings Fernando into focus: he and **Nunzia** seem the only people truly wounded by their son's end. Later, in Elena and Lila's phone-call years, Fernando dies before Nunzia. His passing helps mark the disappearance of the old shoemaker household, even as Lila returns to the apartment where he once ruled her childhood.
