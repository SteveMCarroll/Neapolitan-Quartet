Colombe meets **Elena** and **Nino** at the Montpellier conference and invites them, with **Augustin**, to Paris. Her claim that children are *elastici* — resilient — briefly comforts Elena, who wants to believe **Dede** and **Elsa** will survive her choices.

But Colombe also unsettles her. Her irritation toward her own son feels cold, and Nino's easy intimacy with her triggers Elena's jealousy before they even reach Paris. Colombe is a small figure, yet she concentrates one of Elena's early book-4 illusions: that leaving wifehood and motherhood might be elegant, modern, and free of lasting injury.
