**Dede**, **Elena** and **Pietro**'s elder daughter, carries the first shock of her mother's break with Pietro. In Book 4 she remains loyal to her father and to the cultivated world he represents, even as Elena brings her and **Elsa** back to the rione and into Lila's orbit.

Naples unsettles and attracts her. She does well in school, helps with **Imma**, and falls painfully in love with **Gennaro**, Lila's handsome, surly son. That attachment becomes another way the daughters inherit their mothers' entanglements. Later Dede moves outward again — toward Pietro, study, and America — but not untouched: the rione has entered her family story, and Elena's choices have marked her adolescence.
