**Albertino** is still a small child in book 4, but he matters as evidence. He is the first son of **Nino** and Eleonora, the household that Nino keeps insisting is over while never truly leaving it. When Elena sees Nino with Eleonora and their children, Albertino is part of the official family tableau she tries to pierce by announcing her own pregnancy.

Like Mirko, Albertino exposes Nino's idea of fatherhood as intermittent and self-serving. He is not developed as an independent character; he is the living cost of Nino's divided life, and one of the children Elena must half-acknowledge while pretending she is Nino's real wife.
