**Carmen** remains a crucial bridge between **Elena** and the rione. She watches over Elena's children, appears in clinic corridors during **Immacolata**'s illness, and treats **Lila** almost like an oracle. Yet her deepest loyalty is still to her brother **Pasquale**, whom she insists is brave and good, not merely a hunted terrorist.

That loyalty makes her vulnerable. Under Solara pressure she files a complaint against Elena's novel, believing **Marcello** can have Pasquale killed whenever he chooses. When Elena finds her in Giugliano, Carmen confesses she was forced, then angrily notes that Elena may even gain publicity from the case. After Pasquale's arrest she is calmer but terrified that prison will destroy him as it destroyed their father.
