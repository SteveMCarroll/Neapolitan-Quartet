**Franco** returns in book 4 as a damaged but lucid remnant of Elena's earlier intellectual life. The Montpellier trip with **Nino** makes Elena remember Paris with Franco as a smaller crossing of borders, but when she meets Franco again in Milan he is no nostalgic lover. He is physically marked by political violence, skeptical of slogans, and able to see Elena's gifts without flattering her romance.

At Mariarosa's apartment, Franco praises Elena's reasoning power and tells her not to lose it to the obligations traditionally loaded onto women. His advice helps Elena move to Naples without surrendering the idea of work, though she often betrays that resolve. Late in the novel, Mariarosa invokes him as someone who understood early that half-measures would fail.
