**Elena Greco**, Lenù, enters Book 4 by choosing **Nino** over **Pietro**, a choice that fractures her marriage, wounds **Dede** and **Elsa**, and provokes **Lila**'s blunt accusation that she is a fool. The romance that seems to promise freedom soon becomes another trap: Nino stays entangled with his wife, political ambitions, and other women, while Elena bears **Imma**, writes, travels, and keeps trying to make a coherent life out of passion, motherhood, and work.

Back in Naples, Elena moves into the rione as an *esperimento di ricomposizione* — an experiment in putting herself back together. The experiment binds her again to Lila: pregnancies, the earthquake, the children, the Solaras, and finally Tina's disappearance. By old age Elena is a known writer in Turin, still measuring her books against Lila's silence. Her final account of their friendship is also a wager that writing can preserve what Lila wants erased.
