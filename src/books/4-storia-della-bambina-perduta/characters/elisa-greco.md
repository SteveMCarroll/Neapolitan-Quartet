**Elisa**, once the gentle youngest Greco sibling, appears changed when **Elena** returns to Naples. Pregnant and living in a large apartment with **Marcello Solara**, she has become harder in body, tone, and loyalties. She defends their mother **Immacolata** against Elena's shameful choices and repeats Solara warnings that **Lila** should stay in her place.

Her new respectability is inseparable from compromise. Immacolata forces Marcello to promise marriage before she dies, but Elisa's alliance with him also ties her to the neighborhood's old power. By the end Elena learns from Lila, not her family, that Elisa has left for Caserta and remarried a municipal councillor — another escape from Naples, but not an innocent one.
