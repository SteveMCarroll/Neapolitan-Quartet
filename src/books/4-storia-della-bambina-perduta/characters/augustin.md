Augustin is part of the French couple **Elena** and **Nino** meet at the Montpellier conference. With **Colombe**, he represents the seemingly freer European world Elena imagines she has entered: travel, languages, lovers who have left spouses and children behind. He remains a minor figure, useful mainly as part of the brief Paris detour before Elena and Nino must return to the damage waiting in Italy.
