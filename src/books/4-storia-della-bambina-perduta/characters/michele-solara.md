**Michele** remains the more frightening Solara brother: colder, more intelligent, and more unstable than **Marcello**. By book 4, **Lila** says he has gone mad after **Manuela**'s death. He has thrown out **Gigliola**, remains obsessively tied to Lila, and chases what she calls *l'ombra della mia ombra* — "the shadow of my shadow" — through **Alfonso**, whose feminized resemblance to Lila makes him both desired and endangered.

His violence becomes explicit at Alfonso's funeral, where Lila confronts him and he punches her in the face before Marcello drags him away. Later he mocks **Elena** on a Sunday walk and keeps her mother's bracelet after it breaks in his hand. Like Marcello, he is killed outside the church in 1986, already reduced in the rione's memory to one more murdered Solara.
