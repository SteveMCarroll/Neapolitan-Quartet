**Nunzia** remains the anxious, submissive mother of **Lila**, but book 4 gives her a sharper practical presence. Her name becomes newly tender when Lila, after a brutal labor, gives birth to a daughter and names her Nunzia after her mother. Later the elder Nunzia comes upstairs to help **Elena** with the girls: efficient, uncomfortable, and so poor that she must ask to be paid.

After Tina's disappearance she is one of the few adults still absorbed in the Cerullo family's ordinary catastrophes. She and **Fernando** are the only ones truly wounded by **Rino**'s death. In the later phone-call years, Fernando dies and then Nunzia, closing the old parents' claim on Lila's childhood apartment.
