**Alfonso** begins book 4 already transformed: elegant, soft in gesture, married to **Marisa** as a cover, and visibly shaping himself after **Lila**. After **Michele** closes the Solara shop, Alfonso lands at Basic Sight, where Lila gives him work and a place near her. To **Elena**, he becomes the living sign of Michele's obsession, the "shadow" of Lila that Michele keeps pursuing.

The arrangement cannot hold. Alfonso grows unreliable, painted up, speaking of himself in the feminine and returning with signs of beatings. He disappears and is found at Coroglio, beaten to death and thrown into the sea. His sparse funeral, attended by the Solaras but barely by his own family, exposes both his loneliness and the depth of Lila's grief.
