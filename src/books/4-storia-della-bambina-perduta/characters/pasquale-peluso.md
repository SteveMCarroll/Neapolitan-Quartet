**Pasquale** spends much of book 4 offstage but never loses force. The Solaras suspect him in **Manuela Solara**'s murder, **Carmen** lives in fear for him, and Elena's published work repeatedly brushes against rumors about his clandestine life. After Nadia Galiani is arrested, the question of whether Pasquale should surrender splits Elena, Carmen, and **Lila**: law may protect him, but the state itself looks compromised.

He is finally arrested near Serino without resisting. Elena commits herself to helping him so he will not feel abandoned, while Nadia's accusations attach him to murders from **Gino** and **Bruno Soccavo** to the Solaras. In the epilogue he is still in prison, educated and wry, holding to the generous ideas of his youth.
