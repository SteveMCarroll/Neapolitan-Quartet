Silvana is hired through **Nino**'s mother to help **Elena** with the house and children after **Imma** is born. To Nino, paying her seems to settle the practical problem; to Elena, it becomes part of a larger pattern in which women support his freedom while he preserves his own time.

Silvana matters because Elena comes home unexpectedly for diapers and finds Nino having sex with her in the bathroom. The discovery is devastating not because Silvana herself is important, but because the mirror gives Elena a final, sordid image of Nino. In that moment the charming boy, public intellectual, lover, and father collapse into one man, no longer separable from the betrayals Elena had tried not to see.
