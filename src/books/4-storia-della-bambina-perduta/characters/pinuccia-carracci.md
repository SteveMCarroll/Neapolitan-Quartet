**Pinuccia**, daughter of **Don Achille** and sister of **Stefano** and **Alfonso**, is a small but sharp presence in book 4. When **Lila** throws **Rino Cerullo** out of Basic Sight after discovering **Gennaro**'s drug use, Pinuccia comes with **Nunzia** to beg for Rino's job back, only to be shouted away.

She is also named among the family members absent from Alfonso's funeral, a silence that deepens the bleakness around his death. Later, during one of Lila's bodily and mental crises, Lila irrationally fears that Pinuccia has poisoned her. The mentions are brief, but they keep the Carracci family visible as a damaged remnant of old rione power.
