**Melina** remains one of the rione's old wounds: the Cappuccio widow whose attachment to Donato Sarratore broke her life decades earlier. In book 4 she is mostly glimpsed through **Antonio** and **Ada**. Antonio returns from Germany and settles near her, then later decides to take her away with him because Ada already has too many burdens.

Her name returns with terrible force when Tina disappears. **Lila**, frantic on the stradone, moves "like Melina," running back and forth without logic. By the end of Antonio's book-4 arc, Melina has become both a mother to be protected and a warning image of mental collapse that Antonio fears may also be inside him.
