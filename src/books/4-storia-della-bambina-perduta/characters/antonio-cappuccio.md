**Antonio** returns from Germany for **Manuela Solara**'s funeral, nearly unrecognizable, with a German wife and three children. He settles again near **Melina**, **Ada**, and the old rione circle, still under **Michele Solara**'s command. In the Solara shoe shop he warns **Elena** that the brothers believe **Pasquale** killed Manuela and that even Antonio cannot protect him if they find him.

Book 4 leaves him caught between obedience and exhaustion. He helps the search for Tina, postponing his departure because Michele orders it, and continues giving **Lila** and **Enzo** mysterious reports. But by chapter 119 he leaves at last with his family and elderly Melina, taking his mother to Germany because he fears seeing in her what his own nervous collapse may become.
