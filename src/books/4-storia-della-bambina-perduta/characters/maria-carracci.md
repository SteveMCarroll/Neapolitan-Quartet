**Maria Carracci** is **Stefano** and **Ada**'s daughter, born from the household that replaced **Lila** in the old Carracci apartment. She is a minor child in book 4, important less for action than for what her name records: Stefano's second family and the continuation of the Carracci line after the collapse of its money and businesses.

Her clearest book-4 appearance is an absence. At **Alfonso**'s funeral, Elena notes that Maria, like Stefano, **Pinuccia**, their mother, and Marisa's children, is not there. The omission makes the Carracci family feel fragmented even in mourning.
