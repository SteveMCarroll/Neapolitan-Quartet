**Imma**, formally Immacolata Sarratore, is born in January 1981, the child of **Elena** and **Nino**'s affair and named for Elena's dying mother. From infancy she belongs to a tangled household: half-sister to **Dede** and **Elsa**, watched by **Lila**, and often overshadowed by brilliant **Tina**.

Her Book 4 arc is marked by hunger for fathers and substitute mothers. Pietro and **Enzo** sometimes include her kindly, while Nino gives her public proof of paternity more often than presence. After Tina's disappearance and Nino's disgrace, Imma draws close to *zia Lina*, who guides her through Naples and gives her a lasting curiosity about the city.
