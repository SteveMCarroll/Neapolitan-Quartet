**Bruno Soccavo** is no longer active in book 4, but his factory remains part of the friends' memory of bodily and political damage. During the earthquake, **Lila** remembers the Soccavo *salumificio* as one of the moments when the world's margins dissolved around her. **Elena** also resists Lila's reminder of the old article she wrote about the factory, insisting it was about labor conditions, not Bruno personally.

By the late investigations Bruno is dead. Pinuccia speaks of him as one of her lost men, and Nadia Galiani's confessions attribute his murder to **Pasquale**. Bruno therefore survives in the guide as a past predator and as one more corpse folded into the confused account of political violence.
