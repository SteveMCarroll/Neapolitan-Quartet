**Lidia Sarratore** is a small but telling presence in book 4's Sarratore household. The summaries use the name for the daughter Eleonora gives birth to while Elena and **Nino** are rebuilding their life on via Tasso, a birth that makes Nino's legal family impossible to dismiss. Soon afterward, at lunch with the Sarratores, Nino's mother Lidia treats Elena like a daughter-in-law, briefly staging the acceptance Elena wants.

The name returns in the epilogue, when Elena goes back to Naples for Lidia Sarratore's funeral and sees Nino aged, loud, and unbearable. Lidia's page is therefore minor but revealing: the Sarratore family continues around Elena's affair, before it, during it, and after it.
