**Silvio** is born in 1976 to **Elisa Greco** and **Marcello Solara**, and is named for Marcello's father, the Solara patriarch. His birth shows how completely Elisa has entered the Solara household: exhausted and frightened by the newborn, she accepts help from **Elena** only briefly before both she and Marcello grow irritated by Elena's presence.

He remains a minor child in the guide, but a useful marker of family absorption. When Elena's article against the Solaras appears, Elisa, Silvio, and Elena's father effectively cut Elena off. Years later Silvio is part of the Sunday Solara procession through church, stalls, and library, the next generation carried along inside the family's public performance.
