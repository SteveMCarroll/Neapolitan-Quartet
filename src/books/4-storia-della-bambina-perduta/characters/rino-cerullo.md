**Rino** is one of book 4's saddest remnants of the old Cerullo dream. He inserts himself into Basic Sight, talks wildly about computers and money, and then whispers abuse against **Lila** before fleeing her. Around the office he becomes part of the grim background Elena watches: drugs, debts, rumors, and family need pressing on Lila's success.

Lila eventually throws him out of Basic Sight, and after Tina's disappearance he declines further. In 1986 he vanishes and is found dead in an abandoned railway carriage. Only **Nunzia** and **Fernando** seem truly wounded. Yet Lila later rewrites him, preserving him as the real genius of the Cerullo shoes and freezing his life before failure, drugs, and damage overwhelmed him.
