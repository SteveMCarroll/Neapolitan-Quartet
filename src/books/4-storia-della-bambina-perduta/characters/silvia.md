**Silvia** remains one of the quartet's quiet moral witnesses. In book 4 Elena finds her again at Mariarosa's apartment with Mirko, the son **Nino** has never really claimed. Silvia does not need to accuse him; her presence, and the boy's face, already say enough.

She belongs to the Milan world of damaged political hope, women's collectives, and people who have paid heavily for public commitments. When Elena later asks Nino what he has done for Mirko, Silvia is the unstated measure of his failure. Her dignity makes Nino's evasions look smaller, and reminds Elena that abandoned women and children are not abstractions in someone else's story.
