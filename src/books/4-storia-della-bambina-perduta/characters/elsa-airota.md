**Elsa**, younger daughter of **Elena** and **Pietro**, adapts to Naples faster than **Dede**. The dark apartment and noisy courtyard first repel the girls, but Elsa absorbs the rione's speech and energy, helps with **Imma** and **Tina**, and grows less bound to the refined world Elena imagined for her.

Her adolescence turns disruptive when she leaves school and runs off with **Gennaro**, the boy Dede loves. Elsa treats feeling as something that cannot be commanded, then moves through further attractions with a sharp independence that hurts others. She eventually passes her exams, reconciles with Dede and Pietro, and goes to the United States, carrying both rebellion and affection for Lila with her.
