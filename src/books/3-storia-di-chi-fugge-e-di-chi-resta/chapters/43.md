## Summary

Lila jolts awake at dawn to a tidy queue of bad thoughts: the sick
heart, Gennaro's regressions, the neighborhood fascists, Nadia's
condescension, Pasquale's unreliability, the list of demands. Only
afterward does she remember that she slept in Enzo's bed — and that he
is no longer there. She gets up just in time to hear the front door
close. Did he get up the moment she fell asleep? Stay awake all night?
Sleep in the other room with the boy? Or fall asleep with her,
forgetting every want? Either way he made his own breakfast in silence,
laid the table for her and Gennaro, and left for work without a word.

At the factory Edo asks, a little sullen: *allora ti sei decisa?* — so
have you made up your mind? She bites back into her old tone: I make up
my mind when I please. He: we're a committee, you have to tell us.
*Avete fatto girare la lista?* Did you pass the list around? Yes. *E
che dicono gli altri?* What do the others say? *Chi tace acconsente* —
silence means consent. No, she says, *chi tace si caca sotto* —
silence means they're shitting themselves.

Then a long unspoken monologue while she cuts meat with vengeance: she
wants to hurt and to be hurt, to slip the knife from dead flesh into
live flesh, scream, take it out on everyone. *Ah, Lina Cerullo, sei
incorreggibile.* Why did you make this list? Because you don't want to
be exploited? Want to improve your condition and theirs? Do you believe
you'll join the *marcia vittoriosa del proletariato di tutto il mondo*,
the victorious march of the proletariat of the whole world? Nonsense.
March toward becoming what — workers forever, just workers in power?
*Aria fritta* — fried air, hot air to gild the pill of fatigue. This
condition isn't to be improved, it's to be erased; you've known that
since you were a child.

Have *you* improved? Have you become Nadia, Isabella? Has your brother
become Armando? Is your son Marco? No. We stay us, they stay them. The
sickness is in the head — *per l'insoddisfazione della testa* — the
head's dissatisfaction is making the body sick. She is tired of
herself, of Gennaro, of everything. Then she pivots: take your
responsibilities, Cerullo, do what you've always meant to do — scare
Soccavo, break him of the habit of *chiavarsi le operaie dentro
l'essiccatoio*, screwing his women workers in the curing room. Show the
*studente con la faccia da lupo* — the student with a wolf's face,
Nino — what you can put together. That summer on Ischia, the
drinks, the house at Forio, the luxurious bed where I lay with Nino:
the money came from here, from this stink, from this filth, this work
paid in pennies. *Cosa ho tagliato, qui?* What did I just cut? — a
yellowish paste squirts out, disgusting. The world turns; thank god,
when it falls, it breaks.

Just before lunch break she decides: I'm going up. Edo: *vado*. She
hasn't even taken her apron off when Bruno's secretary comes for her
— *Il dottor Soccavo ti vuole d'urgenza nel suo ufficio*. Lila assumes
a snitch has tipped him off. She fetches the list of demands from her
locker and goes up. In Bruno's office she finds Bruno — and, slouched
in an armchair with a cigarette, Michele Solara.
