## Summary

Lila is lying dressed on a cot, Gennaro asleep beside her. *Entra* —
come in — she tells Elena, I knew you'd come, give me a kiss. Elena
kisses her cheeks and sits on the empty little bed that must be the
boy's. How long had it been? Lila looks even thinner, even paler;
eyes red, *pinne nasali screpolate* (cracked nostrils), long hands
marked by cuts. She rolls on almost without pause, voice low so as not
to wake the child: I've seen you in the papers, how well you look,
what lovely hair, I know everything about you, I know you're getting
married, he's a professor, good for you, going to live in Florence,
sorry to drag you over at this hour, my head won't help me, *si scolla
come la carta da parati* — it's peeling off like wallpaper — thank God
you're here.

"Cosa succede?" Elena asks gently, reaching for her hand. That
question and that touch are enough. Lila's eyes go wide, she gasps,
yanks her hand away. *«Non sto bene»* (I'm not well), she says, "but
wait, don't be scared, I'll calm down in a minute."

She calms. Then she speaks slowly, almost spelling out the words:

> I dragged you here, Lenù, because I need you to make me a promise. I
> only trust you. If something happens to me — if I end up in hospital,
> if they take me to the asylum, if I disappear — you must take
> Gennaro. You must keep him with you. He must grow up in your house.
> Enzo is good, he's kind, I trust him; but he can't give the boy what
> you can give him.

"Why are you saying this? What's wrong? If you don't explain I can't
understand."

*«Prometti prima»* — promise first.

"All right."

Lila stirs again, frightening Elena. "No, don't tell me *va bene* — you
have to say it here, now, that you'll take the boy. And if you need
money, find Nino — tell him he has to help you. *Però prometti*: the
child I'll raise myself."

Elena looks at her, uncertain, and promises. She promised, and stayed
to listen all night long.
