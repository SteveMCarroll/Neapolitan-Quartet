A young worker at the **Soccavo** sausage factory in San Giovanni a
Teduccio, alongside Lila in the first stretch of book 3. Quick‑tempered
and visibly furious about conditions in the meat rooms, he is the
first male worker at the *salumificio* who takes Lila seriously — and
the first one she tentatively trusts. He helps her draft a list of
grievances about pay, the freezing meat‑boning rooms, the way the
Soccavo foremen handle the women, and the timing of the toilet breaks.
After the **Galiani** siblings' student push and Lila's argument with
Bruno he, too, is sacked; later he is one of the workers around the
Pasquale/Enzo circle who keeps a wary eye on the rione's worsening
politics.
