Gina Medotti runs the press office at the Milan publishing house that
has put out Elena's debut novel. She appears for the first time in
book 3, in chapter 10.

When the *Corriere della Sera*'s thick‑eyeglasses professor publishes
a hatchet job on the novel, hers is the first of three urgent calls
that reach Elena via signora Spagnuolo's downstairs telephone — the
others are from **Adele** and from **Pietro**. Her existence as the
publisher's named press contact is what cements, for Elena, the new
fact that her own face and her own sentences are now circulating well
beyond anything she can control.
