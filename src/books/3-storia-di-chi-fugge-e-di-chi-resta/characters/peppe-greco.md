Elena's other younger brother, paired through book 3 with **Gianni**.
Like his brother, he is increasingly dependent on the **Solara** for
work and pocket money, and is one of the things **Immacolata**, in
her resigned way, holds against Elena: *you went away and left them to
this*. Elena's guilt over both boys deepens through the second half of
the book, but she stops short of doing anything practical about it.
