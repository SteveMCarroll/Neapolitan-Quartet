The widow of the Cappuccio porter and mother of **Antonio** and
**Ada**, undone in book 1 by her affair with **Donato Sarratore** and
prone, since then, to long stretches of mental collapse. In book 3 she
is mostly background — kept in her sister's apartment in the rione,
helped by Ada, sometimes seen at the door of the old Carracci grocery
where Ada has taken Stefano's place — but her presence is still a
small open wound under the surface of every Sarratore conversation
Elena has on her visits home.
