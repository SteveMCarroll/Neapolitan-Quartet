The taciturn former fruit‑seller who, at the end of book 2, took Lila
and **Gennaro** out of Stefano's house and into a tiny flat at San
Giovanni a Teduccio. In book 3 he is the calm centre of Lila's life:
he works as a labourer, raises Gennaro as if the boy were his own, and
in his spare time is teaching himself computing through a
correspondence course from **Zurich** — first electromagnetism and
binary algebra, then **IBM** programming, then a paid certificate
exam that he ends up taking jointly with Lila.

His patience with her is almost unbroken: he tolerates her exhaustion
from the **Soccavo** factory, her insomnia, her shouting matches with
Pasquale and the Galiani siblings, and her refusal to sleep with him.
By the closing chapters he and Lila are both being hired as **IBM**
machine operators at a small Naples firm — a quiet first step toward
the life they will build in book 4. Through everything he remains
loyal, watchful, and undemonstrative, the only person in the book whom
Lila trusts without reserve.
