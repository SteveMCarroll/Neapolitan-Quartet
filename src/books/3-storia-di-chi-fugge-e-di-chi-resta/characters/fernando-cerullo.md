Lila's father, the rione shoemaker of book 1 — the man who once
struck the child Lila so hard he broke her arm. In book 3 he is
mostly a quiet, diminished presence: still at the workbench, still
unable to forgive Lila for the separation from Stefano, still embarrassed
by Rino's failures, ageing fast. Elena sees him at the door of the
old workshop on her visits home; he asks after the girls and Pietro,
and avoids saying Lila's name.
