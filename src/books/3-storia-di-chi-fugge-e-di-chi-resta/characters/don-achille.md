Long dead by the time of book 3, but still a name that explains much
of the rione: the loan‑shark and wartime black‑marketeer of book 1,
husband of **Maria**, father of **Stefano**, **Pinuccia** and
**Alfonso**, killed in his apartment in 1955. **Alfredo Peluso** —
**Pasquale** and **Carmen**'s father — was convicted of the murder
and died in prison. He is referenced in book 3 mostly through the long
shadow he still casts: the source of the Carracci money Stefano has
now squandered, the original reason the **Solara** were able to begin
absorbing the Carracci businesses, and the inherited grievance that
keeps Pasquale's politics permanently lit.
