The second daughter of Elena and **Pietro Airota**, born a few years
after **Dede**. Where Dede is wary and clingy, Elsa — as she emerges in
the back half of book 3 — is sunnier, more biddable, the one Elena can
more easily carry in her arms while the older child screams. She tags
along behind her sister, copies her phrases, and is often the smaller
prop in Pietro's last attempts to hold the family together: the toddler
on his knee while Dede is told the news.

In the closing chapters she registers the marriage breakdown mostly
through her sister: she repeats Dede's *non voglio* ("I don't want
to"), follows Dede into Pietro's arms, and ends the book bundled along
with Dede into a neighbour's flat while Elena flies to Nino.
