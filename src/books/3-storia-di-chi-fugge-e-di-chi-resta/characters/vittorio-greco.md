Elena's father — the municipal porter at the *Comune* of Naples,
small, mild, and on the whole proud of his daughter's strange new
life. In book 3 he is mostly a sentimental presence on Elena's visits
home and on her wedding day to Pietro in Florence: tearful at the
*municipio*, quietly amazed by the Airota apartment, anxious to be
polite to the in‑laws. He is the soft counterweight to **Immacolata**'s
fierce, limping anger, and the one who, in the closing chapters of the
book, accepts the news of Elena and Nino with bewildered sadness
rather than the screaming his wife provides.
