Lila's mother — soft, anxious, devout, and over the years steadily
worn thin by **Fernando** and by **Rino**. In book 3 she appears
mostly as the voice of the old rione's grief on the phone or at the
door of the San Giovanni a Teduccio flat: scandalised by Lila's
arrangement with **Enzo**, worried sick about **Gennaro**, ashamed of
Rino's debts, and torn — like the rest of the family — between
loyalty to Lila and fear of the **Solara**.

She is one of the people Elena drops in on, dutifully, on her visits
home, and one of the people who, in the closing chapters, hears the
news of Elena and Nino with old‑fashioned scandal that, this time,
Elena cannot brush off as small‑mindedness.
