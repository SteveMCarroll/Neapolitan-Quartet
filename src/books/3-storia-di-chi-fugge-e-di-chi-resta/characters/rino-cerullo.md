Lila's older brother, the would‑be shoemaker who pushed the original
Cerullo shoe project in book 1 and was then squeezed out of it by the
**Solara**. In book 3 he is a sadder, shabbier figure: married to
**Pinuccia Carracci**, father of a small son, sliding from one
half‑job to another, in debt to **Marcello** and **Michele**, and
periodically arriving at Lila's door at San Giovanni asking for money
or for help fixing one disaster or another.

His relationship with Lila has shrunk to the dull rhythm of these
visits — he borrowing, she refusing or relenting — and to a shared
helplessness over **Gennaro**, whom Rino occasionally takes for
afternoons. By the end of the book the rione is openly saying that
Rino owes the Solara more than he can ever repay.
