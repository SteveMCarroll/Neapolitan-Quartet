Mother of **Marcello** and **Michele** and, in practice, the bank of
the rione: the Solara family's *libro rosso* — the red book in which
she records every loan, debt and favour — is kept in her apartment
above the family bar, and a large slice of the neighbourhood owes
her something.

She has been a background presence through books 1 and 2 — the heavy
woman who hands out cash and collects on it — and reappears in book 3
mostly as the silent matriarch behind the Solara expansion: present
when Marcello brings **Elisa** to dinner, present at the engagement,
present whenever the brothers consolidate another piece of rione
business.

She is murdered in chapter 122: shot dead, in her own kitchen,
according to **Carmen**'s phone call to Elena. The rione immediately
splits into two readings — a Camorra settling of accounts, or a
political hit by the same milieu around **Pasquale** and the
**Galiani** siblings — and the Solara brothers spend the closing pages
of the book openly arming themselves for revenge.
