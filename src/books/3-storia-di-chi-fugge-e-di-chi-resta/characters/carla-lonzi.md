Not a character invented by Ferrante but a real Italian writer: the
art critic turned radical feminist whose pamphlet *Sputiamo su Hegel*
("Let's spit on Hegel", 1970) and whose Rivolta Femminile manifestos
became central documents of Italian *femminismo della differenza*.

In book 3 her work enters the story through **Mariarosa Airota** and
the women's circle around her in Milan. Elena reads Lonzi (and a small
shelf of other feminist texts — *Donne è bello*, the writings of the
American radical feminists in translation) with the disorienting
sensation of finding her own life — her marriage, her body, her
silences with Pietro, her relation to Lila — described from the
outside for the first time. Lonzi's voice is one of the intellectual
hinges of the second half of the book.
