Elena's first boyfriend from book 1: the son of the rione pharmacist,
once a smug young man with a Vespa, briefly Elena's middle‑school
fiancé. In book 3 he reappears as one of the rione's small‑bore
fascists — running a *Movimento Sociale* branch out of a back room
near the pharmacy, organising squadrons of young men who turn up at
factory gates and student demonstrations, and, according to
**Pasquale** and the other militants around **Lila**, almost certainly
behind the beating of Pasquale and the **Soccavo**‑adjacent
intimidation that follows Elena's *l'Unità* piece. The handsome,
spoiled boy of book 1 has hardened into one of the small ugly faces
of the period's political violence.
