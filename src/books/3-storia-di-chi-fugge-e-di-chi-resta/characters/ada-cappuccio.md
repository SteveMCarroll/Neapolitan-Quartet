Daughter of the unstable **Melina Cappuccio** and sister of **Antonio**.
In book 2 she was the shop‑girl Stefano hired into the **Carracci**
grocery and then took, openly, as his mistress; by the end of that
book she had pushed Lila out of the marital flat.

In book 3 she has, in everything but the law, replaced Lila. She lives
in the old Carracci apartment with Stefano, runs what is left of the
grocery, and has a small daughter by him named **Maria**. The rione
treats her, with mild scandal, as *signora Carracci*. She is loyal to
Stefano even as his money collapses, fierce toward Lila when their
paths cross over **Gennaro**, and warmer to Elena than Elena expects.
At the same time the strain of the situation is visible: she is
exhausted, increasingly thin, and — like her mother before her — given
to small, anxious nervous episodes.
