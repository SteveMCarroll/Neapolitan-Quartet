Daughter of the carpenter **Alfredo Peluso** and sister of **Pasquale**.
In book 2 she ran the old **Carracci** grocery for Stefano. In book 3
she has been re‑hired by the **Solara** brothers to run the new
*salumeria* they have opened in the rione — a quiet sign of how much
of the old neighbourhood is now Solara‑owned.

She remains one of Elena's most reliable rione informants: it is from
her that Elena hears about Ada's open installation as Stefano's
mistress, about Lila's working conditions at Soccavo, about Pasquale's
movements through the political underground, and finally — over the
phone in the closing chapters — about **Manuela Solara**'s murder. The
news of the affair with Nino travels back into the rione through her
too, with the steadily widening commentary that Elena, even in
Florence, cannot escape.
