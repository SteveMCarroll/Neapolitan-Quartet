The infant son of **Nino Sarratore** and his wife **Eleonora**, born in
the second half of book 3. Elena first hears about him when Nino, in
Florence, confesses he is married; she meets him only obliquely — a
small bundle in photographs, a baby crying through a phone receiver
while Eleonora screams at her, a child Nino keeps mentioning to remind
both himself and Elena that breaking the marriage will not be free.

Albertino is barely a character in his own right at this stage of the
quartet — he functions, painfully, as a fact: the living proof that
Nino's life with Eleonora is real, that Elena's affair has a cost, and
that any future with Nino will include a second household behind him.
