One of Elena's two younger brothers (with **Peppe**), still in the
rione, working in the kind of half‑jobs the neighbourhood provides for
boys with no education and no protector. In book 3 his life becomes
quietly entangled with the **Solara**: through **Marcello**'s
courtship of **Elisa**, the Greco brothers are slipped work, small
favours, money. Elena sees on each visit how much heavier the debt of
gratitude is becoming, and how little either brother seems to mind.
