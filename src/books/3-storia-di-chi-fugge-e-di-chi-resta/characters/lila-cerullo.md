**Raffaella Cerullo**, called **Lina** by everyone except Elena, who
alone calls her **Lila**. Born August 1944, like Elena. At the end of
book 2 she had left her husband **Stefano Carracci**, moved out of the
*rione nuovo* with her small son **Gennaro** (Rinuccio), and was living
with the quiet, devoted **Enzo Scanno** in a cramped apartment in
**San Giovanni a Teduccio**, working a brutal shift at the Soccavo
salami factory while Enzo studied IBM punch‑card programming at night.
She had asked Elena, before parting, to look after Gennaro if anything
ever happened to her.

In book 3 Lila is not yet on stage. She appears, however, throughout
**chapter 1's 2005 frame** — by then she is rail‑thin and white‑haired,
sharp‑tongued, living alone with her grown son in her parents' old
apartment. She has spent decades half‑threatening to vanish, and she
warns the elderly Elena point blank not to write about her: *«Da me no»*
("Not from me"). In the 1968 timeline of chapters 2–10 she is only
talked about — by Nino, who calls her *fatta male anche nel sesso*
("badly put together, even in sex"); by Elena, who keeps deciding not
to tell her about Nino's reappearance; by Elena's own mother, in every
argument about marriage; and by the rione, where she remains *lo
scandalo sempre vivo* — the still‑live scandal.
