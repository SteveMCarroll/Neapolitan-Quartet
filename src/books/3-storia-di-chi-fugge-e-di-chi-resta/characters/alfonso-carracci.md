Stefano's younger brother, Elena's old high‑school deskmate, and one
of the book's most quietly transformed characters. In book 3 he
manages the **Solara** shoe shop on Piazza dei Martiri in the centre
of Naples, and has married **Marisa Sarratore** — an arrangement that,
the rione half‑knows, is a cover.

The truer story, which Elena pieces together over the second half of
the book, is that Alfonso has been Michele Solara's lover for some
years. He dresses with increasing care, has begun to make himself look
deliberately like Lila in her shop‑window photograph, and is the
person in the rione most able and most willing to keep Elena company
on her visits home without either gossip or claim. He is generous to
**Gennaro**, kind to the girls, and — increasingly — willing in private
to show Elena that the Carracci marriage to Marisa is a mask. By the
closing chapters of book 3 his double life has begun to crack open,
though most of its consequences belong to the years that follow.
