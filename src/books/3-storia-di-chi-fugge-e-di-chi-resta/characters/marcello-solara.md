The older of the two **Solara** brothers and, by the time of book 3,
the de facto economic power in the rione. He runs the family bar,
shops, money‑lending and political contacts together with his brother
**Michele**, and has steadily absorbed the **Carracci** shoe and
grocery businesses as Stefano's fortunes have collapsed.

In this book his most painful presence for Elena's family is his
courtship of her younger sister **Elisa**: a slow, methodical seduction
of a Greco daughter, which Elena's parents accept gratefully because
of the help it brings — refurbishments to the apartment, money slipped
to her brothers, a steady job. Elena, on her visits from Florence,
sees the cost: Elisa is now decorating a Solara villa, going by
*signora Solara* to the neighbours, and quietly being absorbed into the
clan that, in book 2, terrorised Lila.

By the closing chapters his mother **Manuela** — the Solara matriarch
and the family's loan‑shark — has been shot dead in their flat, and
Marcello, dressed in black, is openly hunting whoever ordered it.
