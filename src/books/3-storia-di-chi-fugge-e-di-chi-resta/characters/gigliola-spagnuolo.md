Daughter of the rione's *pasticciere*, the pastry‑chef who supplies the
Solara bar with its cakes. Gigliola was Elena's classmate in primary
and middle school and Lila's main social rival across book 2: engaged
to **Michele Solara**, the colder and more dangerous of the two Solara
brothers, and working behind the counter at the *bar Solara* on the
stradone. By the end of book 2 she was publicly grumbling that if
Michele didn't fix a wedding date by spring 1968 she was done.

In book 3 chapters 1–10 she appears in two registers. In the
**2005 frame** that opens chapter 1, an aging Lila identifies her
body in a flowerbed beside the church: she lies on her side in a
dark‑green raincoat, one shoe a metre away, her hair dyed a thin
ginger. (Elena does not recognise her on sight.) In the 1968 timeline
that fills the rest of these chapters she is alive and still engaged
to Michele — her mother **Signora Spagnuolo** runs the rione's only
private telephone, which is how the publisher and the Airotas reach
Elena; signora Spagnuolo's running commentary on the stairs, in
chapter 10, includes a sigh that *Gigliola è stata sfortunata* —
"Gigliola has been unlucky" — but that at least she's marrying Michele
and won't be slaving in the pastry shop for life.
