Lila's husband by the rione's reckoning. The handsome young grocer of
book 1, son of the murdered Camorra loanshark **Don Achille**, has
not divorced — divorce is barely possible in 1960s Italy — but he and
Lila are completely separated: she lives with **Enzo** at San Giovanni,
he has installed **Ada Cappuccio** in the old apartment and had a
daughter, *Maria*, by her.

In book 3 he is a much smaller, sadder figure than the ambitious young
husband of book 2. The shoe business has collapsed; the grocery has
been folded, in effect, into the **Solara** network; his money is
gone; **Marcello** Solara has bought the family flat out from under him.
He shuttles between the two shops with the resigned air of a man whose
sister‑in‑law and brother have both pawned his life. When Gennaro is
sent to him at the end of the book he barely registers the boy. The
once‑arrogant *salumiere* is now, mostly, the rione's example of how
fast the Solara tide can pull a family under.
