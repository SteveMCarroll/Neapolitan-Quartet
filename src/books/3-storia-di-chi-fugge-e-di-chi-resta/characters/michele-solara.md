The younger son of Silvio and Manuela Solara, just slightly younger
than his brother **Marcello**. The book‑1 recap describes him as
"colder, more intelligent, more violent" than Marcello — the more
dangerous of the two. By the end of book 2 he had used Lila as the
silent designer behind a whole new line of Cerullo‑branded shoes sold
out of the Solaras' *Piazza dei Martiri* shop, had had himself an
all‑out shouting match with her there, and was still publicly engaged
to **Gigliola Spagnuolo**.

In book 3 chapters 1–10 he is off‑stage, but his shadow keeps falling
across the rione. In chapter 10 **Signora Spagnuolo** sighs on the
stairs that Gigliola is at last lucky — *si è fidanzata con Michele*,
she has got engaged to Michele — and so won't have to slave in his
family's pastry shop for life. That brief domestic remark — together
with what readers of book 2 already know about how Michele treats
people — is the only direct trace of him in this batch.
