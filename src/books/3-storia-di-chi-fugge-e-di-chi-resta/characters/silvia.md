A young middle‑class student from the Milan movement, first met by
Elena in book 2 at **Mariarosa Airota**'s salon, and the mother of
Nino's first child **Mirko**. In book 3 Elena finds her again at
Mariarosa's apartment in radically changed circumstances: she has been
beaten and raped by fascists during a counter‑demonstration, has moved
in with Mariarosa, and is raising Mirko there with the help of the
women's collective around her.

Silvia is one of the book's quiet moral witnesses. She is gentle with
Elena, careful around Nino, and refuses to make scenes; but her
existence — and the child's face — does the work of indicting Nino's
pattern with women without anyone having to say it aloud. Her account
of the assault is one of the most harrowing set pieces of the Milan
chapters, and is what first cracks Elena's faith in the movement's
slogans about a coming liberation.
