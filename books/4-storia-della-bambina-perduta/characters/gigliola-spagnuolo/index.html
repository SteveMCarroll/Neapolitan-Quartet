Gigliola Spagnuolo

Michele's discarded wife and Lila's old rival, found dead in 2005.

Family: Spagnuolo (the pastry-chef's family)

Gigliola's long bargain with Michele Solara has curdled by book 4. After Manuela's death, Lila says Michele has gone mad and thrown her out; later Gigliola and Elisa visit after Tina disappears as part of the Solaras' public mobilization. On Sunday walks she appears in the Solara family ritual, one of the wives and children moving through the rione's church, stalls, and library.

Her end comes much later. In 2005, during one of Elena's visits to Naples, Gigliola is found dead in the gardens; what first looks like murder is later understood as a heart attack. The girl who once rivaled Lila has become, for Elena, a disturbing mirror of age, heaviness, and defeat.

This page reflects what's known to a reader who has finished book 4. Earlier knowledge from book 3 is summarized briefly; no events from later books appear here.
