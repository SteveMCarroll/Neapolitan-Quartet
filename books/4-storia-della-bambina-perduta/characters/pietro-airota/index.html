Pietro Airota

Elena's former husband and the daughters' reliable father.

Family: Airota (the academic family)

Pietro remains the steadiest adult in Elena's Book 4 life even after she leaves him for Nino. Hurt but restrained, he helps arrange the separation, keeps sending support, and provides Dede and Elsa with continuity when their mother moves them between Florence, Via Tasso, and the rione.

His visits to Naples gradually become less bitter. He sleeps over, includes Imma kindly when he can, and even forms an amiable intellectual rapport with Lila, who admires the learning he can put into his voice without seeming merely bookish. Later he moves toward a new life and marriage, while the daughters increasingly credit him with the stable foundation Elena could not always give.

This page reflects what's known to a reader who has finished book 4. Earlier knowledge from book 3 is summarized briefly; no events from later books appear here.
