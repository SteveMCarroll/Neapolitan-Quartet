Enzo Scanno

Lila's partner in life, parenting, and computing.

Family: Scanno (fruit-sellers)

Enzo is the quiet constant beside Lila: former fruit-seller, computer worker, partner at Basic Sight, and father to Tina. He helps anchor the practical side of Lila's life, from business to parenting, and extends rough kindness to Elena's girls, especially father-hungry Imma.

After Tina disappears, his steadiness cracks. He searches with furious desperation, later quarrels violently with Lila, and is eventually taken away in handcuffs during the political investigations that touch their circle. Yet his defining quality remains loyalty. Unlike the ambitious or predatory men around Elena and Lila, Enzo's strength is ordinary and durable: work, care, and a love that keeps returning to the same wounded household.

This page reflects what's known to a reader who has finished book 4. Earlier knowledge from book 3 is summarized briefly; no events from later books appear here.
