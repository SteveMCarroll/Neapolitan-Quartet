Gennaro ("Rino") Carracci

Lila's son, handsome and adrift after early promise.

Family: Carracci (Lila & Stefano's son)

Gennaro, called Rino in Book 4, is legally a Carracci though Lila has long treated Nino as his true father. Once the precocious child she trained obsessively, he appears now as handsome, surly, and directionless. Lila's regret over him shadows her hopes for Tina: he is the example of intelligence wasted by neglect, the rione, and passivity.

After Tina's disappearance he becomes more visibly troubled, even returning to drugs before Lila drags him home. Dede adores him, then Elsa runs off with him, briefly turning him into the center of Elena's household crisis. He remains needy, grateful, and unstable, a son Lila cannot remake and a reminder of what her brilliance cannot save.

This page reflects what's known to a reader who has finished book 4. Earlier knowledge from book 3 is summarized briefly; no events from later books appear here.
