Marcello Solara

Older Solara brother, rione boss, and Elisa's husband.

Family: Solara

Marcello enters book 4 as the more outwardly controlled Solara brother: rich, polished, married into Elena's family through Elisa, and still one of the forces that makes the rione bend. After Manuela's murder he has taken over the libro rosso — the red book of debts — and, according to Lila, pushed Michele aside in both legal and illegal business while helping bring heroin into the neighborhood.

His tie to Elena is therefore both familial and threatening. He keeps Elisa and little Silvio in Solara comfort, stays coldly polite when Elena's article exposes the brothers, and even returns Elena's broken bracelet with a terse apology. Yet his power is finally swallowed by the same violence he embodied: in 1986 he and Michele are shot outside church, leaving behind neither real mourning nor innocence.

This page reflects what's known to a reader who has finished book 4. Earlier knowledge from book 3 is summarized briefly; no events from later books appear here.
