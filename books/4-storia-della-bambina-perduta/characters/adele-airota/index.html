Adele Airota

Pietro's mother, Elena's editor-ally turned hostile guardian of the Airota name.

Family: Airota (the academic family)

Adele remains the refined publishing woman who helped make Elena an author, but in book 4 she becomes an adversary as soon as Elena chooses Nino over Pietro and the girls' stability. She and Guido shelter Dede and Elsa in Genoa, but their help is never neutral: Adele presses Elena to leave the children with the Airotas and later insults Elena's new book as a mere opuscolo — pamphlet.

The break is brutal. Elena throws Adele's hidden affairs back at her; Adele answers that Elena's freedom is really a vulgar hunger to take everything, and sends her away. In old age she is still part refuge, part judge, receiving Elsa and Rino with chilly breadth of view and later blaming the Airota scandals on social climbers around Guido.

This page reflects what's known to a reader who has finished book 4. Earlier knowledge from book 3 is summarized briefly; no events from later books appear here.
