Raffaella "Lila" Cerullo

Elena's brilliant friend, computer entrepreneur, and Tina's mother.

Family: Cerullo (the cobbler's family)

Lila begins Book 4 furious at Elena's return to Nino, but the rupture cannot sever their lives. She runs Basic Sight with Enzo, dominates and alarms the rione, and is newly pregnant when the 1980 earthquake exposes her terror of the city's collapsing margins. After Tina is born, Lila pours into her a fierce hope sharpened by regret over Gennaro, the gifted son she believes the neighborhood helped waste.

Her defiance remains dangerous: she breaks from the Solaras, protects Enzo and Gennaro, and even provokes Michele until he strikes her at Alfonso's funeral. Then Tina disappears, and Lila's life bends around an absence no explanation can close. In old age she studies Naples, speaks of erasing herself, refuses Elena's wish to publish her, and finally vanishes — leaving only the returned dolls as a last, unreadable sign.

This page reflects what's known to a reader who has finished book 4. Earlier knowledge from book 3 is summarized briefly; no events from later books appear here.
