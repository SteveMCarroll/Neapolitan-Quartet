Gianni Greco

Elena's younger brother, pulled between Solara dependence and Lila's rescue.

Family: Greco (the porter's family)

Gianni is one of Elena's two younger brothers, usually paired with Peppe. In book 4 he remains in the rione, working vague jobs and living under the protection and contamination of Marcello Solara. Their mother Immacolata comes to see this as one of her failures: she handed the boys to Marcello and now wants Elena to ask Lila and Enzo to pull them out of the lota, the filth.

Gianni and Peppe resist Elena's lectures, reminding her that their money helps keep their mother in the clinic. Yet before she dies, Immacolata forces them to accept jobs Lila has found. Later Gianni recedes into family news, still one of the siblings Elena learns about from others, a sign of how far her escape has carried her from the brothers she left behind.

This page reflects what's known to a reader who has finished book 4. Earlier knowledge from book 3 is summarized briefly; no events from later books appear here.
