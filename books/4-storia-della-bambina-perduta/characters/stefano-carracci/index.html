Stefano Carracci

Lila's estranged husband, now a diminished grocer in collapse.

Family: Carracci (Don Achille's family)

Stefano is still Lila's legal husband, though their marriage exists only as a rione fact and a legal obstacle. The handsome grocer who once seemed rich and promising has become heavy, yellowish, and dependent: Ada lives with him, their daughter Maria carries on the Carracci name, and Lila gives him money while he echoes her phrases.

Book 4 keeps him mostly at the edge of other people's disasters. His old grocery is dismantled; his son Gennaro spends weekends with him and is questioned after Tina disappears; his family is conspicuously absent from Alfonso's funeral. By the years of Enzo's arrest, Stefano is chiefly the tie that prevents Lila from being recognized as Enzo's wife.

This page reflects what's known to a reader who has finished book 4. Earlier knowledge from book 3 is summarized briefly; no events from later books appear here.
