Mirko Sarratore

Nino and Silvia's son, a quiet indictment of Nino's fatherhood.

Family: Sarratore

Mirko reappears in Milan at Mariarosa's apartment, where Silvia and the women around her have made a life after violence and political disappointment. Elena studies the boy for traces of Nino, and his face keeps doing moral work long after he leaves the scene.

Late in her pregnancy Elena suddenly asks Nino what he has done for Mirko: whether he has recognized him, looked after him, or behaved like a father. Nino evades the question and blames Lila for Elena's suspiciousness. Mirko remains almost silent, but his existence strips away Nino's excuses. Before Imma is even born, Elena can see the pattern: children accumulate around Nino, while responsibility slides elsewhere.
