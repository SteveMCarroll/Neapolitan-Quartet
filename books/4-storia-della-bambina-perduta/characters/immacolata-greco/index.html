Immacolata Greco

Elena's fierce mother, dying as Elena gives her name to a daughter.

Family: Greco (the porter's family)

Immacolata dominates Elena's early book-4 return to Naples. She arrives in Florence to defend Pietro, curses Elena for loving Nino, and later, sick with cancer, refuses doctors while accusing Elena of killing her. Her illness repeatedly drags Elena back into the rione and exposes the family's dependence on both Lila and the Solaras.

When Elena's third daughter is born, she names her Immacolata Sarratore, despite Nino's family claims. The older Immacolata briefly enjoys the private clinic Marcello secures for her, the baby, and a new feeling of trust toward Elena. Before dying, she forces Peppe and Gianni into jobs Lila has found and makes Marcello promise to marry Elisa. Elena is present at the death-rattle, wearing her mother's bracelet.

This page reflects what's known to a reader who has finished book 4. Earlier knowledge from book 3 is summarized briefly; no events from later books appear here.
