Manuela Solara

Dead Solara matriarch whose red debt books still govern the rione.

Family: Solara

Manuela Solara is already dead when book 4's main action begins, but her murder keeps pulling the neighborhood into fear. Antonio returns from Germany for her funeral; Carmen and others know the Solaras believe Pasquale killed her; and the chaos after her death helps explain Michele's unraveling.

Her real afterlife is the libro rosso, the red book of loans, debts, and favors. Marcello controls it after her death, but Lila has pieces of the accounting notebooks and gives them to Elena for the article against the Solaras. Manuela therefore remains the rione's old bank and old curse: absent in body, present in every ledger line.

This page reflects what's known to a reader who has finished book 4. Earlier knowledge from book 3 is summarized briefly; no events from later books appear here.
