Marisa Sarratore

Nino's sister, Alfonso's wife, and a nervous keeper of Sarratore respectability.

Family: Sarratore

Marisa stays close to the old neighborhood web: Nino's sister, Donato's daughter, and Alfonso Carracci's wife. In book 4 she appears around family meals and rione gatherings, often as one more sign that private truth and public respectability have come apart. Her marriage to Alfonso continues to function as a social mask, while Alfonso's connection to Michele Solara is one of the neighborhood's open secrets.

Elena notices Marisa less for action than for atmosphere. She belongs to the Sarratore household that Nino wants Elena to accept, and her resemblance to Donato helps make that family feel hereditary: charm, gossip, unease, and compromised arrangements passed from one generation to the next.

This page reflects what's known to a reader who has finished book 4. Earlier knowledge from book 3 is summarized briefly; no events from later books appear here.
