Vittorio Greco

Elena's mild father, left lonely in the rione after Immacolata's death.

Family: Greco

Vittorio remains Elena's quieter parent: frightened, affectionate, and often overrun by stronger wills. During Immacolata's illness he is found sleeping beside her in the clinic corridor or in the private room Marcello arranges, exhausted and nearly wordless. After her death, Elena's return to the rione is pressured partly by concern for her lonely father.

Yet family politics gradually cut him off from Elena. After her article against the Solaras, Elisa, little Silvio, and Vittorio exclude her in practice, absorbed into Marcello's household power. Much later Elena learns family news from Lila rather than from her father or brothers. In the epilogue she returns to Naples for Vittorio's funeral, one more severed link to the old Greco home.

This page reflects what's known to a reader who has finished book 4. Earlier knowledge from book 3 is summarized briefly; no events from later books appear here.
