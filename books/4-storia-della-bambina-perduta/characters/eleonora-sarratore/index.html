Eleonora Sarratore

Nino's wife, the official household Elena cannot make disappear.

Family: Sarratore (Nino's wife)

Eleonora remains mostly offstage, but she is one of book 4's most stubborn facts. She is pregnant as Elena and Nino resume their life together, then gives birth while Elena is setting up the via Tasso apartment and pretending she can take only what she needs from Nino. Later, when Elena runs into Nino with Eleonora and their children, the sight exposes how little he has truly separated from his legal family.

For Elena, Eleonora is less a rival than the proof of Nino's divided, self-protective life. He promises Elena uniqueness while continuing to circulate through Eleonora's home, children, and respectability. Her presence keeps turning Elena's romance back into injury and self-deception.

This page reflects what's known to a reader who has finished book 4. Earlier knowledge from book 3 is summarized briefly; no events from later books appear here.
