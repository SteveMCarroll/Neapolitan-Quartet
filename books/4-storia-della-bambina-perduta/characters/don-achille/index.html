Don Achille Carracci

Dead camorrist whose murder and money still haunt the rione.

Family: Carracci

Don Achille has been dead since the girls' childhood, but book 4 returns to him as the origin point of old fear. He was the black-market loan-shark whose murder sent Alfredo Peluso to prison and left Stefano, Pinuccia, and Alfonso with a contaminated inheritance the Solaras would later absorb.

In the final book he is most vivid in memory. During pregnancy, Elena and Lila laugh over the lost dolls, Tina and Nu, and the terrifying childhood visit to Don Achille's apartment. In the epilogue, when the dolls reappear, Elena again recalls that he denied taking them and perhaps suspected Alfonso. The dead man remains part ogre, part historical explanation.

This page reflects what's known to a reader who has finished book 4. Earlier knowledge from book 3 is summarized briefly; no events from later books appear here.
