Storia della bambina perduta

Characters

What's known about each character through the end of book 4. No spoilers for later books.

Cerullo

  • Raffaella "Lila" Cerullo Elena's brilliant friend, computer entrepreneur, and Tina's mother.
  • Tina Cerullo Lila and Enzo's daughter, the lost child of the title.

Greco

  • Elena Greco Narrator, writer, and Lila's lifelong counterpoint.
  • Elisa Greco Elena's younger sister, drawn into Marcello Solara's world.
  • Gianni Greco Elena's younger brother, pulled between Solara dependence and Lila's rescue.
  • Immacolata Greco Elena's fierce mother, dying as Elena gives her name to a daughter.
  • Peppe Greco Elena's younger brother, dependent on Solara money until Lila intervenes.
  • Vittorio Greco Elena's mild father, left lonely in the rione after Immacolata's death.

Carracci

  • Gennaro ("Rino") Carracci Lila's son, handsome and adrift after early promise.

Sarratore

  • Albertino Sarratore Nino and Eleonora's young son, proof of the family Elena tries to ignore.
  • Donato Sarratore Nino's father, the shabby poet whose old violation shadows Elena's judgment.
  • Eleonora Sarratore Nino's wife, the official household Elena cannot make disappear.
  • Imma Sarratore Elena and Nino's youngest daughter, born into the rione years.
  • Lidia Sarratore A minor Sarratore name tying Nino's mother and daughter to his official family.
  • Marisa Sarratore Nino's sister, Alfonso's wife, and a nervous keeper of Sarratore respectability.
  • Mirko Sarratore Nino and Silvia's son, a quiet indictment of Nino's fatherhood.
  • Nino Sarratore Elena's lover, public intellectual, and Imma's father.

Solara

  • Manuela Solara Dead Solara matriarch whose red debt books still govern the rione.
  • Marcello Solara Older Solara brother, rione boss, and Elisa's husband.
  • Michele Solara Younger Solara brother, violent and ruinously fixated on Lila.
  • Silvio Solara Elisa and Marcello's son, named for the Solara patriarch.

Spagnuolo

  • Gigliola Spagnuolo Michele's discarded wife and Lila's old rival, found dead in 2005.

Airota

  • Adele Airota Pietro's mother, Elena's editor-ally turned hostile guardian of the Airota name.
  • Dede Airota Elena and Pietro's elder daughter, pulled between Florence and Naples.
  • Elsa Airota Elena and Pietro's younger daughter, more at home in the rione.
  • Guido Airota Pietro's father, a socialist professor whose moral authority erodes.
  • Mariarosa Airota Pietro's sister, a Milan intellectual whose refuge and politics both fray.
  • Pietro Airota Elena's former husband and the daughters' reliable father.

Insegnanti

  • Maestra Oliviero The primary-school teacher whose old verdicts still shape Elena and Lila.
  • Professoressa Galiani Elena's former liceo teacher and a lasting measure of intellectual judgment.

Altri personaggi

  • Armando Galiani Former doctor and militant turned journalist chasing Naples's violent stories.
  • Augustin French acquaintance from Montpellier and Paris during Elena and Nino's escape.
  • Colombe French hostess whose ease about leaving children briefly tempts Elena.
  • Doriana Pietro's student and new partner after Elena leaves him.
  • Franco Mari Elena's former lover, a wounded militant who warns her against self-erasure.
  • Nadia Galiani Former student radical whose testimony reshapes Pasquale's fate.
  • Roberto Carmen's husband, briefly present in the search for Tina.
  • Silvana Household helper whose affair with Nino ends Elena's last illusion of him.
  • Silvia Mirko's mother, a Milan woman whose quiet endurance indicts Nino.

Rione

  • Ada Cappuccio Stefano's partner and Maria's mother, worn down by Carracci decline.
  • Alfonso Carracci Stefano's brother, Michele's lover, and Lila's fragile double.
  • Antonio Cappuccio Elena's old boyfriend, now a damaged Solara enforcer trying to leave.
  • Bruno Soccavo Lila's former factory boss, now a dead emblem of old class violence.
  • Carmen Peluso Pasquale's loyal sister and one of Elena's closest rione witnesses.
  • Don Achille Carracci Dead camorrist whose murder and money still haunt the rione.
  • Enzo Scanno Lila's partner in life, parenting, and computing.
  • Fernando Cerullo Lila's old shoemaker father, diminished but still a shadow over her origins.
  • Gino Elena's first boyfriend, remembered as a fascist local figure and later a murder victim.
  • Maria Carracci Stefano and Ada's daughter, a small sign of Carracci continuity.
  • Melina Cappuccio Antonio and Ada's fragile mother, carried into old age by her children.
  • Nunzia Cerullo Lila's worn mother, poor and practical in the ruins of the Cerullo family.
  • Pasquale Peluso Carmen's fugitive brother, still bound to the revolutionary politics of youth.
  • Pinuccia Carracci Carracci daughter who appears at moments of family damage.
  • Rino Cerullo Lila's ruined brother, pulled through business, drugs, and family memory.
  • Stefano Carracci Lila's estranged husband, now a diminished grocer in collapse.
