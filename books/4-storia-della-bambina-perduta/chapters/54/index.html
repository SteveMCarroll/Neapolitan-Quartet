Capitolo 54 / 110

After the night of shaking

Summary

The neighborhood empties and freezes in darkness. Elena dozes in the car, then wakes to find Lila gone. She searches near Carmen's gas pump and finds her among fallen bits of masonry, looking up toward her apartment. Lila is embarrassed, apologizes for filling Elena's head with talk, and says it was lucky they were together. She still speaks strangely, but Elena persuades her back to the car, where she sleeps again.

At dawn they re-enter Lila's apartment and make frantic phone calls. Lines fail or ring unanswered: Lila's parents, relatives near Avellino who might know about Enzo and Gennaro, Nino, and his friends. Elena does reach Pietro and asks him to keep Dede and Elsa a few more days. As news of the disaster grows, Lila's terror seems justified: the earth really had nearly split.

Exhausted, the two women walk through a mourning Naples. Elena encourages Lila to talk about the quake until Lila can speak of fear without shame, though something cautious remains in her voice and step. They reach the building of Nino and Eleonora, but no one answers; a neighbor says it is deserted. Watching pregnant Lila from a distance, Elena recalls her warnings about Nino and imagines Lila's hidden knowledge: of Michele, Marcello, the Solaras' business, drugs, and Nino's ugly facts. Lila seems silently to say that Nino has saved himself with his family and forgotten Elena.
