Capitolo 1 / 2

Waiting on the balcony

Summary

Elena finishes the long account and rereads it, not mainly to polish the writing but to see whether Lila has somehow entered the text and helped write it. She finds no proof. Perhaps Lila never could enter her computer at all; perhaps that was only Elena's fantasy of spiritelli elettronici — "electronic little spirits." Or perhaps Elena has imagined Lila's voice so long that she can no longer distinguish theirs.

During the writing, Elena often calls Rino. There is still no trace of Lila; the police only show him unidentified old women's bodies. Elena returns to Naples for two funerals, first her father Vittorio's, then Lidia Sarratore's, and misses Donato's because she is abroad. At Lidia's funeral she sees Nino, now swollen, loud, and self-celebrating, and finds him unbearable.

Both times Elena visits Pasquale in prison. He has studied, earned diplomas, and remains tied to the generous ideas of his youth. When Elena tells him about Lila, he laughs and imagines her doing intelligent, fanciful things somewhere. Asked who took Tina, he answers, "the Solaras," but Elena understands this as faith born from the neighborhood's primal experience of abuse, not knowledge. Back in Turin, she wonders whether Lila and Tina will return. On her balcony over the Po, she waits.
