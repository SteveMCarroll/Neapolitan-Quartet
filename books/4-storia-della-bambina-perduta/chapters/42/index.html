Capitolo 42 / 110

Only my true daughter

Summary

Immacolata's weakening opens an intimacy with Elena that they have never had. With Vittorio, the brothers, or Elisa, she hides her suffering in the bathroom and snarls that she is fine. With Elena she begins to expose it. One morning she tells how she became lame: as a child, she says, the angelo della morte — angel of death — touched her with the same illness, but she defeated him because she learned how to suffer.

From then on she confesses without restraint. She has never been with any man but Elena's father; she loved him more like a brother; the only beautiful moment in her life was Elena coming out of her belly. Worst of all, she says, she never felt bound to her other children, whom she experienced as punishment. Elena is her only real daughter. This makes her grief over Elena's life sharper: leaving Pietro for Nino, the son of Sarratore, is a pugnalata — a stab wound.

Elena defends Nino, promising divorce and marriage, but Immacolata says Elena has stopped moving forward. Then her guilt turns to Peppe, Gianni, and Elisa. She says she handed all three to Marcello and was wrong: Marcello is worse than Michele, dragging them into lota — filth. She urges Elena to ask Lila and Enzo to employ her brothers and free them from the Solaras. To Immacolata, Lila has become a fierce neighborhood authority, capable of putting things right and, if necessary, breaking both Solara brothers' legs.
