Capitolo 81 / 110

Return as advancement

Summary

The editor's praise instantly restores Elena's confidence. She becomes lively, almost childishly eager, pressing him for fuller approval. He has read the manuscript as a kind of autobiographical novel, a reshaping of Elena's experience of poor, violent Naples. He says he feared her return to the city would hurt her work, but now sees that it has helped her.

Elena does not tell him the book was written years earlier in Florence. He calls it hard, even maschile — masculine — yet delicate, and wants to delay publication until spring 1983 so he can edit it carefully and prepare its launch. He has already spoken to Adele, who mentioned disliking an old version; he dismisses her judgment as either outdated taste or personal bias.

After the call, Elena feels saved. The publisher pays the rest of her advance, and her mood toward Dede, Elsa, and Imma improves. More important, the rione suddenly changes meaning. What had seemed a fall now appears as cultural and political material, proof that returning to the beginning can be another step forward. Lila, Nino, and the Airotas have all, in different ways, lost authority over her. Elena decides she no longer needs Lila's approval. In July 1982 she calls Lila and says she will take the apartment above hers and return to the neighborhood.
