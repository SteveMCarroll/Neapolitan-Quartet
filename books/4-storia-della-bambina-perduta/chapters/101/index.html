Capitolo 101 / 110

The red notebooks

Summary

Elena is stunned by Michele's punch, because everyone had assumed he would never touch Lila. Marcello drags his brother away and mockingly tells Elena to put the scene in her next novel. Back home, Elena and Lila tell Enzo that Lila's swollen face comes from a fall after a fainting spell, but Elena doubts he believes them; in their relationship, even Lila's obvious lie becomes the only speakable truth.

Elena retreats upstairs with Dede, Elsa, and Imma, while Tina insists on tending her injured mother. Elena's shock turns into self-disgust: why has she returned to the neighborhood if she cannot react to its violence? She decides that writing must do harm to those who harm others, un male di parole — a harm made of words — against fists, kicks, and weapons.

She asks Lila what she knows that frightens the Solaras. Lila brings out documents, notes, and two stained red notebooks: part of Manuela Solara's legendary libro rosso (red book), now revealed as dirty school notebooks full of crude accounting. The friends organize the material together, then work at Basic Sight on Lila's computer. Elena is dazzled by the green words appearing and disappearing on the screen. For days their minds fuse over the article, though Lila demands more knowledge than Elena has. Finally Elena sends the pages to her publisher, asking whether they can put the Solaras in prison.
