Capitolo 34 / 53

Enzo in Handcuffs

Summary

Rino is deeply grateful to Elena for bringing him back upstairs. He tells her he owes her everything and will always love her. Elena answers that she is not good at all; she only needs him to remember that the apartment has one bathroom, used by Elsa, Imma, and herself. Rino apologizes constantly and keeps forgetting anyway. He promises to find work, contribute to expenses, and cause no trouble, but nothing changes. Elena stops taking complaints to Lila and simply says that everything is fine.

She is increasingly worried by the tension between Lila and Enzo. Their quarrels have changed. Once Lila shouted and Enzo stayed silent; now, after Lila's sick, animal-like cries — often with Tina's name in them — Enzo explodes in a torrent of violent dialect. Lila falls silent, then leaves the apartment, sometimes for the whole day or night.

Elena imagines Lila wandering Naples, studying buildings, churches, monuments, plaques, or going to the library as Pietro had once said. She thinks uneasily of underground tuff tunnels, catacombs, and the souls of Purgatorio ad Arco, especially when Lila's disappearances follow fights shadowed by Tina.

During one of those dark days, the police arrive. Elena fears something has happened to Lila, but they are looking for Enzo. They silence Elena roughly and take him away in handcuffs. On the stairs he calls out in dialect: tell Lina not to worry; it is una fesseria — a stupid little matter.
