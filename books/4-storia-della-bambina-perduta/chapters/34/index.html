Capitolo 34 / 110

Basic Sight and Pasquale

Summary

At first Elena notices only Lila's voice and feels guilty: once again Lila has run to see her, while Elena keeps her outside her life. Lila embraces her without attacking Nino or the girls, so Elena decides to be cordial. Lila presents Gennaro, now a large acne-marked adolescent, and worries affectionately that Latin and Greek may make him fail school. Elena offers tutoring, then announces calmly that she has moved to Naples and that things with Nino are, nei limiti del possibile ("within the limits of the possible"), clarified. Dede and Elsa happily pull Gennaro away.

Enzo asks about Elena's work; she boasts a little, then realizes nobody noticed her second book. She asks about their business. Enzo explains new IBM machines, BASIC, and their company, Basic Sight: officially he is majority partner and administrator, but Lila is the true soul. Elena is moved by Lila's logo and by the sense of being drawn generously back into their lives.

Conversation turns to exploitation, factories, dirty accounts, petrol, and finally Pasquale. Carmen reveals that he recently came by at night, clean and elegant but terribly sad, saying he would never surrender. They all reject the violent chaos around them, yet refuse to disown him.

Before leaving, Elena asks about the Solaras. Lila says Michele has gone mad after his mother's death, thrown out Gigliola, and that Marcello blames Lila. Lila urges Elena to see them often and asks for her phone number. Elena deliberately gives it wrong. Then Lila asks, in front of everyone, whether Elena will have a child with Nino.
