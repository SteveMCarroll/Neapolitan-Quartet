Capitolo 23 / 53

Pasquale in custody

Summary

The news of Pasquale's arrest absorbs Elena and Lila for weeks, even more, Elena admits, than Dede's exams. They hurry to Carmen, but she already knows the essentials and seems calm. Pasquale was arrested in the mountains near Serino, in the Avellino area. The carabinieri surrounded the farmhouse where he was hiding; he acted reasonably, did not fight, and did not try to run.

Carmen says she must now hope they do not make him die in prison as happened to her father. She still believes her brother is good, and in her emotion even says that she, Elena, and Lila carry far more wickedness inside them than Pasquale does. They have only minded their own business, while Pasquale grew up as their father taught him. For once, Carmen's sincere suffering gives her the upper hand over both friends: Lila does not object, and Elena feels uneasy.

The Peluso siblings confuse Elena. She does not think their carpenter father taught them, as Franco taught Dede, to challenge Menenius Agrippa's fable of the body politic. Yet Carmen and especially Pasquale have always instinctively known that one person's limbs are not fed when another person's belly is full. Elena cannot assimilate them to herself or Lila, but cannot distance them either. She alternates between trusting the law and agreeing that law protects the powerless badly. Though disgusted by violence, she admits that a small amount may be necessary in their ferocious world. On that confused line she commits herself to helping Pasquale, so he will not feel like a nobody abandoned by everyone, unlike the well-treated Nadia.
