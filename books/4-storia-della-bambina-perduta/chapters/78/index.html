Capitolo 78 / 110

Antonio&#39;s reckoning

Summary

Elena returns to via Tasso determined to break with Nino. Instead of Nino, the phone brings Antonio, sent by Lila to stop Elena from doing anything reckless. He comes upstairs and, with the calm of someone giving a report, lists Nino's lovers: women from different circles, some old, some recent, some welcomed into Elena's home and trusted with Dede, Elsa, and Imma.

Elena is furious that Lila and Antonio knew and waited. Antonio says he obeyed Lila because betrayal only matters when some disamore — waning love — has already matured. He recalls spying on Nino and Lila years earlier for the Solaras, refusing to deliver Lila to Michele, calling Enzo, and beating Nino. Love, he says, has neither eyes nor ears.

Elena mocks his role as protector, but then recognizes that Antonio is the one person's affection she does not doubt. Cold and empty, she sits on his lap and asks him to warm her. He resists because he is not sure she wants him. She answers with the truth of their adolescent desire, the unfinished intensity from the ponds. They have sex with a violent tenderness that seems to join that past to the present. When Nino phones, desperate to come over, Elena refuses and tells him why.
