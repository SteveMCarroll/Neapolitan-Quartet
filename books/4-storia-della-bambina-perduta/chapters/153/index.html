Capitolo 43 / 53

Nino returns rightward

Summary

Elena's answer to Imma turns out to be completely wrong. Nino slips, with his usual skill, out of the trap he had fallen into. Imma is overjoyed when she hears it and asks to see him, but for a while he vanishes and is hard to trace. When Elena and Imma finally get an appointment, he takes them to a pizzeria in Mergellina. He is not his usual lively self: he is nervous, distracted, and tells Imma never to trust political camps. He presents himself as the victim of a left that is not really left at all, worse even than the fascists. He reassures her that her father will set everything right.

Later Elena reads his increasingly aggressive articles. Nino returns to an old position of his: judicial power, he argues, must be subordinated to executive power. He writes indignantly that magistrates cannot one day fight those who want to strike at the heart of the state and the next day convince citizens that the state's own heart is diseased and must be thrown away.

Nino fights not to be thrown away. He moves through the collapsing old parties, shifting further right, and in 1994 returns radiantly to parliament. Imma is delighted that her father is again l'onorevole Sarratore — the honorable member Sarratore — and tells Elena that she writes books but cannot see far the way Lila can.
