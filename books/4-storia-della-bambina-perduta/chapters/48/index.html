Capitolo 48 / 110

The shadow&#39;s shadow

Summary

Elena quarrels with Nino about whatever confidences he must have made to the gynecologist, though he indignantly denies them. She does not repeat Lila's claim that he is a lying traitor, but the suspicion remains. One November Sunday, with Dede and Elsa in Florence with Pietro, Nino at his father-in-law's birthday, and Enzo and Gennaro away, Elena visits Lila alone, hoping to ask what Lila knows.

Both pregnant women feel their babies restless. Elena brings pastries and coffee, aiming for intimacy. She begins with the Solaras: why Marcello says Lila is ruining Michele. Lila says Marcello is cowardly and gossipy, then half-reveals the situation. Michele has long been obsessed with her and even chases l'ombra della mia ombra — "the shadow of my shadow." Marcello hates that Lila has put his brother on a leash and led him into humiliations. She also says Marcello now controls the red book once kept by Manuela, has pushed Michele out of legal and illegal business, and has brought drugs into the neighborhood.

Elena, shaped by Mariarosa's Milanese world, first treats drugs as cultured liberation, sfrenamento — unrestrained release. Lila sharply answers that a local woman's son died of heroin two weeks earlier. Elena's imagination fills the gaps: Alfonso as Lila's shadow, Michele desiring him, drugs running through Elisa, Peppe, Gianni, Rino, perhaps Gennaro. She no longer wants to ask about Nino. Then a subterranean roar begins.
