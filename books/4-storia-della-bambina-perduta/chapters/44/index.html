Capitolo 44 / 110

Opposite and together

Summary

Elena reflects that Lila has nothing new, yet remains magnetic: her pregnancy, her hold over Michele, her growing neighborhood power all seem denser than other people's lives. Because Immacolata's illness brings Elena often to the neighborhood, she sees Lila more and feels a new balance. With her public identity and private troubles, Elena now believes she can admire Lila's fascination without suffering from it.

Elena's days are frantic but strangely light. When she needs help with Dede and Elsa, she turns to Carmen, sometimes Alfonso, but most trustingly to Lila, though Lila is overworked and pregnant. Elena and Lila also begin seeing the same gynecologist, recommended by Nino. They arrange appointments together. In the waiting room they become girls again: Elena blond and calm, Lila dark and nervous, opposte e concordi — opposite and in accord — joking about other pregnant women.

One day Elena recalls their childhood dolls, Tina and Nu, and the terrifying visit to Don Achille over the dolls' disappearance. They laugh so much that they disturb the other patients' inhabited bellies. The nurse calls them by their maiden names, Cerullo and Greco, and predicts a boy for Lila, a girl for Elena; they joke about swapping babies. The visits restore affection. Yet when they part, Elena sees the distance again. She tells Lila almost everything; Lila remains vague about Enzo, Gennaro, Rino, the Solaras, and the neighborhood. Elena is still the one who left and now lives in upper Naples.
