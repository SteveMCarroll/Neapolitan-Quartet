Capitolo 77 / 110

The worst cruelty

Summary

Lila has cooked for Elena and the girls. She announces orecchiette for Dede and Elsa, takes charge of Imma and Tina, washes and dresses them alike, and creates a dazzling display of maternal competence. Elena bitterly compares the two toddlers: Tina seems healthier, livelier, the sweet fruit of Lila and Enzo's stable bond.

At lunch, Enzo protects the older girls with cheerful games and answers Dede's questions about Gennaro. Alone with Lila, Elena recounts every detail of Nino and Silvana, laughing crudely in dialect until Lila tells her to calm down. Then Elena, exhausted and frightened about money, work, Pietro, and three daughters, asks if Nino has called. Lila says he has: he called the affair a trivial mistake and urged Lila to help Elena understand that "today one lives like this." Lila hung up on him.

Elena admits she does not know how to live without Nino. Lila tells her bluntly that she has been wrong and should leave him. When Elena asks her to speak plainly, Lila finally does: Nino repeatedly sought Lila out, before and after getting involved with Elena, and during Immacolata's hospital visit claimed he was with Elena only to feel closer to Lila. His worst cruelty, Lila says, is la superficialità — superficiality.
