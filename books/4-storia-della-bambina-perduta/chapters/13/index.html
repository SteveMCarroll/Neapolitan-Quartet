Capitolo 13 / 110

Her mother&#39;s judgment

Summary

Elena is tempted to flee to Naples and Nino, escaping into her mother's city just as her mother arrives in Florence. Instead she stays, telling herself she is a woman, not a child. She and Pietro collect Immacolata at the station. Elena's mother is dressed up, powdered, and furious; she ignores Elena and fusses over Pietro's cast, muttering that she knows who really made him hit the door.

At the apartment, Immacolata drops her composure and performs a long, limping sermon. She praises Pietro extravagantly, orders Elena to beg forgiveness, and swears on Peppe, Gianni, and Elisa that she will not go home until the marriage is repaired. She praises Elena's intelligence too, insisting that husband and wife are made for each other, and urges them to think of Dede — forgetting Elsa.

Elena tries to endure the sceneggiata — the melodramatic scene — until her mother keeps grabbing her and demanding confession. Elena finally says she cannot stay with Pietro because she loves another man. Immacolata explodes: she slaps Elena, calls her a whore, curses the son of Sarratore as worse than Donato, and says she once blamed Lila but now sees Elena herself is shameless. She threatens to kill the daughter she gave birth to.

Elena shoves her away with matching violence, and Immacolata falls. Pietro, shocked by this collision of Elena's world with his, helps her up. Immacolata disowns Elena and sobs, wishing death on herself. Elena locks herself in the bedroom, horrified by the suffering separation has unleashed. Pietro later knocks gently, saying he did not want this and that even Elena does not deserve it.
