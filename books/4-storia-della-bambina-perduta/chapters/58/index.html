Capitolo 58 / 110

A promise to Immacolata

Summary

In her bad mood, Elena cannot even manage the kind lie Lila recommended. Only after Elisa tells their mother that Elena has offended her, and Peppe and Gianni shout that their mother must never send Elena to them again with police-like speeches, does Elena finally lie. She tells her mother that she has spoken to Lila and that Lila has promised to take care of Peppe and Gianni. Her mother senses Elena's lack of conviction and dismisses her gloomily, saying she should go home to her children.

Elena is angry with herself. In the following days her mother becomes even more anxious and mutters that she wants to die soon. But when Elena drags her to the hospital, the older woman seems unexpectedly more trusting. Lila has telephoned her. She says Lila promised that she would think about Peppe and Gianni, and because it is Lila, she believes a solution will be found: Lila knows how to act.

Then the conversation turns to the pregnancies. Elena's mother remarks on Lila's beauty and says Lila has told her that if she has a girl she will call her Nunzia, after her own mother. Elena says Lila will have a boy; her mother insists that if it is a girl, she will be Nunzia. Elena, certain her own child is a girl, promises to give the baby her mother's name. Her mother bitterly predicts that Sarratore's son will want to name her after his mother instead.
