Capitolo 30 / 53

Adele&#39;s House

Summary

The grandmother is Adele. Elena calls her in-laws; Guido answers coldly, then passes the phone to his wife. Adele says Elsa is there — and not alone. Elena has Enzo leave her at the Florence station and makes the complicated trip to Genoa, relieved that Elsa is safe and furious at the trap Elsa has set: forcing Elena to deal with Rino under Adele's watchful, hostile eye.

In Genoa, however, Adele and Guido are welcoming. Elsa appears dressed up, heavily made up, wearing Immacolata's bracelet and an old ring from Pietro. She acts affectionate and casual, as if Elena could not possibly be angry. Rino, by contrast, is silent, eyes lowered, almost pitiable. Elena begins to think Enzo was right: Elsa is the one driving the situation.

Adele has received them as a couple, giving them a room and letting them sleep together. After dinner, she gently presses Elena to admit her aversion to Rino. Elena refuses the opening, saying he is a good boy and that Elsa is in love, so little can be done.

All night Elena weighs the risk of damaging both daughters. She decides she cannot force Elsa and Dede under the same roof. To bring Elsa home and keep her in school, she must send Dede to Pietro. She speaks with Pietro, who accuses both parents of failing to provide emotional continuity. Later Elena confronts Elsa, retrieves the stolen things, threatens police action, and offers a deal: Elsa will return in September, go to school, and live with Rino until she tires of him.
