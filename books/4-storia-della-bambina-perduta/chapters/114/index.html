Capitolo 4 / 53

Imma in the Tangle

Summary

Elena does not know how long Lila believes Tina is alive. As Enzo is worn down by tears and fury, Lila keeps saying they will give her back. She never believes in the hit-and-run truck, and she does not seem to accept Enzo's Solara theory either. For a while she imagines a Basic Sight client kidnapped Tina for ransom, a theory Antonio also supports, though no ransom call ever comes.

The rione divides between those who think Tina is dead and a minority, including Elena and Carmen, who insist she is alive. But old hostility toward Lila resurfaces. People who once treated her like the Madonna now avoid her; they seem to think that if she had truly been as powerful as she appeared, no one could have touched her child. Offerings at her door turn from flowers and poems into cruel tokens — hair ribbons, little shoes, handmade dolls stained red, animal carcasses. When Lila throws them away and curses passersby, she becomes, in local eyes, not a pitiable mother but a terrifying madwoman.

Her certainty that Tina is alive draws her toward Imma. Elena first tries to limit contact, then lets Lila keep the child overnight. One morning she hears Imma ask when Tina will return; Lila answers by telling birth memories, then snaps in dialect that it is none of Imma's business. Elena can forgive Lila's excesses toward adults, even her suspicions of Stefano and Gennaro, but not cruelty to Imma. She begins sending Dede or Elsa to watch whenever Lila takes the child.
