Capitolo 45 / 53

The wrong child

Summary

Just before leaving for Turin, Elena spends a long, affectionate summer day with Lila. They talk for hours, and Lila eventually concentrates on Imma, now fourteen, beautiful, lively, and just finished with middle school. Elena thanks Lila for helping the girl after Nino's troubles, but Lila rejects that framing: she has always helped Imma, she says, because Tina loved her, perhaps even more than Lila herself did.

Then Lila, nervous and reluctant, asks whether Elena remembers the photograph in Panorama that showed Elena with Tina and identified Tina as Elena's daughter. Lila says she has often thought Tina may have been taken because of that photograph: whoever abducted her meant to steal Elena's child and stole Lila's instead. Elena realizes how little she has perceived of the hypotheses, fantasies, and obsessions still tormenting Lila. Had Lila's bond with Imma been protection, fear, a safeguard against kidnappers returning for the “right” child?

Seeing Elena's alarm, Lila retreats into confused talk about her reading: evil breaks through churches, convents, and books, sfonda il pavimento — bursts through the floor — where one least expects it. Then she softens, almost apologizes, and says bad novels are the ones where every cause is clear and everyone says the right thing. Tina might still return tonight, she says. She asks Elena's forgiveness, thanks her for loving Rino, and says she is glad they have been friends so long.
