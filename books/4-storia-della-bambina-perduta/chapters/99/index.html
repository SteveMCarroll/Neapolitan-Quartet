Capitolo 99 / 110

Alfonso on the shore

Summary

After the article and Carmen's complaint, Elena waits for disaster, but ordinary life resumes. Her book keeps selling; at Santa Maria di Castellabate she juggles children, errands, and even Tina, while secretly enjoying the sight of strangers reading her work under beach umbrellas. In autumn she wins a substantial prize, but the old happiness of success does not return.

Around Lila, everything darkens. Enzo quarrels violently with Gennaro, and Lila discovers the boy's arms are full of needle marks. She pushes Enzo into beating him, then throws her brother Rino out of Basic Sight, despite Gennaro's pleas. The girls are shocked, especially Dede, who argues that if life belongs to him, Gennaro should be free even to destroy it. Pinuccia and Nunzia come begging for Rino's job, but Lila shouts them away.

A worse blow follows. Alfonso, once essential to Basic Sight, has become unreliable, painted up, speaking of himself in the feminine, yet losing the Lila-like face he had tried to inhabit. He disappears; his body is later found at Coroglio, beaten to death and thrown into the sea. Elena first blames Lila for reshaping and abandoning him, then changes her mind when Lila collapses under grief. Elena senses that Lila's bond with Alfonso was deeper than she had understood, though Lila never explains it.
