Capitolo 33 / 110

Carmen Calls Lina

Summary

Elisa does not invite Elena and the girls to lunch. At the door she tries to repair the discourtesy by taking Elsa aside, which hurts Dede. Outside, Elsa reveals the secret gift: twenty thousand lire. Elena understands the money is really meant for her, but also meant to prove that Marcello loves Elisa and keeps her in comfort. After interrogation, Elsa admits the money is to be split with Dede.

While the girls quarrel over it, Carmen calls to Elena from the petrol station. Dressed in a blue attendant's smock, she warmly insists on bringing them home for lunch. Her husband appears with their two small sons, cooks, clears, and washes up; Elena is struck by the mildness and harmony of the household. For the first time that day she feels truly welcomed, and her daughters relax too.

When the children go play, Carmen speaks discreetly. She avoids asking whether Elena has moved to Naples for Nino, though she seems to know. Instead she praises her husband and children as her only consolation tra tanto dolore — amid so much pain — and recalls the past: her father's disgrace, her mother's sacrifices and death, work in Stefano's grocery, Ada tormenting her after replacing Lila, even her foolish engagement to Enzo. She does not mention Pasquale until Elena asks, and then she shuts down.

Suddenly Carmen decides to telephone Lina, saying Lila will be angry if she learns Carmen saw Elena without telling her. Elena tries to stop her, but Carmen calls anyway. Lila is delighted and is coming at once.
