Capitolo 110 / 110

Tina disappears

Summary

They search for Tina along the stradone, through the whole neighborhood, and then along the stradone again. Many people join: Antonio, Carmen, Carmen's husband Roberto, and even Marcello Solara, who mobilizes some of his own men and searches the streets himself until late at night. Lila moves like Melina, running back and forth without logic. Enzo seems even madder: he shouts at street vendors, threatens terrible things, and wants to inspect their cars, vans, and carts until the carabinieri have to calm him.

Again and again someone seems to have found Tina, and everyone breathes in relief. She is known by the whole neighborhood, and people swear they saw her a minute earlier at a stall, on a corner, in a courtyard, at the little gardens, or heading toward the tunnel with a tall man or a short one. Each sighting proves false. Gradually people's confidence and goodwill drain away.

By evening one rumor hardens into the story that will prevail. Tina supposedly ran off the sidewalk after a blue ball just as a muddy-colored truck came clattering fast over the potholes. No one saw the rest, but everyone begins to remember an impact, l'urto (the collision), as if the telling itself created the memory. The truck never braked and vanished down the stradone with Tina's body and braids. There is no blood, no trace: in that nothing, the vehicle is lost, and the child is lost forever.
