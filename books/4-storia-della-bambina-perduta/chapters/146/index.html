Capitolo 36 / 53

Elsa&#39;s Great Love

Summary

Around the same period, Elsa and Rino also collapse as a couple. For five or six months they get along well. Then Elsa confides to Elena that she is attracted to a young mathematics teacher who does not even know she exists. Asked about Rino, she says he is her great love. Elena understands that Elsa distinguishes between love and attraction, and that her attraction to the teacher does not seem to lessen her love for Rino.

Because Elena is busy writing, publishing, and traveling, Imma becomes the trusted confidante of both Elsa and Rino, and therefore Elena's source of information. Through Imma, Elena learns that Elsa seduces the teacher, that Rino grows suspicious, that Elsa stops to avoid hurting him, then starts again. After nearly a year of pain, Rino confronts Elsa in tears and asks if she still loves him. Elsa shouts that she loves someone else. Rino gives her a weak slap, tanto per mostrarsi uomo — just to show himself a man — and Elsa beats him furiously with a broom.

Lila tells Elena that Rino comes downstairs to despair when Elena is absent and Elsa stays away. She says they are not made for children, but Elena senses Lila is really trying to normalize her own lack of love for Rino.

Later Elsa leaves the teacher for a schoolmate, and Lila erupts, calling her cruel and, according to Elsa and Imma, a zoccola — a slut. The insult marks Elsa. She becomes gentler with Rino but leaves his bed, passes her exams, goes to Boston, reconciles with Dede and Pietro, then returns calmer. Eventually she leaves again for the United States, unexpectedly praising Lila as the best person she has ever known.
