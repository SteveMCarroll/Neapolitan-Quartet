Capitolo 80 / 110

Puro piacere

Summary

Just as Elena's worries multiply, Nino reappears. He still has the keys and enters without calling or knocking. Elena orders him out: the apartment is hers, and he no longer even pays rent or support for Imma. He claims, with a thin and haunted sincerity, that grief over their separation made him forget. He promises to pay again and speaks tenderly of Imma.

But his real purpose soon becomes clear. He questions Elena about Antonio, then about his own friends, trying to make her admit that she "gave in" only from spite and not desire. When he begins touching her, Elena understands that his despair is not about losing her love. It is about imagining her with other men, preferring them to him. He wants to restore his sexual primacy and then, surely, disappear again. Elena gets the keys back and throws him out. To her surprise, she feels nothing. The long love has dissolved.

The next day she investigates teaching work and puts up notices for private lessons, assuming the publisher will reject her manuscript. Then the editorial director calls: he has read it already. It is not what he expected from her, he says, but it is puro piacere di raccontare — pure pleasure in storytelling. Elena asks plainly whether it is good. He answers: extraordinary.
