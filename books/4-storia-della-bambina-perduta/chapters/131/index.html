Capitolo 21 / 53

Pietro before America

Summary

Elena does not rely only on kindness to Rino: she phones Pietro. He is almost decided on Boston, angry at Doriana, but listens carefully. He remembers Rino and asks twice whether there are drug problems and once whether he works. His conclusion is firm: the situation with Dede is out of the question. He and Elena agree that, given Dede's sensitivity, even a flirt must be prevented.

Elena asks him to come to Naples and talk to Dede, but he appears only just before her exams, essentially to say goodbye before America. He has not seen Lila and Enzo since Tina's disappearance and spends time with them. His grave, gentle attention moves Lila, who thanks him for a beautiful condolence letter he had sent years earlier. Elena is surprised by her gratitude. Lila then talks to Pietro at length about Naples, especially Palazzo Cellamare, whose history and structure she knows in minute detail.

Elena grows impatient for Pietro to speak with the girls, above all Dede. When he finally walks with Dede along the road, Elena watches from the window and notices their physical likeness: the large frame, the awkward gait, Dede seeming to enter and exit her father's body. Elsa and Imma become restless, wanting their own time with him. That evening the three girls gather around Pietro as he describes his future building in Boston, with a dark statue whose polished shoe students touch for luck. Elena reflects that men may be best as occasional fathers: superficial men like Nino disappear, serious ones like Pietro fulfill duties from afar. Maybe Dede's passion for Rino too will pass. When Elsa vows to move to America after school and Imma clings to Pietro, Elena imagines Rino already displaced.
