Capitolo 49 / 110

The earth moves

Summary

The subterranean roar cuts off Elena's thoughts. For a fraction of a second she cannot understand what is happening. The coffee cup trembles in its saucer; the table leg strikes her knee. Lila is alarmed too and tries to rise, but slowly, bent over, one hand toward Elena, the other reaching for the chair back, her eyes narrowed in concentration.

Elena shouts that it is il terremoto — the earthquake. The earth moves like an invisible storm underfoot; the walls creak, swell, pull apart and join again at the corners; dust rains from ceiling and walls. Elena wants to rush to the door but cannot take a step. Everything is heavy: feet, head, chest, above all her pregnant belly. The ground she needs to stand on keeps withdrawing.

She looks for Lila. The chair has fallen, the furniture and glass vibrate, but Lila stands in the center of the room, bent, head down, hands gripping her belly as if afraid it might fly away into the plaster dust. She seems the only solid form there, motionless and sealed off from air, sound, and sight. Elena calls her, then tries to pull her away. A submissive part of Elena wonders whether she should instead imitate Lila and protect her own child by staying still.

Finally Elena grabs Lila's arm. Lila opens pale-looking eyes and screams, non mi toccare — don't touch me. Elena understands: Lila is not in control at all. She is frozen by horror, afraid that the slightest touch will break her.
