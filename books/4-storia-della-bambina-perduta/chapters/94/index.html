Capitolo 94 / 110

Imma in the hospital

Summary

Elena feels that her name, once "the name of no one," has become the name of someone. That is why Adele has called, why Nino has tried to return, why invitations keep arriving. Leaving the children is painful, but it becomes routine. Public appearances, new cities, attractive men, and the pleasure of being admired crowd out Naples and the neighborhood. Sometimes she even forgets to call Lila and say goodnight to the girls. Only when she realizes she could live without them does she come back to herself.

Then comes a particularly ugly episode. Elena leaves for a weeklong promotional tour in the South while Imma is unwell, listless and badly congested. She blames herself rather than Lila, who is careful but too busy to watch every draft or sweaty child. Elena leaves hotel numbers and tells Lila to call if there is any problem.

At first Elena thinks constantly of Imma and telephones when she can. Then she forgets, swept into packed schedules and long celebratory dinners. When she returns, she discovers Imma has been hospitalized for three days with pneumonia. Lila has abandoned her own work and even Tina to stay with Elena's daughter. Elena is devastated, but Lila refuses to yield the hospital role, telling her to rest. Elena feels guilty yet still bright with success and freedom. After Imma is discharged, Elena tries to confess this confusion of gratitude, guilt, and pride. Lila cuts her off: Imma is well, and there are bigger problems. Elena is about to be sued by Carmen.
