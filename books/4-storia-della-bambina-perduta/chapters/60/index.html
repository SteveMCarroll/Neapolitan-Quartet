Capitolo 60 / 110

Immacolata Sarratore

Summary

On January 22, 1981, Elena gives birth for the third time. Compared with her earlier deliveries, this one is easy, almost a happy liberation. The gynecologist praises her self-control and tells her Nino is waiting outside. Elena is glad to hear it, and even gladder to find that childbirth has relieved her not only of the baby but of the bitterness of the previous month.

The newborn is a three-kilo-two-hundred-gram girl, red-faced and bald. Elena lets Nino in after tidying herself, jokes that now they are four females and she will understand if he leaves, and accepts his embraces and a gold necklace with a pendant. She calls her neighbor, then Pietro, who has arrived as expected; Dede and Elsa, happy to be with their father, answer distractedly. Elena asks Pietro to take them to Florence for a few days, but under Nino's watching gaze she cannot say the affectionate thanks she feels.

She phones her parents: her father is cold, her mother wants to see the baby immediately. Lila, busy and still pregnant, only jokes that everything goes smoothly for Elena. Nino remains present, asks about recognizing paternity, and accepts Elena's wish to name the baby after her mother. Before the municipal clerk Elena stumbles through names and marital facts, but the child is officially named Immacolata Sarratore. Nino then briefly objects to Elena's refusal to baptize her, though he drops it as soon as she treats the issue playfully. Elena watches him, happy and disoriented, wondering whether he is truly the man she loves or a stranger she is forcing into a clear shape.
