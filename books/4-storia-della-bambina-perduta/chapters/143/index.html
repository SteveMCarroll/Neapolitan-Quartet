Capitolo 33 / 53

Rino in the House

Summary

When Elsa returns home in September 1988, Elena hopes her liveliness will dispel the feeling that Lila has dragged her into her own emptiness. Instead, Rino's presence makes the apartment feel squalid. He is affectionate and completely submissive to Elsa and Imma, who treat him like a servant. Elena also begins giving him tedious errands, which frees time for work, but his large, slow body and bad bathroom habits depress her.

Elsa does nothing to improve matters. She performs a worldly, uninhibited womanhood in front of Imma, though she is only fifteen. The old bedroom she once shared with Dede becomes, for Elena, almost il luogo di un delitto — the scene of a crime. Elsa refuses to let anything be touched, while neither she nor Rino airs or cleans it. Elena tidies before Pinuccia arrives, embarrassed by the smell and traces of sex.

Pinuccia disapproves, as does the neighborhood. Elena tries not to fight Elsa too harshly, still wounded by Dede's departure. Instead she asks Lila to speak to Rino about acting like an adult. Lila turns each complaint into a quarrel, suggesting Rino should move back downstairs and Elsa can visit him there.

One evening Lila summons Rino with a shout. A violent fight erupts below among Lila, Enzo, and Rino. Later Enzo says Rino will stay downstairs from now on. Elena understands that Lila wants to be begged. After resisting, she gives in and brings Rino back upstairs, hearing Lila and Enzo resume fighting behind her.
