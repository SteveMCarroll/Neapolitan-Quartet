Capitolo 29 / 53

No Trace in Bologna

Summary

In Bologna, Elena and Enzo find no trace of Rino or Elsa. Moreno, frightened by Enzo's ferocious calm, drags them through streets and meeting places where the two would supposedly be welcomed if they were in the city. Enzo phones Lila often; Elena phones Dede. There is no good news.

Elena again says she will go to the police, convinced Rino has ruined Elsa. Enzo urges her to wait and to look at her daughters as they really are. He says Elsa would do anything to hurt Dede, and that the two sisters agree only on one thing: tormenting Imma. Elena rejects this as Lila's way of seeing them, but Enzo insists he is speaking for himself, to help her reason.

They decide to return to Naples. Near Florence, Enzo calls Lila again and learns that Dede urgently needs to speak with Elena. Elena fears something has happened to Imma, but Dede's news is different: she is leaving for the United States at once to study there. Elena tries to postpone the discussion until they can speak with her father, but Dede is immovable. Elsa, she says, can return home only after she has gone.

Then comes the news that ends the search. Elsa has phoned. She is not in Bologna at all; she is at casa di nonna — grandmother's house — meaning Adele's.
