Capitolo 14 / 110

The Airota break

Summary

After the terrible clash with her mother, Elena hopes for some sudden softening: an abrupt sign that her mother still loves her and is proud of her. Instead Immacolata spends the night whispering with Pietro, soothing him and repeating that Elena has always been her cross. Elena wants to apologize for shoving her, yet fears any apology will look like surrender. She tries to understand her mother's judgment by measuring it against the neighborhood, il rione ("the district"): there, the Solaras' prosperity, Elisa's connection to Marcello, and Lila's success with Michele and Enzo make Elena look like the failure.

Immacolata leaves without speaking to Elena, addressing only Pietro and asking about his arm and the girls. Yet her visit has an unexpected effect: Pietro seems to grasp more fully where Elena comes from, and his relations with her become courteous again. They see a lawyer about separation. When Elena brusquely tells the lawyer simply to start the paperwork, Pietro jokes that she is like her mother — or worse, her mother with education and novels.

Elena is relieved by his return to reason. She talks at length with Nino about moving to Naples, keeps quiet about still sleeping under Pietro's roof in separate rooms, and calls Dede, Elsa, and Adele. When Adele offers to keep the girls indefinitely, Elena insists she must take them back; Adele's cool warning about ambition and reality irritates her deeply.
