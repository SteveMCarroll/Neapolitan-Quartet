Capitolo 106 / 110

Calling Nino back

Summary

That day Elena writes a long letter to Nino. She lists the disadvantages weighing on Imma: her sisters have a father who cares for them, Tina has the devoted Enzo, while Imma has no fatherly presence; Elena herself is often away for work and must leave her. Imma, Elena fears, may grow up feeling permanently behind the others.

Nino does not answer. Elena finally telephones his house and reaches Eleonora, who says flatly that he is in Rome. When Elena asks her to tell him his daughter needs him, Eleonora's voice breaks. Her own children, she says, have not seen their father for at least six months. Nino has not exactly left her: he never leaves anyone. Unless a woman has the strength to leave him, he comes, goes, disappears, and returns as he pleases. Elena threatens to track him down and bring Imma to him wherever he is.

Eventually Nino calls, using his usual energetic, cheerful tone as if no time has passed. Elena cuts him short and demands that he make time for Imma. He reluctantly agrees to come to Naples for the weekend; she orders him to spend Sunday concentrating only on Imma, not chatting with Elena or charming Dede and Elsa. He solemnly promises to come weekly, and Elena believes he is sincere in that instant. His visit will be on September 16, 1984, when Elena and Lila have just turned forty and the little girls are nearly four.
