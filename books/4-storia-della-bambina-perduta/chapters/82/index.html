Capitolo 82 / 110

An experiment in recomposition

Summary

Elena moves in the middle of summer, with Antonio organizing men to empty via Tasso and carry everything to the rione. The new apartment is dark, but its dusty light moves Elena like a childhood memory. Dede and Elsa, raised in brighter places, hate the broken tiles, small bathroom, noise, and grime, though they enjoy being near Lila, school, the street, and the courtyard.

Elena eagerly tries to reclaim the neighborhood: she enrolls Elsa in her old elementary school and Dede in her old middle school, reconnects with Carmen, Alfonso, Ada, and others, and tries to believe she can govern what she once merely suffered. Pietro questions the choice, but accepts it when she says she needs the place for her work and wants Imma to remain with her sisters.

When Lila asks why she came back, Elena calls it an esperimento di ricomposizione — an experiment in putting herself back together. Lila warns that she too is in pieces. Elena then tells her the old novel is being published, wounding Lila by proving her judgment wrong. Lila withdraws into her own domain, showing the girls a new personal computer. Elena admires Tina's quickness and Lila's maternal brilliance, but Lila answers Elena's optimism with Gennaro: he too was bright once, and look at him now.
