Capitolo 8 / 110

Piazza Amedeo meeting

Summary

Before meeting Lila, Elena prepares anxiously, fearing Lila will appear at her most beautiful and dazzle Nino again. Nino compliments Elena and walks with her toward Piazza Amedeo, talking artificially about Naples under its communist mayor and urging her to move there with Dede and Elsa. Elena wants Lila to see them as a perfect couple, but Nino lets go of her just before the square; she grabs his hand.

Lila appears not glamorous but thin, a little bent, in an ugly brown coat, with silver in her black hair. She embraces Elena warmly and gives Nino only a distracted handshake. In the bar she speaks almost entirely to Elena, trying to make peace and reclaim space in her life. She asks about Pietro, the girls, divorce, where Elena and Nino will live, and Elena's new French book.

Elena deflects and asks about Enzo, Gennaro, the neighborhood, and Lila's work. Lila mentions Manuela Solara's murder, praises Elena's mother, speaks tenderly of her own parents, and grows animated describing new computer systems, though everything around the machines is, to her, merda — shit. Nino then volunteers full information about Elena's book and their plans. He and Lila debate Naples: his city of reform against her city of revenge and rupture. Lila tries to draw them to Piazza dei Martiri to see Gennaro and Alfonso. Elena refuses until Lila reveals that Antonio Cappuccio is waiting too.
