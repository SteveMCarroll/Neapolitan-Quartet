Capitolo 43 / 110

Gigliola&#39;s curses

Summary

Pregnancy time passes quickly for Elena and slowly for Lila. Elena says she is already in the fourth month; Lila says she is only in the fourth. Lila's color improves and her features soften, but her body still seems to submit to pregnancy with weary resignation, while Elena's collaborates willingly.

One Sunday, walking on Toledo with Dede and Elsa, they run into Gigliola. She is heavily made up, badly dressed, disheveled, overflowing physically, and delighted to attach herself to them. At the Gambrinus she orders and devours both savory and sweet food, then forgets the girls and begins loudly recounting Michele's cruelty. He has kicked her out of the Posillipo house, sent her back to cramped rooms in the neighborhood with the children, and even told her not just to threaten suicide but to kill herself.

As Gigliola's rage grows, she speaks less to Elena than to Lila. She treats Lila as an ally whose indifference can torture Michele: Lila must keep making him buttare il sangue — bleed, suffer — until he goes mad and dies damned. Then Gigliola touches both pregnant bellies. Elena's fourth month seems surprisingly advanced to her, but Lila's pregnancy feels endless, as if Lila were a woman who never gives birth and wants to keep the child inside forever. After Gigliola leaves, Lila is gloomy and asks what is wrong with her belly. Elena says nothing is wrong.
