Capitolo 109 / 110

Where is Tina?

Summary

After Nino leaves with the four girls, Elena hears their excited voices on the stairs and imagines Lila downstairs hearing them too. Only a floor separates the two women, yet Lila can make that distance shrink or expand at will. Elena cooks and thinks of both of them waiting for the voices of their daughters and the man they once loved. She wonders whether Lila still feels anything for Nino, whether she is watching him with Tina, and whether she will intrude on the lunch.

From the window Elena sees the holiday street below: vendors, trains, traffic, and, briefly, Marcello walking with Elisa and Silvio. The neighborhood suddenly repels her. Why is she still here when she has money enough to leave? She feels she has given Lila too much rope, troppo spago — too much leeway — and let too many knots be retied.

Outside, amid the smell of toasted almonds, Elena finds Dede and Elsa at a stall, then spots Nino talking to Lila and Enzo. Lila is holding Imma, who tugs her ear the way she does Elena's when neglected. Angry, Elena takes Imma down and asks where Tina is. Lila assumes she is with the older girls and keeps chatting with Nino, even recalling Gennaro once disappearing from school. As the memory hits, her face empties. She turns to Enzo, alarmed, and asks if he has found Tina.
