Capitolo 21 / 110

Doriana appears

Summary

Elena says the first cold shower comes at the end of 1978, through Lila, but the chain begins in mid-October when Pietro is beaten by two young men with sticks. She rushes to the hospital expecting depression; instead Pietro is cheerful, talking with students and especially with a pretty student named Doriana. Doriana, who is nearly finished with her degree and writing on Catullus, sits on his bed and holds his hand. Pietro praises her devotionally.

That evening Adele arrives in Florence with Dede and Elsa. Elena mentions Doriana, and Adele smiles: she already knows about the relationship. The next day the girls are captivated by Doriana's jewelry and play with her and Adele in the courtyard. Elena realizes a new phase has begun. Pietro has been visiting the girls less, and now she understands why. When she asks whether Doriana might live with him, he says it is too soon, but perhaps yes. Elena says they must think about the children; he agrees.

Elena then confronts Adele. Since Pietro now has a life of his own, she says, Elena too has the right to stability: she will take a house in Naples and move there with the girls. Adele reacts violently. She accuses Elena of selfishness, suggests that bringing Nino into a home with two girls is a grave imprudence, and refuses to let her granddaughters grow up in chaotic Naples. When Adele invokes Elena's mother, Elena strikes back: she once thought Adele was the maternal figure she needed, but she was wrong — her own mother is better.
