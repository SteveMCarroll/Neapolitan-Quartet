Capitolo 26 / 53

Rino Has Gone

Summary

As soon as Elena returns to the neighborhood, she goes to Lila. Lila seems listless and distracted, but Elena is used to that now and does not worry. She tells her in detail everything Nino has said, saving until the end the threatening remark about Nadia possibly harming Lila.

Elena asks whether Nadia can really hurt her. Lila answers with a careless grimace: someone can hurt you only if you love someone, and she no longer loves anyone. Elena immediately names Rino. Lila replies that Rino has gone.

The news terrifies Elena because she thinks at once of Dede and her intention to pursue him. Lila hands her a scrap of paper and comments bitterly that Rino wrote well as a child, but now has become analfabeta — illiterate — again. The note is clumsy in handwriting, spelling, and syntax. Rino says he is sick of everything, insults Enzo, and announces that he is leaving for Bologna to stay with a friend from military service. There is no mention of Dede.

Elena's relief is mixed with horror. How could this broken writing have anything to do with her daughter? Even Lila seems to read it as a failed promise, a defeat, perhaps a prophecy of what Tina might have become had she not disappeared. When Elena asks whether Rino left alone, Lila sees through her anxiety and smiles: is she afraid he left with Dede?
