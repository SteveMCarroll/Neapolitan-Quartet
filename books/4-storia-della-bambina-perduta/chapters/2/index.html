Capitolo 2 / 110

A balance on the page

Summary

Older Elena pauses over the habit of mind that grew in those years: whenever she neglected Dede and Elsa in order to pursue her own desires, she imagined Lila as the girls' advocate. Lila had only said one cruel sentence about them, yet Elena turns that sentence into an invented accusing voice. She admits she does not know what Lila truly thought of her as a mother.

The chapter becomes a reflection on narration itself. Elena wonders whether Lila has somehow slipped into this catena lunghissima di parole — this "very long chain of words" — to alter the text, add missing links, or detach others invisibly. She wants that intrusion, but fears that if she checked now, she would lose the thread. She is tired, and the filo del racconto ("thread of the story") is hard to keep taut amid years, moods, and events.

Elena sees two dangers: if she rushes past herself to recover Lila, Lila will leave fewer traces; if she tells only her own life, Lila would mock her for it and call herself merely a cancellatura — an erasure. Near the painful core of their story, Elena returns to the desk seeking on the page the balance she never found in life.
