Capitolo 39 / 110

The living puppet

Summary

Elena and Lila experience pregnancy very differently. Elena feels a rosy inner light; Lila turns greenish, nauseated, and resentful of the coso nella pancia — the "thing in her belly" — even while insisting she planned and wanted the child. Enzo becomes proudly protective, tells Stefano without drama, and then tells Gennaro, who responds obediently at first but later calls Lila a troia ("whore") to Dede.

Elena has her own announcements to make. She fears Pietro will become hostile, the girls will reject her, and Nino will ask her to abort because he prefers his double life with Eleonora to any definitive rupture. Pietro, however, reacts quietly when she tells him; he simply asks whether the girls know and tells her to take care of herself. Lila, though unwilling to speak to her own son, persuades Dede and Elsa that Elena's baby will be a funny pupazzetto vivo — a living little puppet — made with Nino. The girls accept the news and begin asking each morning how the puppet is.

Finally Elena tests Nino. Lila bluntly says that if he wants an abortion, then wife and children come before Elena. With Carmen backing her up, Elena tells him that night. Nino is delighted, says they should announce it to his parents, and promises to give the child his name. Elena presses him about Eleonora and his intact old life, but he only holds her and says, fìdati — trust me.
