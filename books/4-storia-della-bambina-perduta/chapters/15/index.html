Capitolo 15 / 110

Nino&#39;s old betrayal

Summary

Elena means to go at once to Genoa for Dede and Elsa, but a French editor asks her to turn her public arguments into an article for an important magazine. She chooses work first and writes anxiously day and night. Then Nino announces he has a few free days, and she cannot resist him. They drive to the Argentario, where Elena is dazed by love: winter sea, cultured talk, food and wine, and sex unlike anything she had known with Franco or Pietro. At dawn she drags herself from bed to write.

One evening Nino gives her a difficult essay of his on the Italsider plant at Bagnoli and asks for her opinion. As he urges her to correct it more boldly, he confesses la cosa più vergognosa — "the most shameful thing" — he has ever done. Years earlier, in high school, he had read Elena's brilliant article about the religion teacher, the piece she had worked on with Lila. There had been space for it in the student magazine; he had thrown it away out of envy.

Elena is stunned. The act threatens to connect Nino with every warning she has heard from Adele, Lila, and Antonio. But she chooses the opposite interpretation: he did not need to confess, yet did. His candor moves her, and she decides she can believe him. Their passion intensifies; she helps polish his essay and feels not only loved but esteemed. Only the girls remain at the edge of her happiness.
