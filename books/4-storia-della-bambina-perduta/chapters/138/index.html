Capitolo 28 / 53

The Night to Bologna

Summary

The theft makes Elena more determined, and Dede finally gives her the address and phone number of Rino's Bologna friend. Hating herself for yielding, Dede shouts that Elena is just like Elsa: neither respects anyone. Elena calls the friend, Moreno, and threatens him with prison by claiming she knows he deals heroin. He insists he knows nothing about Rino or Elsa.

Elena returns to Lila, where Enzo is also present and treats her gently. She demands that Lila accompany her to Bologna. Lila says there is no need; when the two run out of money, they will come back. When Elena admits Elsa stole money and jewelry, Lila says brutally that Elena failed to educate her. Enzo intervenes and offers to drive Elena himself.

They travel through the night. Elena is exhausted, ashamed of frightening Imma, uncertain whether to call Pietro or the police, and desperate only to find Elsa. Enzo tries to calm her and then speaks about Tina. A dead child, he says, is eventually mourned; a vanished child leaves no fixed absence. Every possibility remains open and unbearable: whether she is alive, dead, abused, hidden, unrecognizable.

In the car, Elena sees Enzo's tears. The journey becomes intimate. He explains that Lila has remained fixed at Tina, while the rest of life has gone on around her. Her rage is an astio — a bitterness — against everything that continues to live, grow, and prosper.
