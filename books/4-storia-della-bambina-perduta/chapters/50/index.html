Capitolo 50 / 110

The earthquake breaks open

Summary

The earthquake catches Elena and Lila indoors, and Elena has to drag Lila outside with a mixture of force, shoves, and pleading. Elena is afraid another shock will come and bring everything down on them; she keeps reminding Lila that they have to save the unborn children. In the courtyard Lila vomits, while Elena fights her own nausea.

The quake is identified as the earthquake of November 23, 1980, and its effect is not only physical but mental. It drives out the ordinary belief in stability: sounds, gestures, houses, even seconds themselves no longer feel reliable. Outside, the fear multiplies through rumors — Vesuvius has awakened, the sea has flooded the city, tunnels and cemeteries have collapsed, prisoners have escaped. Lila believes everything. Clinging to Elena, she whispers that the city is dangerous and that they must get away to open countryside.

They reach Lila's car, but she has no keys; they have fled without anything and cannot bring themselves to go back inside. Elena breaks a window with a stone and promises to have it fixed. They sit in the car, watching the terrified neighborhood gather, scatter, and surge again whenever another shout starts a panic.
