Capitolo 107 / 110

Waiting for papà

Summary

Elena tells Lila that Nino is coming to lunch and that she has forced him to spend the whole day with Imma. She hopes Lila will understand not to send Tina upstairs, but Lila either misses the point or ignores it. Instead she offers to have Nunzia cook for everyone and even suggests they eat downstairs where there is more space. Elena is unnerved by this intrusion, especially because Lila detests Nino, and insists the day is only for Imma.

The next morning, however, Tina appears at nine, neat and charming, with her toys. Elena lets her in, then immediately quarrels with Imma, who is still in pajamas but wants to play. Elena shuts Tina in another room and forces Imma to wash and dress. When she says papà — daddy — is coming, Imma screams that she does not want him. Elena wonders if she has made a mistake: perhaps Imma does not want this particular father, or any substitute, but the stable fathers Tina and her sisters already have.

Feeling ashamed of having punished Tina for the day's tension, Elena calls her back kindly. Tina advises her on making Imma's braids like hers, and Imma calms. Elena then wakes Dede and Elsa. Elsa is delighted to see Nino; Dede, distressed by puberty and her changing face, cries that she is disgusting and refuses to see anyone. Elena rushes to make herself presentable, no longer caring for Nino but not wanting him to find her aged or neglected — and still fearing Lila's power to seize a man's gaze.
