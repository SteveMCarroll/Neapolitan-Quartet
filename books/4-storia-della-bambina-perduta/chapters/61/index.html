Capitolo 61 / 110

Blood under the black dress

Summary

No relatives or neighborhood friends visit Elena in the clinic. Once she is home, she thinks she should perhaps hold a small party for them too. She has kept her origins so separate from the via Tasso apartment that no one from childhood or adolescence has ever been invited there, and she now feels that separation as a childish residue. Then Lila calls: she and Elena's mother are on their way.

Elena finds the visit intrusive. It is freezing, she is newly home, tired from nursing, baths, and stitches, and Nino is in the apartment. She does not want her mother upset, and she is uneasy about Nino and Lila meeting while she herself is not in good form. Nino, however, stays and seems pleased. When the women arrive, Elena is struck by the contrast: Lila, still pregnant and radiant with two lives, and her mother shrunken, clinging to Lila's arm like a lifeline.

Nino devotes himself to Elena's mother, pointing out the misty gulf and islands, but does not greet Lila. Elena takes Lila to see the baby. Lila smells Immacolata, naming the odor of talcum powder, milk, disinfectant, and newness, then jokes again that they can exchange babies. Elena brings the infant to her mother, who approves the name only if the child is called Imma. As the mother whispers over the baby, Lila becomes distracted, staring at the older woman's feet. Elena looks too: blood is dripping from beneath her mother's black dress.
