Capitolo 38 / 110

The market of mothers

Summary

Elena learns from Dede and Elsa how completely Lila has taken care of them: waking them, feeding them, taking them to school, supervising homework, and making life in Naples feel manageable. The girls now compare Elena unfavorably to "zia Lina" — Aunt Lina — in everything from tomato sauce to hair-drying. Dede especially adores Lila because she is the mother of Gennaro, whom Dede calls Rino and sees as the ideal boy.

Elena is hurt. During one quarrel she tells the girls to go to the mercato delle madri ("market of mothers") and buy another mother; Dede answers that they will sell Elena and buy Lila. Meanwhile Elena still has not told them she is pregnant by Nino, not Pietro.

Then Dede reports Lila's explanation of family names: Lila has Stefano as husband but sleeps with Enzo; Elena has Pietro but sleeps with Nino. Since children come from their mothers' bellies, Dede says, maybe Rino should be Cerullo and the girls Greco. Elsa blurts out that Dede wants to marry Rino, or at least sleep beside him like Lila with Enzo and Elena with Nino. The exchange relieves Elena. She sees that Lila has made the girls' situation not only acceptable but interesting. Their hostility eases, and Elena admits that Lila has helped her do less harm to them. Remembering their girlhood wounds, Elena lets solidarity return; the two pregnancies complete the reconciliation.
