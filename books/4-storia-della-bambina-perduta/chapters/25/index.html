Capitolo 25 / 110

Mariarosa&#39;s room

Summary

Elena stays at Mariarosa's for several months, and the arrangement is sometimes difficult. Mariarosa has already heard about the fight with Adele and, with her usual frankness, tells Elena that although she loves her, she was wrong to treat her mother that way. Elena insists Adele behaved terribly and helped her only so Pietro would not look bad. Mariarosa grows unusually irritated and lays down a rule: Adele is her mother. Elena may say what she likes about her father or brother, but she must leave Adele alone.

Otherwise Mariarosa is kind in her careless, open-house fashion. She gives Elena, Dede, and Elsa a large room with three camp beds, hands over towels, and then leaves them to themselves like all the other guests who come and go through the apartment. Elena notices Mariarosa's bright eyes and worn dressing gown, but barely registers her unusual pallor and thinness. She is too absorbed in her own pain.

Elena tries briefly to organize the dirty, crowded room, making beds and lists of what she and the girls need. But the effort collapses. Her head is in the air, and she spends the first days on the telephone. She misses Nino so much that she calls him immediately. He gets Mariarosa's number and begins calling morning and evening. Each call starts with joy and almost surrender, then turns into rage. Elena tells herself she too hid Pietro's return home, but it is not the same: she never slept with Pietro, while Nino sleeps with Eleonora; she has begun separation, while he has strengthened his marriage. Nino says he cannot live without her and that the Via Tasso apartment is ready, but he never says the words she needs: that Eleonora is truly finished.
