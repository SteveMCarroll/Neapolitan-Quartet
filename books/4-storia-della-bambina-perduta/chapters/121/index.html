Capitolo 11 / 53

The Wind and the Tresses

Summary

Elena becomes convinced Lila is near one of her crises. Lila seems debilitated and full of anxiety, as if something uncontrollable might split the building, the apartment, and herself in two. Elena, however, is down with flu; Dede has fever, Elsa and Imma demand attention, and Elena also owes an urgent piece for a magazine issue on the female body. A cold wind shakes the windows.

On Friday Enzo says he must go to Avellino to see an old aunt, while Gennaro will spend the weekend with Stefano, helping dismantle the grocery store furniture. Lila will be alone, and Enzo asks Elena to keep her company. The next day Elena goes downstairs to invite her to lunch. Lila opens in slippers, a dark green dressing gown, and heavy makeup on her eyes and mouth. Her apartment smells bad and is in disorder. She says that if the wind blows harder, it will carry the rione away. She claims it is a joke, but Elena is alarmed.

Elena brings Lila lunch and later dinner. This time Lila asks her to stay. In the bathroom, brushing her hair and making braids, Lila talks tenderly about Elena's daughters and about how physical resemblances move. She recalls once believing Gennaro was Nino's son, then says for some years he truly was. She imitates Elena's limp and argues that Elena has invented it to keep her mother alive inside her; by limping, Elena has stopped being a daughter and become truly a mother. Then Lila asks whether Tina, at forty-two, will be like that. Elena answers: probably, maybe yes.
