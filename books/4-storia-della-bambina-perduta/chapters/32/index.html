Capitolo 32 / 110

Elisa&#39;s Warning

Summary

Back on the main road, Elena hesitates: should she wait for her father, look for her brothers, or see whether Elisa is home? She calls her sister from a phone booth and brings Dede and Elsa to Elisa's large apartment with a view of Vesuvius.

Though Elisa does not yet look pregnant, Elena finds her changed: coarsened in body, words, and tone, with a sallow face and a poisoned mood. She shows none of the old younger-sister affection or admiration. When Elena mentions Immacolata's health, Elisa becomes unexpectedly aggressive. The doctor says their mother is physically well, she insists; it is her soul that suffers. If Elena had not disappointed her, she would not be reduced to this. Since Elena now lives in Naples and knows better than the doctor, Elisa says, she can take care of their mother herself: dalle un po' di corda — give her a little rope, humour her — and she will bloom again.

Elena tries not to quarrel and asks about Marcello. Elisa says things are fine, except for worries since his mother's death. She refuses details, then tells Elena to speak to her friend Lina. Among Marcello's worries, Elisa says, is Lila, who should learn to stay in her place.

Elena recognizes the Solaras' threatening reticence. She says her relations with Lila have loosened, but she has heard Lila left Michele and started her own business. Elisa snaps that Lila did it with "our" money: she twisted Michele around as she liked, but she will not manage that with Marcello.
