Capitolo 32 / 53

Dede Leaves

Summary

Elena prepares for Dede's departure. From time to time she asks her to stay, saying that she is causing her great pain. Dede answers coldly that Elena has so many things to do she will not even notice her absence. Elena insists that Imma adores her and that Elsa does too; the sisters can explain themselves, it will pass. But Dede cannot bear even Elsa's name. Whenever Elena mentions her, Dede looks disgusted and leaves, slamming the door.

A few evenings before leaving, Dede suddenly turns pale at dinner, begins trembling, and says she cannot breathe. Imma quickly pours her water. Dede takes a sip, then comes to sit on Elena's lap. It is startling: she is tall, bigger than Elena, and has long avoided the slightest physical contact. Elena feels her weight and warmth, holds her around the waist, and Dede wraps her arms around Elena's neck, sobbing harshly. Imma joins them, trying to be included in the embrace.

Imma believes the crisis means Dede will stay and becomes cheerful in the following days. But Dede leaves anyway. After that moment of cedimento — yielding or collapse — she becomes even harder. She kisses Imma many times and asks for weekly letters. With Elena, she accepts kisses but does not return them. Her goodbye is devastating: there can be no real relationship with Elena, because only work and Lila matter, and everything is sucked into them. Elsa's true punishment, Dede says, is having to remain there.
