Capitolo 79 / 110

A package to the publisher

Summary

Separating from Nino takes months. Elena suffers whether she rejects him or takes him back. At first he lies completely, even suggesting she misread what she saw in the bathroom. Then he admits some affairs, minimizes others, and turns his compulsive womanizing into long, learned speeches about nature, bodies, and virilità strabocchevole — overflowing virility. Elena sometimes feels moved, more often disgusted.

When she wounds his pride by saying Antonio and some of Nino's friends were better lovers, Nino disappears. Though he had sworn devotion to Dede, Elsa, and Imma, he stops paying rent and bills. Elena looks for cheaper housing but finds none. Lila mentions three rooms above her own apartment in the neighborhood. Elena resists: moving back to the rione feels like hitting bottom, despite pressure from Elisa about their lonely father Vittorio and despite the exhaustion of caring for three children.

Then the publisher calls asking about her book. In a flash, Elena lies: it is finished. She sends the old manuscript that Adele and Lila disliked years earlier, hoping at least to protect her professional reputation. After mailing it, panic returns. Adele may be asked to read it and expose the manuscript as old and bad. Elena feels she has closed one leak only to open another.
