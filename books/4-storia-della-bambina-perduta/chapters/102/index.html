Capitolo 102 / 110

Lila sends it

Summary

After a week or two, Elena's publisher calls and praises the Solara text extravagantly. Elena says she worked on it with a friend, but he insists the writing shows her at her best and even jokes that Nino should read it to learn how material becomes compelling prose. Elena wants only the lawyers' verdict. It disappoints her: the article may cause trouble, but it is not enough to send the Solaras to prison, especially given their political roots and money.

The publisher urges her to publish anyway, perhaps in L'Espresso, as a public act showing how corrupt Italy really is. Elena refuses. She does not want another querela (legal complaint), nor does she want to teach her daughters that laws bind only those who respect them. When she tells Lila, Lila stays calm but coldly asks why Elena will not publish if she trusts the publisher. Elena says it would serve no purpose; Lila says it would make things clear.

They do not see each other for a while. Then the publisher congratulates Elena on changing her mind: the piece is about to appear in L'Espresso. Elena realizes Lila sent it without permission, under Elena's name alone. Lila is cheerful, saying Elena was the writer and the famous one. Elena feels used, sent allo sbaraglio — into danger — for Lila's private war and guilt.
