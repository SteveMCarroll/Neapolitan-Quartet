Capitolo 100 / 110

Michele&#39;s threat

Summary

Very few people attend Alfonso's funeral. Elena is struck not only by the absence of the old Piazza dei Martiri circle, but by the absence of his own family: his mother Maria, his siblings Pinuccia and Stefano, and Marisa with the children who may or may not be his. The surprise mourners are the Solaras. Michele looks gaunt and unstable; Marcello appears almost solemn, though expensively dressed. They follow the procession all the way to the cemetery and the burial.

Elena keeps wondering why they have exposed themselves to the ritual, and she tries to catch Lila's eye. Lila never looks back. She watches the brothers instead, openly provoking them. When they begin to leave, she seizes Elena's arm and orders her to come along. Elena resists because of the girls, but Enzo takes them, and Elena follows as she once followed Lila toward Don Achille's stairs or into childhood stone fights.

Lila blocks Michele and asks whether he has come from remorse. He tells her to leave while she can. She warns him not to touch Gennaro or Enzo and says she knows enough to ruin both brothers. Michele answers that she knows nothing and that he no longer cares about her. He calls Elena nobody, but tells Lila that even her shadow is more alive than others. Then he promises to strip her of everything and punches her violently in the face, knocking her down among the cemetery loculi.
