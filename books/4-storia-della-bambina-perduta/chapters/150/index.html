Capitolo 40 / 53

Nino falls from grace

Summary

The news about Nino devastates Imma most of all. Though she has seen little of her father, she has made him her idol: she boasts about him at school, shows everyone a photograph of the two of them hand in hand outside Montecitorio, and imagines that the man she will marry must be tall, dark, and beautiful like him. When she learns that Nino has ended up in prison like some ordinary man from the rione — the neighborhood she now openly fears — the fragile calm Elena had created for her collapses. Imma sobs in her sleep and wakes in the night wanting Elena's bed.

One day they meet Marisa, disheveled and angrier than ever. Without considering Imma, Marisa says Nino deserves what has happened: he has always thought only of himself, refused to help his relatives, and played the honest man only with them. Imma cannot bear the attack and runs away. Elena chases her, explaining that Nino and his sister have never got along.

From then on Elena stops criticizing Nino in front of Imma, and eventually in front of anyone. In the past she had used Nino as a santo in Paradiso — a saint in heaven, an influential helper — to learn about Pasquale or Enzo. Now that the saints are falling into hell, she has no one reliable to ask about him except the bad chaos of his lawyers.
