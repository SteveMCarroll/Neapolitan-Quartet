Capitolo 46 / 110

The shadow of Lila

Summary

Elena senses that something is wrong, even when Lila seems merely to be playing her old game of suggesting hidden meanings beneath visible facts. At Basic Sight, Elena meets Lila's brother Rino after many years. He is thin, dazed, over-affectionate, babbles about computers and business, then suddenly whispers abuse against his sister before disappearing in fear of her.

Elena asks Lila if Rino is ill and then mentions her mother's wish that Peppe and Gianni be removed from Marcello Solara's influence and given work. Lila's eyes narrow. She says sharply that she cannot bring them into the office: Rino is already enough, not to mention the risks to Gennaro. When Elena presses, Lila shifts away.

The same opacity surrounds Alfonso. He now works competently for Lila and Enzo, gathering data, but his bond with Lila seems more than professional, as if a secret current from her is reshaping him. Questions about Michele, Alfonso's firing from the shoe shop, and Lila's escape from Michele are blocked by Lila's little laugh.

Shopping for maternity clothes in via dei Mille, Alfonso accompanies them to a friend's shop. Elena knows Alfonso is homosexual, that he and Marisa live separate lives, and hears gossip that Marisa is Stefano's lover. Lila suddenly asks Alfonso to show how a dark dress looks on her by putting it on himself. When he emerges, hair loose and elegant, he is almost Lila's double, a beautiful maschio-femmina — male-female figure. Lila smiles and gives him the dress, as if Elena has now seen enough.
