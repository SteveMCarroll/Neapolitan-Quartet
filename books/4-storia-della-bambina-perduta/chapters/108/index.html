Capitolo 108 / 110

Gifts for Imma

Summary

Nino arrives unusually on time, loaded with gifts. Elsa runs to meet him, followed by Tina and, cautiously, Imma, whose right-eye tic appears. Elena announces, "Here is papà," but Imma weakly signals no. Nino, however, performs well. On the stairs he sings for his little Imma, then, without greeting Elena, sits on the floor, places his daughter on his crossed legs, and showers her with kisses and compliments before warmly greeting Elsa and Dede.

Tina is bewildered. Strangers are usually dazzled by her at once, but Nino ignores her while distributing presents. She tries her gentle voice, leans against him, and attempts to share his lap; nothing works. Imma, meanwhile, is moved and flattered, seeing him as a magician who has come only for her, and shouts è mio — it's mine — whenever Tina reaches for a gift.

Elena takes Tina in her arms, and Nino realizes he has overdone it. He gives Tina an expensive-looking pen, then finally notices her resemblance to Lila. Tina writes her name, and Nino praises her, then quickly turns back to Imma out of fear Elena will reproach him. Imma grabs the pen and scribbles; Elsa mocks her, while Tina wants it back. To end the tension, Nino proposes seeing the most beautiful car in the world and leads all four girls downstairs.
