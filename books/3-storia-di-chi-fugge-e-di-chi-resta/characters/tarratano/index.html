Tarratano

Elderly literary critic; Adele's old friend. The kind voice at Elena's launch.

Family: Editoria e critica

Tarratano is an elderly Italian literary critic and a long‑time friend of Adele Airota. He appears for the first time in book 3.

He is the speaker who introduces and warmly praises Elena's debut novel at her Milan launch, in counterpoint to the man with the thick eyeglasses who attacks her. Despite having been jabbed by Nino's on‑the‑spot political reading of his colleague's intervention, Tarratano stays courtly throughout — he is friendly to Nino afterwards, patient with Elena while she signs books, and gracious at the restaurant table.

At dinner he is one of the three who carry the political conversation (Adele, Nino, Tarratano), at a level of detail that leaves Elena silently mortified at how much she doesn't yet know. He is staying, together with Adele, at Mariarosa's flat in Milan for the trip.

This page reflects what's known to a reader who has finished book 3. Earlier knowledge from book 2 is summarized briefly; no events from later books appear here.
