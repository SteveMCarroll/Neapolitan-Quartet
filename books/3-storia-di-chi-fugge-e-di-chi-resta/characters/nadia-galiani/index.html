Nadia Galiani

Once Nino's polite high‑school girlfriend; now a hard‑line revolutionary.

Family: Galiani (the Vomero professor's family)

The daughter of Elena's former high‑school teacher Professoressa Galiani and sister of Armando. In books 1 and 2 she was a sleek, well‑mannered Vomero girl who briefly dated Nino. In book 3 she reappears transformed: ostentatiously plain, badly dressed, full of slogans, and aligned with the extreme left of the student movement — the kind that, by the late chapters, is sliding from theory into political violence.

She comes down from Milan with Armando and a group of student agitators to "help" Lila and Pasquale at the Soccavo factory, holding meetings at the factory gates and pressing Lila to lead a strike. Lila, seeing the consequences other workers will pay, treats her with open contempt; Nadia and her circle treat Lila as raw material for an abstract working‑class their books promised them. Through Nadia, Elena sees how the language of liberation has begun, in some hands, to glide into something cold and dangerous.

This page reflects what's known to a reader who has finished book 3. Earlier knowledge from book 2 is summarized briefly; no events from later books appear here.
