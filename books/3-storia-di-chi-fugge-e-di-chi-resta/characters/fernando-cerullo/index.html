Fernando Cerullo

Lila's father; the old rione shoemaker.

Family: Cerullo

Lila's father, the rione shoemaker of book 1 — the man who once struck the child Lila so hard he broke her arm. In book 3 he is mostly a quiet, diminished presence: still at the workbench, still unable to forgive Lila for the separation from Stefano, still embarrassed by Rino's failures, ageing fast. Elena sees him at the door of the old workshop on her visits home; he asks after the girls and Pietro, and avoids saying Lila's name.

This page reflects what's known to a reader who has finished book 3. Earlier knowledge from book 2 is summarized briefly; no events from later books appear here.
