Marisa Sarratore

Nino's younger sister; engaged to Alfonso Carracci.

Family: Sarratore

Nino's younger sister, met by Elena on Ischia in book 1 and again in book 2. In book 3 she has married, or is on the point of marrying, Alfonso Carracci — Stefano's gay younger brother, who works at the Solara shoe shop on Piazza dei Martiri and has been carrying on, for some time, a quiet relationship with Michele Solara. The marriage is one of the rione's small, fragile masks: Alfonso behaves as the respectable Carracci husband, Marisa as the gossipy Sarratore wife.

She brings Elena into the orbit of the wider Sarratore family on Elena's Naples visits — and, painfully, of her father Donato — and is one of the channels through which the news of Nino's marriage to Eleonora first reaches Elena. She is loyal to her brother in the way Sarratore sisters are: with mounting unease.

This page reflects what's known to a reader who has finished book 3. Earlier knowledge from book 2 is summarized briefly; no events from later books appear here.
