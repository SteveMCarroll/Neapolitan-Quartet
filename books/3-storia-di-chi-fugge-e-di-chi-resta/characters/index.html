Storia di chi fugge e di chi resta

Characters

What's known about each character through the end of book 3. No spoilers for later books.

Cerullo

  • Raffaella "Lila" Cerullo Elena's brilliant friend. Now living with Enzo in San Giovanni a Teduccio.

Greco

  • Elena Greco Narrator. Now a published novelist, engaged to Pietro Airota.
  • Elisa Greco Elena's youngest sibling. The household's softening voice.
  • Gianni Greco One of Elena's younger brothers.
  • Immacolata Greco Elena's mother. Newly furious about a civil wedding.
  • Peppe Greco One of Elena's younger brothers.
  • Vittorio Greco Elena's father; municipal porter.

Carracci

  • Gennaro ("Rinuccio") Carracci Lila's small son. Legally a Carracci; called Rinuccio at home.

Sarratore

  • Albertino Sarratore Nino and Eleonora's baby son, born during the events of book 3.
  • Donato Sarratore Railwayman‑poet; Nino's father; Elena's first violator on Ischia.
  • Eleonora Sarratore Nino's wife — a rich, pregnant Neapolitan he marries while still pursuing Elena.
  • Marisa Sarratore Nino's younger sister; engaged to Alfonso Carracci.
  • Mirko Nino's son with Silvia, born in book 2 and roughly ten years old here.
  • Nino Sarratore Now an academic in Milan. Reappears at Elena's bookshop launch.

Solara

  • Manuela Solara Solara matriarch and rione loan‑shark; murdered in the closing chapters.
  • Marcello Solara Older Solara brother; effective ruler of the rione.
  • Michele Solara Younger Solara brother. Long‑time fiancé of Gigliola Spagnuolo.

Spagnuolo

  • Gigliola Spagnuolo Childhood friend and rival; engaged to Michele Solara.

Airota

  • Adele Airota Pietro's mother. Editor, translator; Elena's literary godmother.
  • Dede Airota Elena and Pietro's elder daughter, born early in the Florence years.
  • Elsa Airota Elena and Pietro's younger daughter.
  • Guido Airota Pietro's father. Professor of Greek literature at Genoa; prominent socialist.
  • Mariarosa Airota Pietro's older sister. Art historian at the Statale and an activist of the new student movement.
  • Pietro Airota Classical philologist; now an unusually young tenured professor at Florence.

Insegnanti

  • Maestra Oliviero Elena and Lila's primary‑school teacher; Elena's first patron.
  • Professoressa Galiani Elena's high‑school Latin teacher; one of Elena's most exacting silent judges.

Altri personaggi

  • Franco Mari Elena's ex‑boyfriend from the Normale; wealthy Milanese student militant.

Rione

  • Ada Cappuccio Stefano's live‑in lover; mother of his daughter Maria.
  • Alfonso Carracci Runs the Solara shoe shop on Piazza dei Martiri; married to Marisa.
  • Antonio Cappuccio Returned from Germany; works for the Solara as their bodyguard / enforcer.
  • Bruno Soccavo Heir to the Soccavo salumificio; Lila's boss for the first third of book 3.
  • Carmen Peluso Runs the new Solara salumeria; Pasquale's sister.
  • Don Achille Carracci Black‑market camorrist whose murder shaped the rione's grown‑up grudges.
  • Edo Lila's co‑worker at the Soccavo salumificio; one of her first organising allies.
  • Enzo Scanno Lives with Lila and Gennaro; teaching himself computing by correspondence.
  • Fernando Cerullo Lila's father; the old rione shoemaker.
  • Gino Pharmacist's son; now a local fascist organiser.
  • Melina Cappuccio Antonio and Ada's unstable mother.
  • Nunzia Cerullo Lila's mother.
  • Pasquale Peluso Construction worker turned full‑time revolutionary.
  • Rino Cerullo Lila's brother; once a shoemaker, now adrift and in debt.
  • Stefano Carracci Lila's estranged husband; the once‑rich grocer now in steep decline.

Movement

  • Armando Galiani Doctor and far‑left activist; Nadia's brother.
  • Carla Lonzi Real‑life Italian feminist writer; her *Sputiamo su Hegel* shakes Elena's thinking.
  • Nadia Galiani Once Nino's polite high‑school girlfriend; now a hard‑line revolutionary.
  • Silvia Milan student and Nino's former lover; mother of Mirko.

Editoria e critica

  • Gina Medotti Head of press at Elena's Milan publishing house.
  • Tarratano Elderly literary critic; Adele's old friend. The kind voice at Elena's launch.
