Bruno Soccavo

Heir to the Soccavo salumificio; Lila's boss for the first third of book 3.

Family: Soccavo (rich industrialist family)

The amiable, slightly slow son of the wealthy Soccavo family, owners of the sausage factory at San Giovanni a Teduccio where Lila works through the opening half of book 3. Elena first met him on Ischia in book 2 as one of Nino's group of well‑off friends.

In book 3 he turns out to be a far darker figure than his easy Ischia manners suggested. As Lila's boss he is shown to be sexually predatory — pulling her into a back room, putting his hand on her, expecting her silence — and, when Lila refuses, vindictive: she is moved to the freezing meat‑boning room, docked, and threatened. Around him the factory is also routinely abusive to the other women workers.

When Elena hears Lila's account she writes a piece for l'Unità about conditions at the salumificio; in retaliation, fascists hired through the Soccavo network beat Pasquale and torch the family's Solara‑lit shop. Bruno himself — last we see him in this book — is left as the embodiment of the polite young Italian capitalist who, scratched even slightly, becomes a thug.

This page reflects what's known to a reader who has finished book 3. Earlier knowledge from book 2 is summarized briefly; no events from later books appear here.
