Gennaro ("Rinuccio") Carracci

Lila's small son. Legally a Carracci; called Rinuccio at home.

Family: Carracci (Don Achille's family)

Lila's son, born about two thirds of the way through book 2 — long claimed by Lila in private as Nino's child, but legally a Carracci and registered as Gennaro, called Rinuccio in the family after his maternal uncle Rino. Through book 2 he was a placid, alert, very early‑speaking child whom Lila taught obsessively with flash‑cards and sentences. After she left Stefano and moved to San Giovanni a Teduccio with Enzo, Rinuccio was uprooted, briefly capricious, and largely looked after each day by the neighbour Titina so Lila could work at the Soccavo factory.

In book 3 chapters 1–10 he is not on stage but he is twice on Elena's mind. In chapter 4, alone in the restaurant bathroom after Nino's cruel speech about Lila being fatta male... anche nel sesso, Elena rehearses what she will say back to him — that this same "crooked" sex of theirs produced il piccolo Gennaro intelligente, "small intelligent Gennaro," and so he doesn't get to talk about it that way. In chapter 7, when Elena weighs Nino against Pietro, the existence of Lila's child by Nino is one of the things that makes any rerun of that old affair impossible to walk into clean.

This page reflects what's known to a reader who has finished book 3. Earlier knowledge from book 2 is summarized briefly; no events from later books appear here.
