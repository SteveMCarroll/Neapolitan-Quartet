Ada Cappuccio

Stefano's live‑in lover; mother of his daughter Maria.

Family: Cappuccio

Daughter of the unstable Melina Cappuccio and sister of Antonio. In book 2 she was the shop‑girl Stefano hired into the Carracci grocery and then took, openly, as his mistress; by the end of that book she had pushed Lila out of the marital flat.

In book 3 she has, in everything but the law, replaced Lila. She lives in the old Carracci apartment with Stefano, runs what is left of the grocery, and has a small daughter by him named Maria. The rione treats her, with mild scandal, as signora Carracci. She is loyal to Stefano even as his money collapses, fierce toward Lila when their paths cross over Gennaro, and warmer to Elena than Elena expects. At the same time the strain of the situation is visible: she is exhausted, increasingly thin, and — like her mother before her — given to small, anxious nervous episodes.

This page reflects what's known to a reader who has finished book 3. Earlier knowledge from book 2 is summarized briefly; no events from later books appear here.
