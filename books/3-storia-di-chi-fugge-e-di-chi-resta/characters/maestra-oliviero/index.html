Maestra Oliviero

Elena and Lila's primary‑school teacher; Elena's first patron.

Family: Insegnanti del rione

The elementary‑school teacher of both Elena and Lila. In book 1 Maestra Oliviero first marked the two of them as exceptional and made sure Elena's parents — much against their own inclination — let her sit for middle school. She tried, and failed, to do the same for Lila; she never quite forgave Fernando Cerullo for refusing. By the end of book 2 she had died in Potenza, where her sister had taken her to convalesce — Elena heard the news the autumn after her laurea, when a parcel arrived from Potenza containing the teacher's preserved bundle of Elena's primary‑school notebooks and, tucked among them, a reading copy of Lila's child story La fata blu, annotated bellissimo in the margins.

In book 3 she is gone but not silent. In chapter 3, while signing copies of her first novel at the Milan bookshop, Elena dedicates each one slowly in her best handwriting — the careful, slanted hand that la maestra Oliviero had drilled into her decades earlier.

This page reflects what's known to a reader who has finished book 3. Earlier knowledge from book 2 is summarized briefly; no events from later books appear here.
