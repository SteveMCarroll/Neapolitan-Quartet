Don Achille Carracci

Black‑market camorrist whose murder shaped the rione's grown‑up grudges.

Family: Carracci

Long dead by the time of book 3, but still a name that explains much of the rione: the loan‑shark and wartime black‑marketeer of book 1, husband of Maria, father of Stefano, Pinuccia and Alfonso, killed in his apartment in 1955. Alfredo Peluso — Pasquale and Carmen's father — was convicted of the murder and died in prison. He is referenced in book 3 mostly through the long shadow he still casts: the source of the Carracci money Stefano has now squandered, the original reason the Solara were able to begin absorbing the Carracci businesses, and the inherited grievance that keeps Pasquale's politics permanently lit.

This page reflects what's known to a reader who has finished book 3. Earlier knowledge from book 2 is summarized briefly; no events from later books appear here.
