Melina Cappuccio

Antonio and Ada's unstable mother.

Family: Cappuccio

The widow of the Cappuccio porter and mother of Antonio and Ada, undone in book 1 by her affair with Donato Sarratore and prone, since then, to long stretches of mental collapse. In book 3 she is mostly background — kept in her sister's apartment in the rione, helped by Ada, sometimes seen at the door of the old Carracci grocery where Ada has taken Stefano's place — but her presence is still a small open wound under the surface of every Sarratore conversation Elena has on her visits home.

This page reflects what's known to a reader who has finished book 3. Earlier knowledge from book 2 is summarized briefly; no events from later books appear here.
