Mariarosa Airota

Pietro's older sister. Art historian at the Statale and an activist of the new student movement.

Family: Airota (the academic family)

Pietro's older sister, twenty‑four when Elena first met her in book 2 — already a lecturer in Art History at the University of Milan, working on Piero della Francesca, easy and politically engaged. She charmed Elena across one long Pisa lunch and remains Elena's first model of a woman who has inherited, rather than laboriously copied, the ironic intellectual habits of her family.

In book 3 chapters 1–10 Mariarosa is mostly off‑stage but increasingly present in the gossip around Elena. Nino has had un litigio vero, a real argument, with her two weeks before the bookshop launch — both are now visibly on the front line of the rising movimento studentesco, the student movement. At dinner, when Nino praises her father Guido's article in Il Ponte, Adele dryly notes that "Mariarosa thought it was awful — she gets excited only by what the world cannot give." She is also the Milan host of both Adele and Tarratano during their stay. Her own politics, restless and to the left of her father's, are clearly going to matter more as book 3 goes on.

This page reflects what's known to a reader who has finished book 3. Earlier knowledge from book 2 is summarized briefly; no events from later books appear here.
