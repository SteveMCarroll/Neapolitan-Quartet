Pasquale Peluso

Construction worker turned full‑time revolutionary.

Family: Peluso (the murdered carpenter's family)

Son of the rione carpenter Alfredo Peluso (convicted in book 1 of murdering Don Achille) and brother of Carmen. In book 2 he was a young Communist bricklayer in love with Lila and briefly with Ada. In book 3 he is older, harder, and central to the political plot: still a construction worker by day, but increasingly a full‑time militant on the far left, moving between Lila and Enzo's flat at San Giovanni, the Soccavo factory gates, and the new armed groups forming around the city.

He is the one who first drags Elena down to the salumificio to see how Lila really lives, and the one who is then beaten almost to death by fascists working for the Solara/Soccavo network in retaliation for Elena's l'Unità piece. By the time Manuela Solara is murdered in chapter 122 the rione is openly whispering that Pasquale and the people around him know more than they should. In the final chapters Lila sends him quietly into hiding.

This page reflects what's known to a reader who has finished book 3. Earlier knowledge from book 2 is summarized briefly; no events from later books appear here.
