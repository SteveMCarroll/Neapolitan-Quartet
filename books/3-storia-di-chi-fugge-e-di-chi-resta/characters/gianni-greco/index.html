Gianni Greco

One of Elena's younger brothers.

Family: Greco

One of Elena's two younger brothers (with Peppe), still in the rione, working in the kind of half‑jobs the neighbourhood provides for boys with no education and no protector. In book 3 his life becomes quietly entangled with the Solara: through Marcello's courtship of Elisa, the Greco brothers are slipped work, small favours, money. Elena sees on each visit how much heavier the debt of gratitude is becoming, and how little either brother seems to mind.

This page reflects what's known to a reader who has finished book 3. Earlier knowledge from book 2 is summarized briefly; no events from later books appear here.
