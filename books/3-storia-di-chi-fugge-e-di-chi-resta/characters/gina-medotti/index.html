Gina Medotti

Head of press at Elena's Milan publishing house.

Family: Editoria e critica

Gina Medotti runs the press office at the Milan publishing house that has put out Elena's debut novel. She appears for the first time in book 3, in chapter 10.

When the Corriere della Sera's thick‑eyeglasses professor publishes a hatchet job on the novel, hers is the first of three urgent calls that reach Elena via signora Spagnuolo's downstairs telephone — the others are from Adele and from Pietro. Her existence as the publisher's named press contact is what cements, for Elena, the new fact that her own face and her own sentences are now circulating well beyond anything she can control.

This page reflects what's known to a reader who has finished book 3. Earlier knowledge from book 2 is summarized briefly; no events from later books appear here.
