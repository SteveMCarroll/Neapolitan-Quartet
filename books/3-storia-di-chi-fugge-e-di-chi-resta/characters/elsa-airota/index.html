Elsa Airota

Elena and Pietro's younger daughter.

Family: Airota (Elena's marital family)

The second daughter of Elena and Pietro Airota, born a few years after Dede. Where Dede is wary and clingy, Elsa — as she emerges in the back half of book 3 — is sunnier, more biddable, the one Elena can more easily carry in her arms while the older child screams. She tags along behind her sister, copies her phrases, and is often the smaller prop in Pietro's last attempts to hold the family together: the toddler on his knee while Dede is told the news.

In the closing chapters she registers the marriage breakdown mostly through her sister: she repeats Dede's non voglio ("I don't want to"), follows Dede into Pietro's arms, and ends the book bundled along with Dede into a neighbour's flat while Elena flies to Nino.

This page reflects what's known to a reader who has finished book 3. Earlier knowledge from book 2 is summarized briefly; no events from later books appear here.
