Rino Cerullo

Lila's brother; once a shoemaker, now adrift and in debt.

Family: Cerullo

Lila's older brother, the would‑be shoemaker who pushed the original Cerullo shoe project in book 1 and was then squeezed out of it by the Solara. In book 3 he is a sadder, shabbier figure: married to Pinuccia Carracci, father of a small son, sliding from one half‑job to another, in debt to Marcello and Michele, and periodically arriving at Lila's door at San Giovanni asking for money or for help fixing one disaster or another.

His relationship with Lila has shrunk to the dull rhythm of these visits — he borrowing, she refusing or relenting — and to a shared helplessness over Gennaro, whom Rino occasionally takes for afternoons. By the end of the book the rione is openly saying that Rino owes the Solara more than he can ever repay.

This page reflects what's known to a reader who has finished book 3. Earlier knowledge from book 2 is summarized briefly; no events from later books appear here.
