Elena Greco

Narrator. Now a published novelist, engaged to Pietro Airota.

Family: Greco (the porter's family)

Elena, called Lenuccia or Lenù, is the first‑person narrator of the whole series. Born in August 1944 in the same Naples rione as Lila, she has gone from the porter's daughter at the city council to a laureata of the Scuola Normale Superiore and the author of a small first novel about a love affair on Ischia. By the end of book 2 she was engaged to Pietro Airota — a young classical philologist — with the wedding set for September 1969 and a small diamond ring on her finger.

Book 3 opens with two Elenas. In a brief 2005 frame, Elena is old, divorced, has been a public intellectual for decades, and is walking through the rione one last time with a Lila she can no longer fully reach. From there the narrative jumps back to the spring of 1968: Elena at her bookshop launch in Milan, where she is rescued mid‑attack by a transformed Nino Sarratore and where her future mother‑in‑law Adele has arranged a surprise — Pietro himself. She returns to Naples, breaks the news of a civil wedding to her parents, weathers a brutal Corriere della Sera hatchet job that is immediately followed by an enthusiastic l'Unità counter‑review, and ends chapter 10 told that the book is starting to sell. She is also beginning a new discipline of self‑study — Freud, the daily papers, a fresh notebook — determined never again to be left speechless the way she was in Milan.

This page reflects what's known to a reader who has finished book 3. Earlier knowledge from book 2 is summarized briefly; no events from later books appear here.
