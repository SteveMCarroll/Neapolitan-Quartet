Edo

Lila's co‑worker at the Soccavo salumificio; one of her first organising allies.

Family: —

A young worker at the Soccavo sausage factory in San Giovanni a Teduccio, alongside Lila in the first stretch of book 3. Quick‑tempered and visibly furious about conditions in the meat rooms, he is the first male worker at the salumificio who takes Lila seriously — and the first one she tentatively trusts. He helps her draft a list of grievances about pay, the freezing meat‑boning rooms, the way the Soccavo foremen handle the women, and the timing of the toilet breaks. After the Galiani siblings' student push and Lila's argument with Bruno he, too, is sacked; later he is one of the workers around the Pasquale/Enzo circle who keeps a wary eye on the rione's worsening politics.

This page reflects what's known to a reader who has finished book 3. Earlier knowledge from book 2 is summarized briefly; no events from later books appear here.
