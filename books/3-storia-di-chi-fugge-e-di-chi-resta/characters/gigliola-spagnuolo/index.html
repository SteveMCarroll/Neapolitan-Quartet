Gigliola Spagnuolo

Childhood friend and rival; engaged to Michele Solara.

Family: Spagnuolo (the pastry‑chef's family)

Daughter of the rione's pasticciere, the pastry‑chef who supplies the Solara bar with its cakes. Gigliola was Elena's classmate in primary and middle school and Lila's main social rival across book 2: engaged to Michele Solara, the colder and more dangerous of the two Solara brothers, and working behind the counter at the bar Solara on the stradone. By the end of book 2 she was publicly grumbling that if Michele didn't fix a wedding date by spring 1968 she was done.

In book 3 chapters 1–10 she appears in two registers. In the 2005 frame that opens chapter 1, an aging Lila identifies her body in a flowerbed beside the church: she lies on her side in a dark‑green raincoat, one shoe a metre away, her hair dyed a thin ginger. (Elena does not recognise her on sight.) In the 1968 timeline that fills the rest of these chapters she is alive and still engaged to Michele — her mother Signora Spagnuolo runs the rione's only private telephone, which is how the publisher and the Airotas reach Elena; signora Spagnuolo's running commentary on the stairs, in chapter 10, includes a sigh that Gigliola è stata sfortunata — "Gigliola has been unlucky" — but that at least she's marrying Michele and won't be slaving in the pastry shop for life.

This page reflects what's known to a reader who has finished book 3. Earlier knowledge from book 2 is summarized briefly; no events from later books appear here.
