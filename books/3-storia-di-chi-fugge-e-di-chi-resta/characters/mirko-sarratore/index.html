Mirko

Nino's son with Silvia, born in book 2 and roughly ten years old here.

Family: Sarratore (illegitimate)

The little boy Nino fathered, in book 2, with Silvia — the Milan student he barely knew. By the time of book 3 he is around ten years old and Elena meets him for the first time at Mariarosa Airota's apartment in Milan, where Silvia and the child have been living after Silvia was raped during a fascist counter‑demonstration.

Elena is shaken by him: he has Nino's face. Nino, when forced to acknowledge the boy, is cool and embarrassed; he refuses, in front of Mariarosa and Silvia, to behave as a father in any practical sense, and the meeting becomes one of Elena's recurring private indictments of him through the middle chapters — the model of how he will, in due course, deal with Albertino, with Gennaro (whom Lila will eventually ask Elena to take in), and with any future child of his own.

Mirko himself barely speaks; he is, in this book, mostly a presence — Nino's face, on a small body that Nino will not own.

This page reflects what's known to a reader who has finished book 3. Earlier knowledge from book 2 is summarized briefly; no events from later books appear here.
