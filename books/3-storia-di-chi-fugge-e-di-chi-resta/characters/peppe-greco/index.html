Peppe Greco

One of Elena's younger brothers.

Family: Greco

Elena's other younger brother, paired through book 3 with Gianni. Like his brother, he is increasingly dependent on the Solara for work and pocket money, and is one of the things Immacolata, in her resigned way, holds against Elena: you went away and left them to this. Elena's guilt over both boys deepens through the second half of the book, but she stops short of doing anything practical about it.

This page reflects what's known to a reader who has finished book 3. Earlier knowledge from book 2 is summarized briefly; no events from later books appear here.
