Antonio Cappuccio

Returned from Germany; works for the Solara as their bodyguard / enforcer.

Family: Cappuccio

Elena's old boyfriend from books 1 and 2: the mechanic son of the dead Cappuccio father and the unstable Melina, who once worshipped Elena and then collapsed when she went to Pisa. He spent most of book 2 in the army; in book 3 he reappears in the rione as one of the Solara's men — their driver, bodyguard, fixer.

He is one of the saddest figures of the book. He has lost his hair, grown gaunt, sleeps badly, and is haunted by what he saw and did in the army (Elena gleans hints of work in West Germany on Italian state errands). He treats Elena with the careful distance of a man who has decided she belongs to a different country now; he is gentle with the girls when they are in the rione, and respectful with Lila. By the closing chapters, with Manuela dead and the Solara arming themselves, he is once again at Marcello and Michele's shoulder — the old rione promise, made good in the worst possible way.

This page reflects what's known to a reader who has finished book 3. Earlier knowledge from book 2 is summarized briefly; no events from later books appear here.
