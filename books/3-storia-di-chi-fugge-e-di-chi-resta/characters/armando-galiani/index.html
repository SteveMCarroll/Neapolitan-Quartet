Armando Galiani

Doctor and far‑left activist; Nadia's brother.

Family: Galiani

Son of Professoressa Galiani and brother of Nadia. In book 2 he was the smart, slightly arrogant medical student Elena met at the Galiani party. In book 3 he is a young doctor in Naples and an active militant of the extra‑parliamentary left, moving between hospital shifts, factory‑gate meetings at Soccavo, and the back rooms of the new revolutionary groupuscoles.

He drives Nadia south from Milan to organise the Soccavo workers and treats Lila with the slightly patronising fascination of a bourgeois revolutionary meeting his first real proletarian. He re‑enters the story again at the end as one of the small circle of Enzo, Lila and Pasquale — the comrade who patches up beaten militants, knows where to send people to hide, and who, when the murders begin in the rione, will turn out to know more than is good for him.

This page reflects what's known to a reader who has finished book 3. Earlier knowledge from book 2 is summarized briefly; no events from later books appear here.
