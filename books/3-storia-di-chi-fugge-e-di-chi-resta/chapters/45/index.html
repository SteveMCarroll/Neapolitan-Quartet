Storia di chi fugge e di chi resta · Tempo di mezzo

Capitolo 45 / 123

Summary

Back downstairs, white as a sheet, Lila brushes off Edo's questions and locks herself in the bathroom. She watches the courtyard through the window until she sees Michele — tall, nervous step, receding hairline, carefully shaved, black leather jacket over dark trousers — get into his car and drive off. Then she returns to the boning room.

Edo asks again. È andata. Ma da adesso ve la vedete voi. It's done. From now on, this is on you. — In what sense? She can't answer; Bruno's secretary arrives breathless: the boss wants her immediately.

She goes back up come quella santa che, pur avendo la testa ancora sul collo, la porta anche in mano come se gliel'avessero già tagliata — like the saint who, even with her head still on her neck, carries it in her hands as if it had already been cut off.

Bruno is already shouting before she sits. What kind of novelty is this, Lina? Volete che la mattina vi serva pure il caffè a letto? Should we bring you coffee in bed in the morning? She explains the demands one by one, in the tone she uses with Gennaro when he refuses to understand. She tells him it's in his interest to take the paper seriously and address each point con spirito costruttivo, because if he doesn't, the ispettorato del lavoro — the labor inspectorate — will be on him in no time. And what kind of trouble has he gotten into that ended him up nelle mani di gente pericolosa come i Solara?

Bruno loses what's left of his calm. Red turns to purple, eyes bloody. He'll ruin her; a few lire under the table to the four idiots she's got behind her will fix everything. His father has been bribing the inspectorate for years. The Solara will cure her of her voglia di fare la sindacalista — her urge to play union rep. Fuori, fuori immediatamente, fuori.

Lila reaches the door, turns: È l'ultima volta che mi vedi: da questo momento ho finito di lavorare qua dentro. This is the last time you'll see me; as of now I'm done working here.

The threat snaps Bruno back — he must have promised Michele he wouldn't fire her. He scrambles to walk it back: are you getting offended now? Throwing a tantrum? Come back here, decido io se ti devo licenziare o no. Stronza, ho detto vieni qua.

For a fraction of a second, the morning at Ischia comes back to her — those mornings she and Elena waited for Nino and his rich friend with the house at Forio, the polite, endlessly patient boy. She walks out and closes the door behind her. A violent shudder takes her; she's soaked in sweat. She doesn't go to the boning room, doesn't say goodbye to Edo or Teresa, walks past Filippo at the gate, who calls out Cerù, dove vai, torna dentro.

She runs down the dirt road, catches the first bus to the Marina, reaches the sea. Wanders. Cold wind. Takes the funicular up to the Vomero, walks piazza Vanvitelli, via Scarlatti, via Cimarosa, takes the funicular back down. Only very late does she realize she's forgotten Gennaro. She gets home at nine, finds Enzo and Pasquale frantic, and asks them to go fetch me — venire a cercarmi al rione.

And here the narrative breaks open. The voice that has been ours all along reveals itself: E ora eccoci qui, in piena notte, in questa stanza spoglia di San Giovanni a Teduccio. Gennaro dorme, Lila parla e parla a voce bassa, Enzo e Pasquale sono in attesa in cucina. And now here we are, late at night, in this bare room in San Giovanni a Teduccio. Gennaro sleeps; Lila talks and talks in a low voice; Enzo and Pasquale wait in the kitchen.

We have been listening to Elena. She closes the chapter on herself: Io mi sento come il cavaliere di un romanzo antico che, chiuso nella sua armatura splendente, dopo aver compiuto mille prodigiose imprese in giro per il mondo, s'imbatte in un mandriano cencioso, denutrito, che senza mai muoversi dal pascolo piega e governa a mani nude orribili bestie con un coraggio portentoso. I feel like the knight of an old romance who, sealed in his shining armor, after a thousand marvelous exploits around the world, runs into a ragged, undernourished cowherd — who, without ever leaving the pasture, bends and governs hideous beasts with bare hands and prodigious courage.

The long flashback that began many chapters ago snaps shut.
