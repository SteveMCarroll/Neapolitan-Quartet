Capitolo 95 / 123

The IBM centre at Acerra — and Nino is back

Summary

Elena is up at dawn, bags packed, ready to flee. Marcello has promised his brother he'll take them to Acerra, and Pietro — to Elena's silent fury — accepts. They leave the girls with Elisa and let the giant drive them in his car to a long low yellow building, a big shoe warehouse. On the road Pietro asks about the Solaras' business in Germany and Marcello evades in slogans: Italy, Germany, the world — professó, io sono più comunista dei comunisti, più rivoluzionario dei rivoluzionari, "I'm more Communist than the Communists, more revolutionary than the revolutionaries" — if we could flatten everything and start over I'd be the first in line. In any case, he adds, looking at Elena in the mirror, love comes first for me.

Inside, a low-ceilinged neon-lit room smells of ink, dust, overheated insulation, leather and shoe polish. Qui c'è il coso che ha affittato Michele — here's the thing my brother rented — Marcello says. The Sistema 3 sits anonymous against a wall: metal panels, dials, a red switch, a wooden shelf, keyboards. Non ci capisco niente, he shrugs — Lila handles all this, and she's never on a fixed schedule. Pietro examines the panels with care; the modernity disappoints him, and every question he puts gets the same answer: this is my brother's stuff, I have other troubles in my head.

Lila walks in just as they are about to leave, flanked by two young women carrying metal containers, snapping at them irritably. Spotting the visitors she dials up a forced courtesy. She ignores Marcello and addresses Pietro, but for Elena's benefit too, sarcastic: what do you care about all this? if you like, we'll swap — you work here and I'll take care of i romanzi, le pitture, le antichità (novels, paintings, antiquities). Once again Elena feels Lila has aged faster — not just in looks, but in voice and manner, in the dim, half-bored register she now adopts to explain not just the Sistema but the schede magnetiche, the tapes, the five-inch disks, and the coming calcolatori da tavolo one could keep at home for personal use. This is not the Lila who phoned her, childlike and elated, about the new job. She speaks now like a hyper-competent clerk on whom her boss has unloaded a chore — cioè the tourist visit by the in-laws. She finally orders the women to show Pietro the perforatrice — the keypunch — and steers Elena out into the corridor.

"Well? Did you congratulate Elisa? Do you sleep well in Marcello's house? Happy that the old witch turned sixty?"

Elena, on edge: if my sister wants this, what am I supposed to do — split her head open? Lila, dry: nelle favole si fa come si vuole e nella realtà si fa come si può — "in fairy tales you do what you want, in real life you do what you can." Elena snaps back: who is forcing you to be used by Michele? Sono io che uso lui, non lui me — "I'm using him, he isn't using me." Ti illudi. Aspetta e vedrai. Lila: stop, you don't know anything about us any more, better keep quiet. Elena: must I live in Naples to be allowed to criticise you? Lila: Napoli, Firenze: non stai combinando niente da nessuna parte, Lenù — "Naples, Florence: you aren't pulling anything off either way."

Lila notices Elena is wounded and softens. "I'm pissing you off and saying things I don't mean. You did right to leave Naples — really. Anyway, guess who's back?" Who? "Nino."

The news scorches through Elena's chest. How do you know? Marisa told her. He has won a cattedra all'università — a university chair. But wasn't Milan working out? Lila narrows her eyes. S'è sposato con una di via Tasso che è imparentata con mezzo Banco di Napoli. He has married a woman from via Tasso related to half the Banco di Napoli. They have a one-year-old son. Elena isn't sure if she is hurt; mostly she struggles to believe it. He really got married? Yes. She looks at Lila to read her face. "Do you intend to see him again?" "No. But if I happen to run into him, gli voglio dire che Gennaro non è suo — I'm going to tell him Gennaro is not his."
