Storia di chi fugge e di chi resta · Tempo di mezzo

Capitolo 42 / 123

Summary

Pasquale, driving Lila home, won't let go of it. Che ha detto Armando, come stai? What did Armando say, how are you? Lila snaps: fine, I have to eat more. Pasquale tries again — see, you neglect yourself — and she explodes. Pasquà, tu non sei mio padre, non sei mio fratello, non sei nessuno. Lasciami in pace, chiaro? You aren't my father, you aren't my brother, you aren't anybody to me. Leave me alone.

He pushes the political line: Capone advised against confronting Soccavo, the committee advised against it, it's una questione di opportunità politica — a question of political timing. Lila answers with the dialect equivalent of "I don't give a damn about your political timing — siete stati voi a mettermi nei guai — it was you people who got me into this mess, and now I'll do as I please." She doesn't ask him up.

At home she fusses over Gennaro, then loses patience and yells at him in dialect: do you want to stay still for one minute, I should slap you, why do you want to ruin my life like this? Enzo walks in mid-yell. She tries to be gentle. They eat — every bite scratches its way down her chest. After Gennaro is asleep they sit with the Zurich correspondence-course pamphlets, but Enzo is exhausted and politely keeps trying to go to bed. Lila stalls. She doesn't want to be alone in the dark with the symptoms she hid from Armando.

Finally Enzo, quietly: mi dici cos'hai? — are you going to tell me what's wrong? Nothing. — You and Pasquale, what secrets do you two have? — It's union stuff, he got me to sign up, now I have to deal with it. Then the wound: Pasquale has already told him everything she's doing at the factory. Perché l'unico che non si merita di sapere niente sono io? — why am I the only one who doesn't deserve to know anything?

Lila slips into the bathroom, fights down the panic. So Pasquale couldn't keep his mouth shut. L'amicizia tra maschi ha i suoi patti non scritti ma solidi, non come quella tra femmine — male friendship has its solid unwritten pacts, not like female friendship. She comes back out. "Pasquale is a snitch." "Pasquale is a friend. What are you?"

The tone undoes her. Tears she can't push back. Non voglio darti più guai di quanti già te ne ho dati — I don't want to give you more trouble than I already have, ho paura che mi mandi via — I'm afraid you'll send me away. Then, almost a whisper: Posso dormire con te? Can I sleep with you?

Enzo, stunned: sleep how? — Come vuoi tu — however you want. — And do you want to? Lila murmurs at the water carafe in the middle of the table — the one with a rooster's head that Gennaro loves: l'essenziale è che mi tieni vicina, the main thing is that you hold me close. She admits she feels nothing — for him or for anyone — but she does love him, she wants every night to be called in and held. Ti disgusto, do I disgust you, he asks, going pale; she begs no, no, we'll do whatever you want, right now, I'm ready.

He doesn't take her up on it. After a long silence he just says: let's go to sleep. Ognuno nella sua stanza? Each in our own room? No — mine. She undresses, comes in shaking with cold, slides under the covers, lays her head on his shoulder. He gives off a violent heat. "Can I put my cold feet next to yours?" "Yes." "Can I stroke you a little?" Lasciami stare — leave me alone. Slowly the cold and the chest-grip dissolve. Posso dormire? Can I sleep? Dormi. Sleep.
