Capitolo 113 / 123

Sex in the car — and &#39;don&#39;t go back home&#39;

Summary

Nino drives through the night and arrives in Florence around nine in the morning. He phones; Pietro picks up; he hangs up. He phones again; Elena gets there first. He's parked under her building. Scendi — come down. I can't. Come down now, or I'm coming up. With only a few days left before Viareggio, Pietro on summer break, she leaves the girls with him, says she has urgent shopping to do for the beach, and runs out to Nino.

Seeing each other again is a terrible idea. They discover that, instead of subsiding, desire era divampato — has blazed up — and makes a thousand demands with impudent urgency. At distance, on the phone, words let them fantasise and constructed enthralling prospects but also imposed an order, held them back, frightened them; quel ritrovarci insieme, this meeting again in the cramped space of the car, indifferent to the atrocious heat, gives their delirium concretezza — concreteness, fits it into la grande stagione sovversiva in corso — the great subversive season under way, makes it consistent with the era's forms of realism, the ones that demanded l'impossibile.

Non tornare a casa, he says — don't go home. She answers: and the girls, Pietro? E noi? — and us?

Before driving back to Naples he says he isn't sure he can stand not seeing her for the whole of August. They say goodbye, desperate. The rented house at Viareggio has no telephone; he gives her the number of the house on Capri, makes her promise to call every day. Se risponde tua moglie? — if your wife answers? Riattacchi — hang up. Se sei al mare? — if you're at the beach? Ho da lavorare, non sarò quasi mai al mare — I have to work, I'll hardly ever be on the beach.

In their fantasy these calls should also serve to fix a date, before or after Ferragosto (mid-August), to see each other at least once. He presses her to invent some excuse, come back to Florence, dinner at her place, avremmo dormito insieme, they would sleep together. Another madness. She kisses him, scratches him, bites him, tears herself away in a state of infelice felicità — unhappy happiness.

She runs to buy, a vanvera — at random — beach towels, a couple of swimsuits for Pietro, bucket and spade for Elsa, un costumino blu per Dede — a little blue swimsuit for Dede; in quel periodo amava il blu — in that period she loved blue.
