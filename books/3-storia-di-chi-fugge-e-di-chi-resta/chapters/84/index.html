Storia di chi fugge e di chi resta · Tempo di mezzo

Capitolo 84 / 123

Coffee in dialect: Michele&#39;s offer and Alfonso&#39;s secret

Summary

Enzo wants to be on the road by six; Elena hears him stirring at four, gets up and makes coffee. Alone in the silent kitchen they drop both the computer language and the Italian Pietro's authority imposed, and slip into dialect. She asks about him and Lila. He says it's good, but Lila never stops — work problems, fights with her mother, her father, her brother, helping gennaro-carracci with his homework, then helping Rino's children, then every child who turns up in the courtyard. Non si riguardava (she doesn't take care of herself), and she's molto affaticata, near collapse like before. Elena ventures: maybe you should make her divorce Stefano. Enzo: she couldn't care less. Anyway Stefano doesn't even know divorce exists now (the law passed in 1970). And Ada has her own survival problems: la ruota gira — the wheel turns; the Carracci have no money left, only debts to the Solaras, and Ada is grabbing what she can before it's too late.

Why don't you marry her, Elena asks. Enzo would, gladly; Lila refuses. Not just out of unwillingness to bother with divorce — chi se ne fotte che resto sposata a quello lì — but the idea itself makes her laugh: io e te che ci sposiamo? They're fine as they are, the moment they tire of each other they go their own way.

Then Enzo brings out what is really on his mind. For years now, Michele Solara has never stopped trying to hire Lila. To run a new Vomero shop. To handle accounts and taxes. To be a Christian Democrat politician's secretary. He even offered her duecentomila lire al mese just to sit there and invent things, pensate pazze, tutto quello che le passava per la testa. Michele lives in Posillipo but keeps all his business in the rione, at his parents' house, and Lila runs into him constantly. Friendly, gifts for gennaro-carracci, then serious; he doesn't insist, but he never gives up: io non m'arrendo, ti aspetto per l'eternità.

When he found out she was working for IBM he was furious. He pulled strings to get Enzo struck off the consultants' list — and with him Lila. It didn't work; good technicians are scarce. But the climate changed. Enzo found Gino's fascists outside his door and barely got inside in time. Then something more sinister: Nunzia went to pick gennaro-carracci up from school as always; the boy was gone. Teacher: stava qua un minuto fa. Lila rushed back from work and found him on a bench in the gardens, in his smock, satchel intact, looking at her with empty eyes when she asked where he'd been. She wanted to go kill Michele on the spot — for the attempted beating, for the "kidnapping." Enzo stopped her. The fascists were beating up everyone on the left; nothing proved Michele had ordered it; and gennaro-carracci himself admitted he'd just been disobedient. Still, Enzo went and talked to Michele alone at the Solara bar. Michele heard him out and said, roughly: non so di che cazzo parli, Enzù: io a gennaro-carracci sono affezionato, chi lo tocca è morto. But yes — Lila is brilliant, it's a shame she wastes her intelligence; for years he's been asking her to come work for him.

After that, Michele kept turning up "by chance" and asking eager questions about the Sistema 3. He told her, amused, that he'd asked an IBM man who was better, him or Lila, and the man had said Enzo was certainly good, but la migliore sulla piazza era Lila. Then the serious offer: he intends to rent a Sistema 3 himself and use it across all his businesses; he wants Lila as capocentro, at quattrocentomila lire al mese — four times what she earns now, more than Enzo. Nemmeno questo t'ha raccontato? No. Combined, Enzo says, they'd be at 750,000 a month. She has to give Michele an answer in September.

Walking him down to the Cinquecento, Elena nearly tells him what she knows from Lila — that Michele's love for Lila is un amore a tela di ragno (a spider-web love), not about physical possession or subaltern devotion. She doesn't, because Enzo is already at the wheel. She asks: and if Michele tries to take her? Impassible: Lo ammazzo. Ma comunque non la vuole, ha già un'amante e lo sanno tutti. Who? Marisa, l'ha messa un'altra volta incinta. Marisa Sarratore — la moglie di Alfonso. Elena, who once shrank from Alfonso's tangled confidences, asks: e Alfonso? A lui non gliene frega, dicono che è ricchione. Tutti who? Enzo, with a glint: the rione gossips about everything. And old stories are coming back up too — dicono che ad ammazzare don Achille fu la madre dei Solara. Manuela Solara. Their childhood riddle, leaked into common rumour.

He drives off. Elena, agitated, calls Lila and pours out the grievances at once: why didn't you tell me about Michele's offers, specialmente di quest'ultima; why have you given away Alfonso's secret; why have you put about the story of Manuela Solara, era un nostro gioco; why did you send me gennaro-carracci, are you worried, tell me clearly; why for once won't you tell me what is actually in your head? She is hoping, between the lines, that the conversation will finally pry their whole bond open. Lila gets irritated, treats her coldly: Elena left years ago; the Solaras, Stefano, Marisa, Alfonso, contano meno di zero in your life now. Stattene in villeggiatura, scrivi, fa' l'intellettuale, qua siamo rimasti troppo terra terra per te, sta' lontano; e mi raccomando, fai prendere un po' di sole a gennaro-carracci, che se no mi viene rachitico come suo padre. Elena, deflated, decides to mind her own business and go to the sea.
