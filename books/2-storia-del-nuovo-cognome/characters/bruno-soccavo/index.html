Bruno Soccavo

Son of the salami magnate. Nino's friend from Ischia.

Family: Soccavo (the San Giovanni a Teduccio factory)

The wolf‑faced, awkward son of the Soccavo family whose name is on every cured‑meat label in Naples. Elena meets him on Ischia in book 2's middle stretch as part of Nino's small circle: Nino, Bruno, and the Galiani siblings Armando and Nadia. He is courting Pinuccia through most of that summer; she eventually breaks it off, and he disappears from Elena's daily horizon.

He returns very late in book 2. After he has dropped out of university, he runs into Lila on the streets of San Giovanni a Teduccio, learns she has left her husband and has a child, and scribbles a recommendation on a paper napkin that gets her hired as a casual operaia in the family salami factory. By book 2's last chapters Bruno is the absent‑landlord son whose factory floor — freezers, vats, deboning rooms — has become Lila's daily ordeal.

This page reflects what's known to a reader who has finished book 2. Earlier knowledge from book 1 is summarized briefly; no events from later books appear here.
