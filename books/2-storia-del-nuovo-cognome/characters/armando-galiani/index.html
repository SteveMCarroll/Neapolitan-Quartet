Armando Galiani

Professoressa Galiani's son. Medical student. Part of Nino's Ischia circle.

Family: Galiani (the communist Vomero family)

Elder son of Professoressa Galiani; a medical student in Naples and already a competent player at his mother's political dinner parties. Elena first meets him at the New Year's Eve party at the Galiani flat in book 1.

In book 2 he turns up on Ischia in the middle stretch as a friend of Nino's, often in a small group on the Maronti beach with his sister Nadia, Nino, Bruno Soccavo and the three young women from the rione. He is gentle, attentive, slightly playboyish in a clean, well‑bred register that fascinates Pinuccia. He drifts out of the page again once the Ischia idyll collapses.

This page reflects what's known to a reader who has finished book 2. Earlier knowledge from book 1 is summarized briefly; no events from later books appear here.
