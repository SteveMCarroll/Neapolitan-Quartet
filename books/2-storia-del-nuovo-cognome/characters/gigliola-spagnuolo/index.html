Gigliola Spagnuolo

The pasticciere's daughter. Michele Solara's increasingly frustrated fiancée.

Family: Spagnuolo (the pastry-chef's family)

Daughter of the rione's pasticciere who supplies the Solara bar with its cakes. She was Elena's classmate in primary school and then again in middle school; she has been Michele Solara's fidanzata since late book 1.

In book 2 she is Lila's main rival in social standing — engaged to the cleverer Solara brother, working behind the counter at the bar Solara on the stradone, fully inside the family's network. She is the one who first picks Ada up out of pity in the parish cinema and then deliberately draws her into pizza nights with Michele, priming Ada to push for a real place at Stefano's side. By the end of the book she is publicly grumbling that if Michele doesn't fix a wedding date by spring 1968 she's done.

This page reflects what's known to a reader who has finished book 2. Earlier knowledge from book 1 is summarized briefly; no events from later books appear here.
