Antonio Cappuccio

Mechanic. Elena's boyfriend; older brother of Ada; intensely jealous of Nino.

Family: Cappuccio (the mad widow's family)

The eldest son of Melina Cappuccio, the widow whose mind broke when Donato Sarratore — Melina's lover — left the rione with his family. Antonio is a mechanic, a couple of years older than Elena. He took on the role of family head after his father's mysterious death. He has a younger sister, Ada, who is engaged to Pasquale Peluso.

Antonio and Elena are a couple as book 2 begins, though they keep the relationship informal and largely hidden from her parents. He has gone deeply into debt to Signora Solara to buy a new suit for Lila's wedding — entirely so he could spend the day at Elena's side, as she had begged him to. When he watches her spend that entire day talking with Nino instead, the humiliation lands very hard.

In the early chapters he is by turns devoted, jealous to the point of shaking, and physically forceful with her (gripping her wrist hard enough to bruise), then immediately ashamed. He refuses to take her virginity at the abandoned cannery near the ponds, telling her in dialect that he wants to do that "as one does with a wife, not like this."

This page reflects what's known to a reader who has finished book 2. Earlier knowledge from book 1 is summarized briefly; no events from later books appear here.
