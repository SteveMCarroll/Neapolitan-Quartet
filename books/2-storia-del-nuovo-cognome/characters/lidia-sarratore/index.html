Lidia Sarratore

Donato's long-suffering wife. Marisa and Nino's mother.

Family: Sarratore (the railwayman-poet's family)

Donato Sarratore's wife and the mother of Nino, Marisa, Pino, Clelia and Ciro. In book 1 the family lived in the rione, in the apartment above Melina Cappuccio; after the affair between her husband and Melina, Lidia insisted the family leave for the Mergellina.

In book 2 she rarely appears on the page. Elena, summering on Ischia with the family in book 1's flashback‑like opening to Lila's parallel story, finds her dignified, exhausted, and very far from naïve about her husband. By book 2's main chronology she is mainly an absence behind the always‑awkward fact of Nino's family, glimpsed only at fleeting moments around the Sarratore household.

This page reflects what's known to a reader who has finished book 2. Earlier knowledge from book 1 is summarized briefly; no events from later books appear here.
