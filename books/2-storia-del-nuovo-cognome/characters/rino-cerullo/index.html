Rino Cerullo

Lila's older brother. Co-runs the Cerullo shoe workshop; engaged to Pinuccia Carracci.

Family: Cerullo (the cobbler's family)

Lila's older brother and a cobbler like their father Fernando. With Lila's design help and Stefano Carracci's money he has set up the calzaturificio Cerullo — the Cerullo shoe factory — the family's shot at climbing out of poverty. Rino is as ambitious as his sister but lacks her instincts; he resents the way the neighbourhood is run by violence yet is happy enough to do business with the Solaras when it serves the factory.

He is engaged to Pinuccia Carracci, Stefano's sister, which knits the Cerullos and the Carraccis together commercially as well as personally.

In the opening chapters Rino is delighted to see Marcello Solara strut into the wedding wearing the prototype shoes — to him this is the deal landing, not a betrayal. When Lila pointedly snubs the Solaras on her way out he laughs it off to Stefano: "That one was born crooked, I'm sorry for you."

This page reflects what's known to a reader who has finished book 2. Earlier knowledge from book 1 is summarized briefly; no events from later books appear here.
