Storia del nuovo cognome

Characters

What's known about each character through the end of book 2. No spoilers for later books.

Cerullo

  • Fernando ("Dino") Cerullo Rino and Pinuccia's first son, called Dino. The object of Lila's reading experiments.
  • Fernando Cerullo The rione's shoemaker. Lila's father; opposed her schooling.
  • Nunzia Cerullo Lila's mother. Worn out, devout, the household's anxious peacemaker.
  • Raffaella "Lila" Cerullo Elena's brilliant childhood friend. Newly married to Stefano Carracci.
  • Rino Cerullo Lila's older brother. Co-runs the Cerullo shoe workshop; engaged to Pinuccia Carracci.

Greco

  • Elena Greco Narrator. Lila's lifelong friend; daughter of the porter.
  • Elisa Greco Elena's youngest sibling.
  • Immacolata Greco Elena's mother. Stern housewife with a wandering eye and a pronounced limp.

Carracci

  • Alfonso Carracci Stefano's youngest brother. Elena's deskmate at school; dating Marisa Sarratore.
  • Gennaro ("Rinuccio") Carracci Lila's son. Bears Stefano's surname; nicknamed Rinuccio.
  • Maria Carracci Don Achille's widow; Stefano, Pinuccia and Alfonso's mother.
  • Pinuccia Carracci Stefano's sister. Newly engaged to Lila's brother Rino.
  • Stefano Carracci Lila's husband. Son of the late Don Achille; runs the family salumeria.

Peluso

  • Carmen (Carmela) Peluso Lila's first employee. Pasquale's sister; a fierce, faithful gossip.
  • Pasquale Peluso Bricklayer, communist militant. Carmen's brother; engaged for years to Ada.

Cappuccio

  • Ada Cappuccio Antonio's sister. Becomes Stefano's mistress, then his second household.
  • Antonio Cappuccio Mechanic. Elena's boyfriend; older brother of Ada; intensely jealous of Nino.
  • Melina Cappuccio The mad widow; Donato Sarratore's old lover. Antonio and Ada's mother.

Sarratore

  • Donato Sarratore Railway worker and amateur poet. Nino's father; Melina's old lover; the man on the beach.
  • Lidia Sarratore Donato's long-suffering wife. Marisa and Nino's mother.
  • Marisa Sarratore Nino's younger sister. Dating Alfonso Carracci.
  • Nino Sarratore Brilliant student. The lifelong, mostly secret object of Elena's love.

Scanno

  • Enzo Scanno Fruit-and-vegetable seller turned factory worker. Lila's quiet protector.

Solara

  • Marcello Solara Older Solara brother. Rejected by Lila in book 1; now wearing her shoes.
  • Michele Solara Younger Solara brother. Engaged to Gigliola Spagnuolo.

Spagnuolo

  • Gigliola Spagnuolo The pasticciere's daughter. Michele Solara's increasingly frustrated fiancée.

Airota

  • Adele Airota Pietro's mother. Editor, translator; Elena's literary godmother.
  • Mariarosa Airota Pietro's older sister. Art historian at Milan; Elena's first model of an easy intellectual life.
  • Pietro Airota Classical philologist. Son of Professor Airota; Elena's fiancé.
  • Professor Airota Pietro's father. Professor of Greek literature at Genoa; prominent socialist.

Insegnanti

  • Maestra Oliviero Elena and Lila's primary-school teacher. Elena's first and fiercest patron.
  • Maestro Ferraro Librarian of the rione's lending library. Elena's first reader.
  • Professoressa Galiani Elena's high-school Latin teacher. Communist patrician; lender of newspapers.

Altri personaggi

  • Armando Galiani Professoressa Galiani's son. Medical student. Part of Nino's Ischia circle.
  • Bruno Soccavo Son of the salami magnate. Nino's friend from Ischia.
  • Franco Mari Wealthy Milanese student militant. Elena's first lover at the Normale.
  • Nadia Galiani Professoressa Galiani's daughter. Nino's first official girlfriend.
  • Nella Incardo Maestra Oliviero's cousin in Barano d'Ischia; landlady of the Ischia summers.
