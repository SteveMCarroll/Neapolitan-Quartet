Pinuccia Carracci

Stefano's sister. Newly engaged to Lila's brother Rino.

Family: Carracci (Don Achille's family)

Don Achille and Donna Maria's daughter, sister to Stefano and Alfonso. She works in the family salumeria. She is engaged to Rino Cerullo, Lila's older brother — the cross-marriage that braces the Cerullo and Carracci businesses together.

A few days after Lila returns from her honeymoon, Pinuccia tells Elena that there has been a big lunch at Lila's new apartment to celebrate Pinuccia's engagement to Rino. Elena was not invited. Pinuccia adds, with a sly smirk and a dialect phrase, that Lila si sta imparando — she's learning her place.

This page reflects what's known to a reader who has finished book 2. Earlier knowledge from book 1 is summarized briefly; no events from later books appear here.
