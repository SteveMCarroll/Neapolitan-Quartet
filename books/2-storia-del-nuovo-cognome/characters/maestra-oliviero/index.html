Maestra Oliviero

Elena and Lila's primary-school teacher. Elena's first and fiercest patron.

Family: Insegnanti del rione

The elementary‑school teacher of both Elena and Lila. Maestra Oliviero is the one who, in book 1, first marked them out as exceptional and made sure Elena's parents — much against their own inclination — let her sit for middle school. She bullied, cajoled and at one point physically intimidated Fernando Cerullo into trying the same for Lila; he refused, and Oliviero never quite forgave her.

In book 2 she falls seriously ill and is taken away to Potenza by her sister to convalesce. Elena finally hears the news of her death, in the autumn after her laurea, when a parcel arrives from Potenza: Oliviero's preserved bundle of Elena's primary‑school notebooks — and, tucked among them, an unsuspected reading copy of Lila's child story La fata blu, annotated bellissimo in the margins.

This page reflects what's known to a reader who has finished book 2. Earlier knowledge from book 1 is summarized briefly; no events from later books appear here.
