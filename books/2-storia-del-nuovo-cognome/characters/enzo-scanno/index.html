Enzo Scanno

Fruit-and-vegetable seller turned factory worker. Lila's quiet protector.

Family: Scanno (the fruit and vegetable cart)

Son of the rione's fruit‑and‑vegetable vendor; in book 1 he ran the family cart with a donkey through the rione's mornings. Reliable, laconic, never showy, he has loved Lila since they were children — he was the boy whose stone fight with Lila and Elena ended their infancy and the boy who later, on the way home from the Solaras' car ride, declared his love in passing.

In book 2 he is largely silent for a long stretch, until he begins travelling out to Ischia and then to the Maronti in the worst weeks of Lila's collapse. After Nino flees and Antonio knocks him about, it is Enzo who drives Lila and her mother home from the island. When Lila finally needs to escape her marriage he arrives ten minutes after she phones him; he has rented three rooms at San Giovanni a Teduccio and found a factory job. He sleeps on a brandina, asks nothing of her, and at night studies a self‑taught course on computer programming and Boolean algebra — and gradually pulls her in with him.

This page reflects what's known to a reader who has finished book 2. Earlier knowledge from book 1 is summarized briefly; no events from later books appear here.
