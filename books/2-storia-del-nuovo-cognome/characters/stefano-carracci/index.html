Stefano Carracci

Lila's husband. Son of the late Don Achille; runs the family salumeria.

Family: Carracci (Don Achille's family)

The son of the late Don Achille Carracci, the loan shark and black-marketeer known to the children as "the ogre of fairy tales." Don Achille was murdered when Stefano was still young. Stefano now manages the wealth his father accumulated and is co-owner, with his sister Pinuccia, his brother Alfonso, and his mother Maria, of a profitable salumeria — the deli that anchors the neighbourhood's commerce.

In book 1 Stefano courted Lila quietly and with enormous patience, buying the first pair of shoes she designed and promising to keep them forever as a sign of his love. He invested heavily in the Cerullo family's shoe factory so that the marriage could go forward. At their wedding, scheduled when Lila was sixteen, he seemed the mildest possible match for her — handsome, indulgent, in love.

In book 2's opening pages, that image cracks. At the reception Marcello Solara appears wearing the very pair of prototype shoes Stefano had sworn to safeguard, signalling that Stefano has been trading his fiancée's work to the Solaras to keep the peace. He spends the rest of the day apologising — "She's tired, you have to be patient" — and leaves with Lila for the Amalfi coast as if nothing has happened. When the couple returns, his manner remains affectionate, but Lila now hides parts of her face behind dark glasses and a scarf.

This page reflects what's known to a reader who has finished book 2. Earlier knowledge from book 1 is summarized briefly; no events from later books appear here.
