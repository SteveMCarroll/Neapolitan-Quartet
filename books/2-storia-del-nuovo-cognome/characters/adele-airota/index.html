Adele Airota

Pietro's mother. Editor, translator; Elena's literary godmother.

Family: Airota (the academic family)

Pietro's mother. Magra, non bella ma fine, elegante senza sfoggio. She translates for a Milan publishing house and has done so for years. Elena first meets her on the family's Sunday visit to Pisa near the end of book 2.

It is Adele who quietly rescues Elena's life as a writer. After Pietro brings her Elena's quaderno — the secret little novel — Adele has it typed and passes it to her publisher. The publisher reads it quickly, decides to put it out in the spring, and from then on Adele shepherds Elena: she arranges hotels, sends cordiale phone calls to Naples, schedules a Milan bookshop launch, travels up from Genoa specifically to attend, and parries the hostile audience question about the racy pages with a single facial expression. Elena's gratitude is real, and so is her terror of disappointing her.

This page reflects what's known to a reader who has finished book 2. Earlier knowledge from book 1 is summarized briefly; no events from later books appear here.
