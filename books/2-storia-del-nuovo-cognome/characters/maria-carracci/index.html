Maria Carracci

Don Achille's widow; Stefano, Pinuccia and Alfonso's mother.

Family: Carracci (Don Achille's family)

The widow of Don Achille Carracci, the rione loan shark and black‑ marketeer murdered in his own kitchen when Stefano was still a boy. Mother of Stefano, Pinuccia and Alfonso. She is co‑owner of the salumerie and runs the older shop at the entrance of the rione, while the new one in the rione nuovo belongs to Stefano and Lila.

Maria began book 1 as the disliked widow of the rione's ogre; by book 2 she is something close to a matriarch, dignified, devout, and heavily invested in her son's marriage looking respectable. She is quietly outraged by Lila's bookishness and by her failure to fall pregnant immediately. When the marriage collapses she sides with Stefano and Ada without much hesitation; she is the likely cause of Stefano's later half‑hearted visits to see Rinuccio in San Giovanni.

This page reflects what's known to a reader who has finished book 2. Earlier knowledge from book 1 is summarized briefly; no events from later books appear here.
