Marisa Sarratore

Nino's younger sister. Dating Alfonso Carracci.

Family: Sarratore (the railwayman-poet's family)

Nino's younger sister and a friend of Elena's from childhood — they became close during Elena's summer on Ischia with the Sarratores. Marisa is studying half-heartedly to be a company secretary. She is engaged (in the loose teenage sense) to Alfonso Carracci, and at Lila's wedding the two of them are inseparable at Elena's table.

After the reception ends she and Alfonso slip away early to meet her brother Nino in town.

This page reflects what's known to a reader who has finished book 2. Earlier knowledge from book 1 is summarized briefly; no events from later books appear here.
