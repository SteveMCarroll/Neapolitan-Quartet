Carmen (Carmela) Peluso

Lila's first employee. Pasquale's sister; a fierce, faithful gossip.

Family: Peluso (the murdered carpenter's family)

Pasquale's sister, daughter of the late carpenter Alfredo Peluso. Formally Carmela, but called Carmen by everyone in the rione. In book 2 she is hired by Lila to staff the new salumeria in the rione nuovo — Lila pays her well, gives her shifts she can manage with the family at home, and uses the shop to mock the Carracci/Solara respectability the marriage has saddled her with.

Carmen is loud, devoted, easily wounded, and a fierce gossip. She is the rione's main early conduit of news about Lila and Nino over the Ischia summer, and later the chief sympathetic ear for Ada once Ada becomes Stefano's mistress. By book 2's end she is engaged to a kind forty‑something fuel‑station attendant on the stradone.

This page reflects what's known to a reader who has finished book 2. Earlier knowledge from book 1 is summarized briefly; no events from later books appear here.
