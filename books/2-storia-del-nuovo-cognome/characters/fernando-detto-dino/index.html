Fernando ("Dino") Cerullo

Rino and Pinuccia's first son, called Dino. The object of Lila's reading experiments.

Family: Cerullo (the shoemaker's family)

The first child of Rino Cerullo and Pinuccia Carracci, named Fernando after his grandfather but called Dino in the family. He is born during book 2's middle stretch and lives with his parents in the apartment above the new salumeria.

Lila, struggling with her own son Rinuccio, latches onto Dino for a while as a kind of pedagogical project: she sits with him every day, reading flash‑cards, naming objects, working out word‑games. The obsession scares Rino, who is convinced his sister is somehow draining the boy's life force, and ends in a violent scene in which Stefano flings Rino down the stairs. Dino is fine.

This page reflects what's known to a reader who has finished book 2. Earlier knowledge from book 1 is summarized briefly; no events from later books appear here.
