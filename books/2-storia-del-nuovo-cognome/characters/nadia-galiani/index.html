Nadia Galiani

Professoressa Galiani's daughter. Nino's first official girlfriend.

Family: Galiani (the communist Vomero family)

Daughter of Professoressa Galiani and younger sister of Armando. Slim, pale, very well‑bred, with carefully chosen unisex clothes and the manners of an only‑slightly older sibling than her years. Elena meets her at the Galiani New Year's Eve party in book 1 and recognises her instantly as the girl who can hold Nino's attention.

In book 2 Nadia is Nino's official girlfriend for the long run of the year; she is the one Lila steals him from on Ischia. Lila reads her goodbye letters with cool curiosity, and writes (and never sends) her own apologies. Once Nino disappears from Lila's life, Nadia disappears from book 2's pages too; but the relationship between her family and Elena's fidanzato‑era moves continues to shape Elena's anxieties about prestigio.

This page reflects what's known to a reader who has finished book 2. Earlier knowledge from book 1 is summarized briefly; no events from later books appear here.
