Professor Airota

Pietro's father. Professor of Greek literature at Genoa; prominent socialist.

Family: Airota (the academic family)

Pietro's father. A medium‑height, balding man in a rumpled grey suit, with a tired wide face and large glasses; professor of Greek literature at the University of Genoa and a figura di spicco of the Italian Socialist Party. Elena first meets him on the family's day in Pisa near the end of book 2: she has been afraid of formality and an autista, and finds instead an old Fiat 1100 driven by Mariarosa and parents whose colleagues greet them affectionately on every street corner.

Across one long lunch he spars amicably with his daughter about interclassismo and mediazione, the Patto Atlantico, the Vietnam bombings, Latin America, the campus revolts in America — and praises Elena for her outburst on Hiroshima and Nagasaki. He is the first adult in Elena's life who, in her own phrase, has given his children fairy weapons before the battle.

This page reflects what's known to a reader who has finished book 2. Earlier knowledge from book 1 is summarized briefly; no events from later books appear here.
