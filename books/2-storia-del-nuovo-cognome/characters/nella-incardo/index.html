Nella Incardo

Maestra Oliviero's cousin in Barano d'Ischia; landlady of the Ischia summers.

Family: Incardo (the Barano boarding-house)

Maestra Oliviero's cousin in Barano d'Ischia, who lets out rooms to summer holiday‑makers. In book 1 it was Nella's place that Elena stayed in with the Sarratore family for her first‑ever holiday by the sea — the holiday that ended with Donato Sarratore creeping into her bed.

In book 2 Stefano pays Nella for several weeks each year so that Pinuccia, Lila and Elena can stay there with Nunzia as chaperone. Nella is matter‑of‑fact, generous, and a quiet ally — she folds the girls into the rhythm of her household, feeds them well, and never gossips down to the rione about what she sees. Her bed‑and‑breakfast becomes the background to the long second half of book 2's middle section, including Lila's parallel courtship with Nino on the beach and Elena's second, much more disastrous encounter with Donato.

This page reflects what's known to a reader who has finished book 2. Earlier knowledge from book 1 is summarized briefly; no events from later books appear here.
