Donato Sarratore

Railway worker and amateur poet. Nino's father; Melina's old lover; the man on the beach.

Family: Sarratore (the railwayman-poet's family)

Nino's father. Railway conductor, occasional journalist and amateur poet — author in book 1 of a slim, pretentious volume titled Sussurri e parole ("Whispers and Words"). In the rione he is remembered above all for the affair that broke Melina Cappuccio; after the scandal he moved his family out to a flat near the Mergellina.

In book 1 he sexually harasses the fifteen‑year‑old Elena during her first holiday with the Sarratores on Ischia. In book 2 he reappears, unbearably, on the same Ischia beach when Elena is back there as a near‑adult; on a black night on the Maronti, after she has watched Lila and Nino disappear together, she lets him take her virginity in the dark. It is a private, degrading episode she tells no one about until, much later, she translates it into the central scene of her first novel.

This page reflects what's known to a reader who has finished book 2. Earlier knowledge from book 1 is summarized briefly; no events from later books appear here.
