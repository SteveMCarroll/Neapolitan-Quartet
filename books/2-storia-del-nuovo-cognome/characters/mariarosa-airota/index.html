Mariarosa Airota

Pietro's older sister. Art historian at Milan; Elena's first model of an easy intellectual life.

Family: Airota (the academic family)

Pietro's older sister, twenty‑four when Elena meets her — already a lecturer in Art History at the University of Milan, working on Piero della Francesca, driving the family's old Fiat 1100 around Pisa for the day with her parents and Pietro. She hugs and kisses Elena like an old friend on first meeting, and across a single political lunch puts Elena at ease and changes the social weather of her last Normale year.

In book 2 Mariarosa is Elena's first glimpse of a woman who has inherited the easy, ironic intellectual habits Elena has only laboriously copied — l'addestramento a sentire intimamente mie le questioni del mondo. She also makes time, even as it is inconvenient, to come to Milan and stop by the bookshop on the evening of Elena's first public appearance, before her own commitments take her elsewhere.

This page reflects what's known to a reader who has finished book 2. Earlier knowledge from book 1 is summarized briefly; no events from later books appear here.
