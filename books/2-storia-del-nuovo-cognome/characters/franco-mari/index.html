Franco Mari

Wealthy Milanese student militant. Elena's first lover at the Normale.

Family: Compagni di università

A wealthy, charismatic Milanese student at the Scuola Normale Superiore and a well‑known militant of the student left. He is two or three years ahead of Elena and is, for a long stretch of book 2's final third, her boyfriend, her unofficial finishing school, and her public certification at Pisa.

Franco buys her clothes, takes her to Paris and to Versilia, lends her books, gives her the political slogans she will later embarrass herself with at the Airota family lunch, and gently bullies her into becoming the woman who can take her laurea without flinching. He also pushes her past her own scruples about libertà sessuale and gives her a private rule about mezze vergini that she silently mocks for the rest of the book. He is expelled from the Normale for political activism near the end of her third year — and with that expulsion her unofficial protection at piazza dei Cavalieri lapses.

This page reflects what's known to a reader who has finished book 2. Earlier knowledge from book 1 is summarized briefly; no events from later books appear here.
