Capitolo 45 / 125

Holding hands in Forio

Summary

The husbands arrive a day early, on Saturday evening, on rented Lambrettas. Nunzia has cooked a feast. Rino shoves shoe sketches under Lila's nose; she nods through them. By ten, both men have dragged their wives off to the bedrooms. Elena retreats to her cubicle and, sweating, lies awake to noises she can't shut out — risate e gemiti, doors, faucets, flushes. She thinks about how lentamente Lila si era piegata: no tenderness for Stefano, but no visible resistance either, a quiet, precario equilibrium. The next morning the two couples roar off on the Lambrettas around the island; Elena is left out of every plan.

They come back at three, sunburned and tipsy, fizzy with news. They ran into Nino on the beach at one of the small towns. Rino liked him — si è interessato a come si fanno le scarpe — and discovered something useful: Nino's friend is Soccavo, the heir of a Naples sausage factory. Punta a Soccavo, ti conviene, Stefano teases Elena. Lila says nothing.

Elena puts on her wedding-best dress and walks all the way to Forio. She finds Nino's friend's address and calls up to the window. He comes down strangely cordial. They wander the Sunday market. Conversation about Nadia (tutto bene; ci scriviamo ogni settimana) dries up; Nino pivots to politics — violence, the Cortona peace march, the southern migrant uprising in Turin brutalised by police, li chiamano napoli, li chiamano marocchini. Elena risks her one impressive name-drop: Hai letto Federico Chabod? — having read only a few pages of his book on the idea of nation. For an instant Nino flinches; he doesn't know Chabod. The sense of elettrizzante pienezza is enormous. But he quickly sidesteps to ground he commands — regional autonomy, decentralised planning — and Elena, recognising that his strength is primeggiare and his fragility is quando gli mancano le parole, hides her small advantage. They keep this up for an hour, in their tight shared "vigilant Italian" — ci sentimmo unici. She privately thinks it's nothing like the lightning-storm exchanges with Lila as kids; with Nino she has to be careful what she shows.

Then Nino abruptly says basta, takes her hand, leads her to piazza del Soccorso — ora ti porto a vedere un paesaggio che non dimenticherai più — and the curving blue sea is eclipsed by his grip on her fingers. He keeps holding her hand the entire way back. The Lambrettas of Stefano-Lila and Rino-Pinuccia roar by, slow, U-turn. Rino offers Nino a Lambretta loan to take Elena for a ride; Nino, blushing: non so andare in Lambretta. Stefano laughs: questo è uno che studia, lascia stare. Lila, tight against Stefano, calls back: Mi raccomando, accompagnala a casa. She is playing big sister. Once they roar off, Nino takes Elena's hand again and says: Rino è simpatico, ma perché Lina ha sposato quell'imbecille?
