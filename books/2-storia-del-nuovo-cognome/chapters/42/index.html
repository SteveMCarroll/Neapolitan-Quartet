Capitolo 42 / 125

Visit to Nella; the Sarratores

Summary

Nunzia goes hunting for groceries while the three young women head to the beach at Citara — which they all somehow mishear as "Cetara" and keep calling that all summer. Lila and Pinuccia unzip their cover-ups to reveal new one-piece costumes (Stefano and Rino vetoed the bikinis), prints brilliant on the skin. Elena is still in the faded, baggy old suit she had stitched at Nella's in Barano years ago — and she peels her dress off reluctantly. She walks the shoreline north toward Forio looking for Nino; nothing. She is so sunburned that night she has a fever and is house-bound for several days, mottled with blisters across her shoulders. She cleans, cooks, reads, and pulls every-evening pilgrimages to Forio with Lila and Pinuccia. Qui sì che è bello, Pinuccia complains, da noi è un mortorio. For Elena Forio is the mortorio: Nino never appears.

At the end of the week Elena talks the others into a trip back to Barano and the Maronti — ostensibly to greet Nella, the maestra Oliviero's cousin who had hosted her on Ischia in book 1. Her real purpose: find Marisa, get the Forio address. She is braced for Donato Sarratore but counts on his being at work.

Nella opens the door, takes one look at Elena and bursts into tears. È la contentezza, she says — and adds that la maestra Oliviero, who is now in Potenza with a sister, is not getting better. She fusses over Pinuccia's pregnancy. Elena gives Lila a small tour: here is the corner of the terrace where I sat in the sun; here is where my plate went; here is where I made up my bed each night. For a flash she relives Donato's hand sliding under the sheet onto her in the dark. But she manages to ask, casually, how the Sarratores are. They're at the beach, Nella says. He has "got himself certified sick" and the whole family is there. Marisa is in Naples — she failed four subjects, has to repeat. And then the line that decides Elena's afternoon: Today Nino is there too. When he needs money he turns up for half a day, then goes back to his friend's house in Forio.
