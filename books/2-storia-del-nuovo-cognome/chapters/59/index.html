Capitolo 59 / 125

Behind the bar at Forio

Summary

To match the boys' new schedule the women come down at nine instead of ten — and the boys aren't there. They appear only in the early afternoon, smug and conspiratorial: avevano deciso di anticipare lo studio. Lila's reaction stuns Elena. She passes into un dialetto violento, tells the two they can study whenever they damn well please, di pomeriggio, di sera, di notte, subito, slings on her prendisole, grabs her bag, and walks off toward the road. Nino runs after her and comes back con una faccia da funerale: she would not listen. Elena pretends not to be rattled, swims, eats a panino, leaves. At home she finds Lila sitting on her bed scrivendo on a notebook propped on her knees, one of Elena's books open beside her. Hai avuto una reazione esagerata, Elena tells her. Lila shrugs without looking up and goes on writing all afternoon.

That evening Lila si agghindò come quando doveva arrivare il marito and they go to Forio by motorbuggy. Nunzia, who never takes the sun and is bianchissima, borrows her daughter's rossetto to look less like a corpse. Sat under the bar's awning over a spumone, she launches on the topic of Nino's mother — Lidia, bellissima — and stories from the war years that she insists Nino must ask his mother to confirm. Lila grows restless, announces she has to call Stefano, and disappears into the bar to find the phone booth. Bruno, no longer tongue-tied with Nunzia present, picks up the conversation easily. Then Nino, scusatemi un attimo, also gets up and goes inside.

Lila doesn't come back. Elena goes in to look. Both phone booths empty. A door at the back open to a courtyard. The courtyard empty too, but on the far wall an apertura to a side garden. Un chiarore di notte estiva lambiva le piante. And there, in the dim light, against a wall, are Nino and Lila. Erano stretti l'uno all'altra, si baciavano. Lui le teneva una mano sotto la gonna, lei cercava di allontanargliela, ma intanto seguitava a baciarlo. Elena retreats silently. Mi sentivo come se stessi bruciando, ma le fiamme erano fredde e non provavo dolore. È sposata, è sposata da poco più di un anno. Lila returns to the table alone, impeccabile, but Elena reads the disordine in the dress and the body. They wait; Nino doesn't appear. As the buggy is leaving he runs up: a domani! — cordiale come non mi era mai successo di vederlo. That night Elena lies awake waiting for Lila to come confess what she has done, what she means to do. She never comes. Oggi credo che non lo sapesse nemmeno lei.
