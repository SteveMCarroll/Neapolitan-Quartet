Capitolo 16 / 125

Two husbands

Summary

Elena drags Lila back to the courtyard so she can tell Antonio the "good news." She calls him down; he comes stone-faced and tries not to look at Lila. When Elena enthusiastically announces that Marcello will help him, he goes white and answers in a strangled voice: I never asked you to talk to the Solaras. He thanks them dryly, turns, and walks back inside. Elena, gut-punched, complains all the way home that Antonio is worse than his mother — Lila barely listens.

She is steered upstairs to Lila's flat. Stefano is in pyjamas, the pastries from the bar in front of him. You went to the Solaras? Dressed like that? He bites into a cannoli and tells Elena, with mock affection, to explain to your friend that I work in this rione and she can't keep making me look like shit. He goes to the bathroom. Lila imitates his "shopkeeper-man" voice and they both laugh in the doorway about how exhausting men are. Elena leaves — but before she reaches the bottom of the stairs she hears Stefano roaring obscenities at Lila. Ora aveva una voce d'orco come quella di suo padre: an ogre's voice, his father's voice.
