Capitolo 12 / 125

Military service and a payment to the Solaras

Summary

Antonio is breaking down. He has applied for an exemption from military service as the only male provider for his widowed mother Melina, but the army is taking its time. Enzo Scanno is being called up in the autumn and Antonio is terrified the same notice is coming for him. He hops off work without warning to come find Elena outside school. Find out from Lina, he begs, whether Stefano was excused because he's a widow's son or for some other reason.

A pizza-night with Pasquale, Carmela, Enzo, and Ada turns political. Pasquale, exempted because of a childhood tubercolosi, says the fascists are creeping back to power with the democristiani's help and that "people like us" had better learn to use weapons. Enzo nods. Antonio is mainly preoccupied with whether he'll be drafted. Alone with Elena afterwards he blurts out his real fear: if I leave you'll be with someone else.

Elena asks Lila about Stefano's exemption. Lila finally pulls Pinuccia aside on a Rettifilo errand and Pinuccia tells her, cheerfully: the only sure way out was to pay — and the Solaras arranged it. The Solaras had paid off the same district commission years ago to get themselves marked "insufficient chest capacity"; Stefano simply used their contacts. Lila relays this to Elena not with horror but with something close to triumph: he tricked me from the start. It's proof, she says, that her marriage to Stefano was already an alliance with the Solaras before it was a marriage at all. Elena timidly asks whether the Solaras might do the same favour for Antonio if needed. Lila's face goes mean: Antonio would never go to the Solaras.
