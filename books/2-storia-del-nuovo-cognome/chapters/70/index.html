Storia del nuovo cognome · Giovinezza

Capitolo 70 / 125

L&#39;aquilone, la spiaggia, la notte

Summary

The Sarratores come back from the beach trailing sand, sea-smell, and reproaches that Elena never joined them. Elena lays the table, washes plates, and walks the three children — Pino, Clelia, Ciro — to a canneto to cut reeds for an aquilone. Building the kite absorbs her completely; for most of the afternoon she doesn't think about Nino and Lila. Down at the shore in the late afternoon the whole house turns out to fly it. Elena runs back and forth, can't get it airborne despite Donato shouting instructions from the beach umbrella. Eventually she gives up; Donato comes over, checks the cane frame and the carta velina azzurra, judges the wind, and runs backwards with surprising spring for his soft body. The kite climbs; he hands the string to Ciro, then Clelia, then Pino, then Elena — and stays behind her, breathing on her neck, così, bene, tira un po', lascia.

After dinner the Sarratores walk into town dressed for the evening, sun-burned and vestiti a festa. Elena, pressed to come, stays. She and Nella tidy, make up the kitchen bed, sit on the terrace. The moon is hidden, clouds are gonfio bianco. Nella drifts off mid-chat about the Sarratore children. The whole weight of the day suddenly falls on Elena. She tiptoes out of the house and walks down to the Maronti beach.

Did Michele keep his mouth shut? Is everything filando liscio? Is Nunzia asleep, or trying to calm a son-in-law who arrived on the last vaporetto to find his wife gone? Has Lila phoned home, confirmed Stefano is in the rione nuovo, and is now in bed with Nino senza paure, coppia segreta, coppia intenta a godersi la notte? In the dark Elena reaches a verdict on herself: non ero capace di affidarmi a sentimenti veri. Non sapevo farmi trascinare oltre i limiti. Lila had earned Nino because for her loving him meant provare ad averlo, non sperare che lui la volesse. The moon emerges between clouds with pale edges. The sand is cold; she takes off her shoes. The sea sounds ipnotico. La bellezza delle cose è un trucco, il cielo è il trono della paura. She thinks of Nino and Lila avvinti, of Stefano sleeping alone in the new flat, of the furies storing up violence for tomorrow. She wishes for branchi di cani rabbiosi, vipere, scorpioni, enormi serpenti marini, for assassini che mi strazino il corpo — anything to spare her the days to come and her costituzione inadatta. A hand touches her shoulder with cold fingers; a voice says Lena. She turns — Donato Sarratore, and the breath comes back into her chest come il sorso di una pozione magica.
