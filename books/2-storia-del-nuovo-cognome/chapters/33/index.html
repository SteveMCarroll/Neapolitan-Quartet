Capitolo 33 / 125

The Galiani invitation

Summary

Pinuccia and Rino are married in a rush. Elena barely notices; Galiani has invited her — and her alone from the school — to a party her children are hosting at the family flat on Corso Vittorio Emanuele. Come as you are, alone or with someone, with a boyfriend or without. The point is that you come. For Elena it lands like being summoned to a royal palace. She has no clothes that aren't inadequate (one wedding outfit, too warm; one pair of worn shoes), no manners she trusts, no idea what to say to a roomful of ragazzi whose families have been studying for generations.

She thinks of asking Alfonso along, then realises he wasn't invited. After days of panic she goes to Lila for counsel. Lila has a yellow bruise under her cheekbone today. Why are you going? Where does this professoressa live? Can you see the sea from her place? What does her husband do? Are her kids still studying? Each answer deepens the gap. Lila offers a dress: Elena reminds her non mi vanno, ho il petto più grosso, ho tutto più grosso. Lila advises: don't go. Elena, relieved, agrees, and walks away through the oleanders. Then Lila calls her back.

Ti accompagno io. Lila will go with her. Stefano won't allow it? Quello poi si vede. Elena, terrified again — what will she wear? what if she speaks dialect? what if Galiani is so dazzled by Lila she forgets me? — tries to evaporate the plan by silence. But Lila comes hunting her. She has not only got Stefano's permission; he has agreed to drop them off and pick them up. How did you manage him? Lila smiles a strange smile and tells Elena, in dialect: If I want a thing, I just have to act a little like a slut. Then the heavier line — Là, per favore, non parlare così. And Lila, perplexed: Ti vergogni di me?
