Capitolo 20 / 125

The Oliviero collapses; Melina in the ponds

Summary

To placate her mother, Elena takes a summer job back at the cartoleria: she'll take the stationer's three daughters to the Sea Garden every day. Energised, she goes to see la maestra Oliviero to tell her the news and to drop a teaser about Lila maybe going back to school. Oliviero doesn't answer the door. Eventually a neighbour's son climbs in through the balcony and finds her unconscious on the kitchen floor. They carry her down in her nightgown, swollen-faced, eyes terrified. The car drives off with its horn blasting.

That same afternoon, Melina's children are heard calling her in the courtyard with rising panic. Melina has disappeared. Half the rione runs to look, including Stefano with Ada in the convertible. Elena follows Antonio without a word, all the way out to the stagni. She tries to take his hand for comfort. He pushes her off and snarls: Lasciami stare, che non sei una femmina — let me be, you're not even a woman to me. The pain of it lands hard, but in that same moment they spot Melina: sitting in the green pond water up to her neck, cooling off, hair soaked, lips smeared with leaves and muck, completely silent for the first time in ten years of public madness.

They bring her home between them. The rione exhales. Lila is at the edge of the crowd, having not joined the search — news from the new rione apartment travels slowly. She doesn't return Elena's wave. Her face is hard to read: moved, hurt, almost frightened, as if she felt the same scattering inside herself.
