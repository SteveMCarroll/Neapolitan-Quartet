Capitolo 35 / 125

Elena finds her voice

Summary

The discovery that Nino is dating Galiani's daughter does not, to Elena's surprise, ruin the night. She loves him and knows she will never have him; that he has chosen a girl in every way superior to her, raised in this very flat among these books, has the strange effect of calming her — un esempio così perfetto di simmetria che bisognava goderne senza fiatare. And then Nadia, blushing, runs to embrace her and praises Elena's school essays without a trace of her brother's irony. Elena, suddenly amplified in the eyes of the two people she most cares about (Nino and Lila), takes off.

She chats, dances rock 'n' roll (her glasses fly off her nose mid- spin without breaking), shifts into an unfamiliar fluent Italian che non sentii artificiale come quello che usavo a scuola. On the terrace Galiani holds a small circle with Armando, Nino, and a boy called Carlo. The older boys spar with each other through her; Galiani plays referee. Armando is the firebrand: non è con la pianificazione che il mondo cambia, il sangue è necessario, è necessaria la violenza. Nino answers him calmly: planning is indispensable. The conversation ranges over the nuclear arsenal, peace, colonialism, the OAS and the FLN in Algeria, French Gaullism, Saragat, Nenni, Fanfani, Moro, the Christian-Democrat left, Italian "apertura a sinistra," Beirut, Danilo Dolci, Martin Luther King, Bertrand Russell. Elena understands maybe half — and yet, when Nino speaks against the bomb, something in her un'altra persona più sicura speaks for her: non dobbiamo ripetere gli errori delle generazioni che ci hanno preceduti. Se permettiamo l'uso di quelle armi, diventeremo tutti molto più colpevoli dei nazisti. She uses the high Italian she learned doing Greek and Latin versions. Galiani, taking the floor next, quotes her twice by name: "Come ha detto giustamente Elena." Nadia whispers quanto sei brava, quanto sei coraggiosa in her ear.

Lila stays at the edge of the bookshelves through it all. Earlier, Nino tried to invite her to dance. She refused, slipped off, never came back to the dance room. While Galiani is still speaking Lila tugs Elena's sleeve and hisses in dialect: muoio di sonno, ti fai dire dov'è il telefono e chiami Stefano?
