Capitolo 38 / 125

Rino&#39;s wedding; the wine in the face

Summary

Elena attends Pinuccia and Rino's wedding with Alfonso and Marisa. Stefano has cut the guest list ruthlessly — refused to chip in for a new flat for his sister, paid only for the wedding itself, and excised half the rione. Pinuccia and Rino, even though Rino now owns a shoe factory, will move into Maria's bedroom in Don Achille's old apartment. Mio fratello è uno stronzo, Pinuccia tells Elena. Spende e spande for his wife but not for his sister.

Two incidents puncture the day. (1) Gino — Elena's perfidious ex from the elementary days, now engaged to a Carracci cousin — sidles up to the children's table where Alfonso has been kind to a shy thirteen-year-old German cousin of Nunzia's. Gino whispers to the boy that Alfonso is a ricchione (a queer): be careful, he took you to the terrace, next time he'll take you to the toilet. Alfonso flushes and freezes. Marisa snaps back, extracts the smear, then publicly kisses Alfonso on the mouth — a kiss so long and unembarrassed that Elena, watching, suddenly sees Marisa for the first time: brave, capable of stubborn love. Lila, from the head table, applauds — but the applause merges disgustingly with Michele's, Stefano's, and the metal-merchant's chortling. Marisa hisses to Gino: get out of here or I'll slap you. He goes.

(2) Near the end of the night the metal merchant's wife — the Florentine relative they've imported as a partner — grabs the hem of Pinuccia's wedding dress and yanks it up, baring her thick legs, and bellows at Stefano: guarda che cosce e che culo tiene tua sorella; oggigiorno vi piacciono le femmine che paiono scopettini per il cesso, ma è la mia Pinuccia che Dio ha fatto per darvi i figli. Lila, without a beat, throws her wine in the woman's face — onto her shantung dress. Elena flees to the bathroom and locks herself in. When she comes out, the scene has miraculously calmed: Stefano is chatting with the merchant, his wife sits stiffly in her stained dress, the band plays. Lila is alone on the terrace, looking at the sea.
