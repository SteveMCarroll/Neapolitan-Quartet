Capitolo 17 / 125

Antonio breaks it off

Summary

Walking home, Elena is suddenly afraid — of Stefano killing Lila, of Antonio killing her. The dialect register of male rules feels impossible to navigate. Lila, by going to flirt under everyone's eyes with her ex-suitor Marcello as signora Carracci, has humiliated Stefano. Elena, thinking she was helping, has gone to beg a favour for Antonio from the very people who beat his sister Ada's father senseless. Antonio is at his window waiting for her in the courtyard.

He comes down with his hands in his pockets, holding them there as if to keep them under control. His voice is calm. You've humiliated me with the people I despise most. You've made me look like a man who sends his woman to ask for favours. He'd rather do military service a hundred times — die under arms — than kiss Marcello Solara's hand. If Pasquale and Enzo heard, they'd spit in his face. He is leaving her, because she has finally proved she doesn't care about him or his feelings. He tells her she can do what she likes with Sarratore's son.

Then, with no warning, he pulls his hands out of his pockets, drags her into the gateway, and kisses her — pressing too hard, his tongue desperate. Then he turns and walks away. Climbing the stairs Elena thinks dazedly that she is luckier than Lila: Antonio would never hurt her — he can only hurt himself.
