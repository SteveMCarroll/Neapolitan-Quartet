Capitolo 18 / 125

Stefano confides in the car

Summary

Elena drags herself through a disastrous morning at school: a botched chemistry oral, Galiani gently asking what's happened to her. At home, to her surprise, her mother passes on a polite request — Stefano wants to know if you can go with him to the Rettifilo dressmaker. Her father, who's home pretending illness, makes lewd jokes about why Stefano keeps needing accompaniment and why Lila isn't pregnant yet (Carracci che fa, gli piace solo la televisione?).

The drive turns into something Elena has never heard from a man: a long, sustained confession in soft dialect. Stefano admits the first wedding night was a beating. He admits the new beating after the Solara bar. La sera del bar Solara sono stato costretto. He calls Lila a forza cattiva — a bad force — that ruins everything gentle in him. The thing that wounds him most: Lo vedi che non rimane incinta? He's convinced she is killing his children inside her on purpose, to make him look like a man who can't father a child. What he wants from Elena: be his ally, talk Lila into helping with the new salumeria, get her to behave like a wife.

At the dressmaker's, his manner snaps back to hard. He demands the wedding photo down from the window — I am her husband. The dressmaker complies but, to soften the blow, claims that during the photo's run on the Rettifilo three people came in asking after the bride: Renato Carosone, an Egyptian prince, and Vittorio De Sica (plus a Roma journalist who wanted a swimsuit shoot). Stefano, flattered, softens. By the drive back Lila has become, in his speech, una sorta di fluido prezioso in a vessel that belongs to him. Lila's mother Nunzia would tell that story for the rest of her life.
