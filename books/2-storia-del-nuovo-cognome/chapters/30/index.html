Capitolo 30 / 125

The panel catches fire

Summary

The shoes do well — too well. By spring, knockoffs appear in shop windows around the city, identical to the Cerullo designs with a tassel or buckle changed; Michele leans on the imitators and shuts them down. He decides what they need is new models, and convenes a meeting at Piazza dei Martiri: both Solaras, the Carraccis, Rino, Gigliola, Pinuccia. Stefano shows up without Lila. She's tired, he says — a flat refusal.

Michele is furious. If Lila isn't here what the fuck are we talking about? Rino tries to cover by lying — he and Fernando are already working on new models for an Arezzo trade show in September. Michele doesn't believe him. He pushes Stefano: your wife is necessary, you should have forced her to come. Stefano, unexpectedly sharp, snaps that his wife works all day in the salumeria and her evening is his. Vedi se riesce a pensare un poco pure a noi, Michele says — with a grimace that briefly ruins his pretty-boy face.

Pinuccia and Gigliola, each for her own reasons, can't stand how much Michele has just exalted Lila. Their daily friction escalates. One afternoon Gigliola slaps Pinuccia. Pinuccia weeps to Rino. Rino storms over to the shop and announces he can fire Gigliola — from tomorrow you're back to stuffing ricotta into cannoli. Michele appears, walks Rino out under the SOLARA sign, and points out calmly that the shop is Solara, not Carracci. The two girls inside start shrieking at each other again. Michele loses his temper, says he is firing both of them, and lets slip: he'll put Lila in charge of the shop.

The three — Gigliola, Pinuccia, Rino — turn on him in unison. And in the middle of the row the panel itself emits "a hoarse sound, a kind of sick breath" and bursts into flame. The fire leaps up behind Pinuccia and catches her hair. Rino smothers it with his bare hands before her head can burn.
