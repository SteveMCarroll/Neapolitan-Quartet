Capitolo 40 / 125

*Devo fare i bagni a mare*

Summary

Lila agrees to see the doctor if Elena swears she's not angry with her. Elena refuses to swear. Lila finally murmurs va bene, ho sbagliato. She goes — escorted by Nunzia, Maria, and Pinuccia, all three demanding to be present at the examination. The doctor, an elderly man chosen by the rione midwife, pronounces her healthy. La signora è molto giovane, he says. Si deve rinforzare un po'. Rinforzare — to be made stronger. The word slices through Elena: it means Lila is not, after all, a witch annihilating embryos but una femmina insufficiente, an insufficient female. Her grudge softens. Lila tells the story afterwards in raunchy dialect: Mi ha stracciata con una cosa di ferro, gli ho dato un sacco di soldi, e per arrivare a quale conclusione? Che ho bisogno di un rinforzo. The prescription: sea bathing. Pare che se una va a mare si rinforza e i figli vengono.

Stefano organises everything. Lila will go to Torre Annunziata for July and August with Nunzia and Pinuccia (who also wants to rinforzarsi though she's already pregnant). Alfonso will run Piazza dei Martiri with Gigliola; Maria will run the new salumeria. Se sto due mesi con mia madre e Pinuccia, mi ammazzo, Lila tells Elena. She begs Elena to come along — I'll pay you what the bookseller pays you. Quit, come with me. Elena, picturing two months of screaming with Pinuccia under one roof, declines.

The next morning Nino materialises at the bookshop, pale and thinner, having burned through four exams. He praises her summary of his article — neither Galiani nor Armando nor Nadia read it as carefully as you did — and announces he's leaving for Ischia tomorrow. He extends his hand, she takes it; he pulls her gently forward and brushes her lips with his, a half-second kiss, then walks off down the Rettifilo con la sua andatura da condottiero svagato. That night Elena does not sleep. At dawn she runs to the salumeria as Lila is pulling up the shutter and says, in the tone of someone asking the impossible: if you go to Ischia for the bagni instead of Torre Annunziata, I'll quit and come with you.
